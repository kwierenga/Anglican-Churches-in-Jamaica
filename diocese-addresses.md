Notes:
- The diocese organises churches by "cure" (parish church) with several mission churches under each. The diocese typically lists ONE postal/rectory address and ONE phone number for the whole cure. Mission churches are physically in their own town/district, but the listed mailing address belongs to the cure rectory. Where a mission has a placeholder coord, the rectory address only helps if the mission shares the rectory P.O.; otherwise treat the cure address as a contact only and geocode the mission town separately.
- Cure (parent) churches are marked **CURE**; their listed address is the rectory address shown on the diocese page.
- Mission churches list the cure they belong to and the cure's contact address.
