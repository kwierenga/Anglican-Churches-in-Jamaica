## Manual-browse leads

- **JN Foundation parish history PDFs** (parishhistoriesofjamaica.org) — contain archival parish photos; page through St. Mary, St. Thomas, and other parish PDFs for Epiphany Richmond, All Saints' The Abbey, etc.
- **Flickr "Jamaican Churches" pool** (flickr.com/groups/jamaicanchurches/) — not keyword-searchable; browse and check per-photo licenses.
- **NLJ Digital Collection** general browse (nljdigital.nlj.gov.jm) — most productive source class found.
