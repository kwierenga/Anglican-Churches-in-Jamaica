# Explore — Jamaican Anglican Church Research Library

A local, offline research library of primary and secondary sources on the history of the Anglican Church in Jamaica. It exists to support the narrative work in [content/churches/](../content/churches/) with verifiable sources rather than web recall.

## Structure

- [primary-sources/](primary-sources/) — nineteenth- and early-twentieth-century published histories and Project Canterbury chapters
- [diocesan-records/](diocesan-records/) — Jamaica Almanacs and Handbooks (clergy lists, membership statistics), plus the Church of England clergy index
- [secondary-sources/](secondary-sources/) — modern parish histories (JN Foundation project, lead researcher Dr Jenny Jemmott; St Mary by Prof. Veront Satchell) and the National Library of Jamaica's "history notes" series
- [newspaper-articles/](newspaper-articles/) — (placeholder) selected Gleaner, Observer, and international coverage

## Notes on retrieval

Several sources (notably `parishhistoriesofjamaica.org`) block direct automated PDF download. Those files were retrieved via the Wayback Machine (`web.archive.org/web/2024/…`). Links above point to the live source wherever the live source allows direct download.
