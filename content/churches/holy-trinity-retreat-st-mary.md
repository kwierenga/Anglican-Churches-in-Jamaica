# Holy Trinity, Retreat
**St. Mary** · Church · active

Holy Trinity Anglican Church in Retreat, St. Mary overlooks the Rio Nuevo valley and stands as the parish's first Anglican church. Built around 1835 on former sugar estate land donated by planter Henry Rigg, it served as mother church to other Anglican congregations. During Hurricane Melissa (2025), the Rio Nuevo area suffered severe damage including blocked bridges and damaged homes, exposing the church's coastal-hill location to intense weather despite no specific structural reports.

## History
Holy Trinity at Retreat is described by its congregation and by local sources as the oldest-standing Anglican church in the parish of St. Mary. The land on which it stands was donated in 1828 by Henry Rigg (1791–1869), a wealthy English landholder born at Bowness, Westmoreland, who with his wife Ann Martin Rigg (née Willis) owned the Tweedside and Gibraltar properties in St. Mary and resided at Retreat for thirty-five years until his death in 1869. The imposing Georgian church was erected and completed around 1835 — in the immediate aftermath of Emancipation in 1834 — and the *Daily Gleaner* recorded Holy Trinity's celebration of its 180th anniversary in November 2015. The surrounding Rio Nuevo valley carries a deep historical layering, including the Taíno settlement of Rio Nuevo and the decisive 1658 battle at which the Spanish finally lost Jamaica to the English.

## Architecture
Holy Trinity is a striking example of Georgian Jamaican ecclesiastical architecture, its exterior constructed of brick, cut stone, concrete and wood and its form largely unchanged since the 1830s. A fire in the early 2000s required interior remodelling, but the external fabric was preserved. The church stands on the site of what local tradition records as Rigg's former sugar estate at Retreat.

## Clergy
Holy Trinity is the mother church of four other Anglican chapels in St. Mary: St. Matthew's Boscobel, St. John's Gayle, St. Andrew's Labyrinth, and St. Margaret's Mission at Clifton Lodge — a circuit relationship confirmed by the church's treasurer Tamiel Scott in a 2015 *Daily Gleaner* interview. The congregation, intergenerational and approximately eighty strong, sustains Sunday worship, Sunday School, and women's and youth groups.

## Notable Facts
Holy Trinity is celebrated as "St. Mary's first Anglican Church" and as mother church to four daughter chapels in the parish. The land donor, Henry Rigg, is documented in the UCL *Legacies of British Slavery* database as a St. Mary plantation owner who resided in the parish until his death in 1869. The church survived a fire in the early 2000s with its Georgian exterior intact. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought severe damage to the surrounding Rio Nuevo area, including blocked bridges and destroyed homes.
