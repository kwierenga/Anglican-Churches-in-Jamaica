# St. Michael's, Arthur's Seat
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. Michael's Anglican Church serves **Arthur's Seat**, a community in the upland farming country of northern Clarendon. The church is administered as a mission of the **All Saints' Crofts Hill** cure, alongside St. Bartholomew's Good Hope and St. Peter's Rhoden Hall. The cure rectory is at Croft's Hill P.O., Clarendon (telephone 966-7609).

## History
**Founding of the parish (1664).** Clarendon parish was created in **1664**, nine years after the English seizure of Jamaica from Spain (**1655**), and named for **Sir Edward Hyde, 1st Earl of Clarendon** (1609–1674), Lord High Chancellor of England under Charles II. The original parish church was the **Church of the White Cross** at "The Cross" — Jamaica's third Anglican church. **St. Paul's Chapelton** was built as a chapel of ease in **1666** and became the parish church when the Cross Church was destroyed in **1815**. **Vere** was carved out as a separate sugar-plain parish in **1673** and was reabsorbed into Clarendon in **1867**; **Law 20 of 1867** designated **May Pen** as chief town.

**Slavery, Sam Sharpe, and Emancipation.** Clarendon was one of Jamaica's great sugar parishes — the fertile Rio Minho plain held the great estates of the Suttons, Pennants, Ballards, and Dawkins, and the wealth of the parish was built on the labour of enslaved Africans. One of Jamaica's earliest slave rebellions erupted at **Suttons Plantation in 1690**: some 500 enslaved people rose in revolt, and many of those who escaped joined the Maroon communities eventually led by **Cudjoe** into the Cockpit Country. During the **1831 Sam Sharpe (Christmas) Rebellion**, planters and Anglican clergy formed the **Colonial Church Union**, a vigilante alliance that sought to "wipe the non-conformist preachers off the face of the earth"; Sharpe was hanged in Montego Bay on **23 May 1832** and is today a National Hero of Jamaica. Emancipation came in 1834, with full freedom on **1 August 1838**. After the Morant Bay Rebellion of 1865 and Crown Colony rule (1866), the Anglican Church was disestablished in **1870** and reconstituted as the self-supporting Diocese of Jamaica.

**Foundation of St. Michael's Arthur's Seat.** Arthur's Seat is one of the upland farming districts of northern Clarendon, in the broken hill country between the Cockpit Country to the north-west and the parish capital at May Pen to the south. Like the other communities of the Crofts Hill cure, Arthur's Seat developed in the post-Emancipation period as freed people settled the parish's interior in search of provision land. The Diocese of Jamaica extended its mission network into communities like Arthur's Seat during Bishop **Enos Nuttall's** late-nineteenth-century rural church-planting expansion (1880–1916), which extended the rural mission network from 26 stations in 1880 to **122 by 1911** — founding a small chapel under the cure of the larger All Saints' Crofts Hill. The 1900 Handbook of Jamaica records **A. Brown** as serving Arthur's Seat.

**Twentieth century: bauxite, May Pen, Independence.** Chapelton lost its status as parish capital in **1938**, with administrative functions transferring to **May Pen**. **Alcoa** began bauxite operations in Clarendon in **1959**; the **Jamalco** alumina refinery at Halse Hall opened in **1972**. Jamaica's independence on **6 August 1962** found St. Michael's a settled mission of the Crofts Hill cure.

**Hurricane Melissa (October 2025).** Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on **28 October 2025** — the strongest hurricane to strike Jamaica in recorded meteorological history. The specific status of St. Michael's Arthur's Seat after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.

## Architecture
St. Michael's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the Clarendon interior in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. The hill-country position channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
- **1900** — **A. Brown** recorded as serving Arthur's Seat (1900 Handbook of Jamaica).
- Today — St. Michael's is served as a mission of the All Saints' Crofts Hill cure within the Mandeville Region's Clarendon Deanery. Pastoral oversight has historically rotated among neighbouring Clarendon clergy when the Crofts Hill cure has been vacant.

## Notable Facts
The dedication to **St. Michael the Archangel** — leader of the heavenly host, commemorated at **Michaelmas on 29 September** — is a common Anglican choice for hilltop or elevated sites, where Michael's traditional association with high places gave such dedications a particular resonance.
