# St. Stephen's, Cornwall Mountain
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Stephen's Anglican Church serves the community of Cornwall Mountain in the hills of northern Westmoreland. The placename carries a double colonial echo: Cornwall was the historic county of western Jamaica (alongside Middlesex and Surrey), and Cornwall Mountain reflects that administrative geography imposed on the interior landscape. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory under the Westmoreland Deanery; no substantive foundational or architectural history has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed, and the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica — indicating a post-1891 foundation.

The dedication is to St. Stephen, the first Christian martyr, whose feast day is 26 December. Stoning is the traditional iconographic mark of the martyrdom recounted in Acts 7.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Interior Westmoreland communities experienced destructive winds and heavy rain. The specific status of St. Stephen's Cornwall Mountain after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
