# St. Mark's, Hopewell
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Mark's Anglican Church serves Hopewell, a small community in the Sheffield District of interior Westmoreland, not to be confused with the more familiar coastal Hopewell in Hanover on the Montego Bay–Lucea corridor. It is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory under the Westmoreland Deanery. No substantive architectural or foundational history has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed.

## History
Westmoreland is one of Jamaica's largest sugar parishes, its economy built on the labour of enslaved Africans until Emancipation in 1834 and full freedom on 1 August 1838. Until 1870 the Church of England in Jamaica was the established state church. The Hopewell in Westmoreland's Sheffield District is an interior community whose Anglican ministry was most likely established during the post-disestablishment rural expansion of the Diocese, though no specific founding date has been verified. A "Hopewell" mission station is recorded in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica under the Hanover cure of Rev. J. Ord at St. Saviour's — but that entry refers to the Hanover/coastal Hopewell, not the Westmoreland one. The Westmoreland Hopewell does not appear in the 1891 list, indicating a later foundation.

## Architecture
St. Mark's is a small masonry rural chapel. No detailed architectural description has been identified in the public sources reviewed.

## Clergy
St. Mark's is served within the Westmoreland Deanery under shared clergy arrangements, with lay leaders and wardens sustaining regular worship.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Interior communities across Westmoreland experienced destructive winds. The specific status of St. Mark's Hopewell after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.

## Notable Facts
There are three separate Hopewells in western Jamaica with Anglican associations — in Hanover (on the north coast near Round Hill), in St. James (central parish), and in Westmoreland (Sheffield District). This entry is the Westmoreland one; the CSV coordinates (18.25, -78.29) place it in interior Westmoreland near Darliston. Earlier draft text for this page described the coastal Hanover Hopewell; that description has been corrected here.
