# St. Faith's, Bodles / Old Harbour area
**St. Catherine** · Church · unknown

St. Faith's Anglican Church in the Bodles/Old Harbour area of St. Catherine provides a second St. Faith's entry in the parish, alongside St. Faith's at Faith's community. This entry is located in the south-western part of St. Catherine near Old Harbour, in the flat coastal plain area, distinct from the interior hill community of Faith's. Bodles is associated with the Bodles Research Station, a significant agricultural research institution in Jamaica.

## History
The Bodles/Old Harbour area of southern St. Catherine is in the flat coastal plain and agricultural zone between Old Harbour and the parish boundary with Clarendon. Bodles Research Station is a government agricultural research facility in this area that has been important to Jamaican crop science. The Anglican church here serves the farming and residential community of the south-western coastal plain.

## Architecture
St. Faith's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Catherine. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the coastal landscape of St. Catherine. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
The Bodles/Old Harbour area church has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements, likely connected to the Old Harbour circuit.

## Notable Facts
Bodles Research Station is one of Jamaica's principal agricultural research institutions, conducting trials on crops including sugarcane, vegetables, and tropical fruits. The presence of an Anglican church near this research facility reflects the Diocese's coverage of the south coast agricultural communities of St. Catherine.
