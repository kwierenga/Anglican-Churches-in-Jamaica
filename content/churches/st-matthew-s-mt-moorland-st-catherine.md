# St. Matthew's, Mt. Moorland
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Mount Moorland, a community in the interior of St. Catherine parish in the hill country. Mount Moorland is a small farming district, and the church has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The dedication to St. Matthew the Evangelist and tax collector is found in several Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Mount Moorland sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country north of Spanish Town. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew's to serve this community. The church has served the Mount Moorland farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Mount Moorland has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Mount Moorland's name combines the highland character of its position with a landscape description — a 'moorland' on a Jamaican hill is evocative rather than geographically precise, since Jamaica has no true moorland in the British sense. Such transplanted English landscape names are common across Jamaica's interior communities.
