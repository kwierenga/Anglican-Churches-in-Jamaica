# St. Barnabas', John's Hall
**Portland** · Mission · unknown

St. Barnabas' Anglican Church serves John's Hall, a community in the western interior of Portland parish near the Buff Bay area. John's Hall is a small farming community in the hill country of western Portland, and St. Barnabas' has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The dedication to St. Barnabas, the 'son of encouragement' celebrated for his generosity and support of early Christian mission, is shared with several other Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
John's Hall sits in the interior hills of western Portland, part of the agricultural landscape that surrounds Buff Bay and its hinterland. The Anglican Diocese established St. Barnabas' as a mission chapel to serve this community. Note that a separately named John's Hall appears to exist in western Portland associated with this entry, distinct from the John's Hall in St. Mary.

## Architecture
St. Barnabas' is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Portland. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of Portland. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
John's Hall has been served within the western Portland deanery circuit, likely linked to the Buff Bay cluster of churches.

## Notable Facts
St. Barnabas is commemorated on 11 June in the Anglican calendar, and churches with this dedication across Jamaica reflect the widespread veneration of the early Christian missionary who travelled with Paul and supported the first Gentile church at Antioch. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought serious rain and wind to western Portland; the John's Hall community was in the affected zone.
