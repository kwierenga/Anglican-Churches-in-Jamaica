# St. Mary's Church (Old), Rio Nuevo
**St. Mary** · Church · ruin

St. Mary’s Anglican Church at Rio Nuevo occupies a site of deep historical significance on the north coast of St. Mary, near the location of the Battle of Rio Nuevo in 1658, the last major engagement of the Spanish-British conflict over Jamaica. Rio Nuevo is a small coastal community east of Ocho Rios, on the boundary between St. Mary and St. Ann, and the church here sits within a landscape that saw some of the earliest European military history on the island. The ‘Old’ designation in the entry reflects the church’s historical character.

## History
The Rio Nuevo area was the site of the Battle of Rio Nuevo in June 1658, when British forces under Sir Edward D’Oyley decisively defeated a Spanish expedition attempting to recapture Jamaica, effectively ending Spanish resistance to British rule on the island. The Anglican church established at Rio Nuevo came later, as part of the Diocese’s mission network along the north coast. The historical significance of the Rio Nuevo battlefield has given the site an enduring presence in Jamaican historical memory, and the church occupies a position in a landscape layered with 17th-century colonial history.

## Architecture
The ‘Old’ designation suggests this is a historic or possibly ruinous building, rather than a fully active modern church. The coastal position on the St. Mary/St. Ann border exposes the site to north-coast weather systems. Rio Nuevo’s small harbour provided some historical shelter for vessels, but the exposed coastline remains vulnerable to storm surge.

## Clergy
Rio Nuevo has been served within the coastal St. Mary circuit of the Diocese. Its historical character suggests it may function more as a heritage site than an active weekly congregation, though formal services may still be held on significant occasions.

## Notable Facts
The Battle of Rio Nuevo (1658) is one of the decisive moments in Jamaican colonial history. The site is marked by a monument, and the battlefield is recorded in heritage documentation. The presence of an Anglican church at this location adds an ecclesiastical layer to a site already significant in military and colonial history. Hurricane activity along the north coast has periodically threatened coastal heritage sites in this area. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along the north coast and the Rio Nuevo area was in the storm’s path.
