# St. Nathaniel's, Swallowfield
**St. Andrew** · Mission · unknown

St. Nathaniel's Anglican Church serves Swallowfield, a community in southern St. Andrew in the residential zone between the urban centre and the southern coastal area. The dedication to St. Nathaniel — identified in Christian tradition with Bartholomew the Apostle — is very unusual in Jamaica, making this church one of the most distinctively named in the Anglican network. Swallowfield is a neighbourhood in the southern metropolitan zone of St. Andrew.

## History
Swallowfield sits in southern St. Andrew, in the urban residential landscape near the Kingston/St. Andrew boundary. The Anglican Diocese established St. Nathaniel's to serve this community. The unusual dedication to Nathaniel — who in the Gospel of John is told 'here is an Israelite in whom there is no deceit' — may reflect a specific founding tradition or clerical preference.

## Architecture
St. Nathaniel's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Andrew. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Andrew. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Swallowfield has been served within the southern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
The dedication to Nathaniel is exceptionally rare among Anglican churches worldwide, making St. Nathaniel's Swallowfield one of the most distinctively named churches in Jamaica's Anglican network. Nathaniel/Bartholomew is typically commemorated alongside Philip on 1 May in some Anglican calendars, and on 24 August in others.
