# St. Paul's Church (Clarksonville), Clarksonville
**St. Mary** · Church · ruin

St. Paul’s Anglican Church serves Clarksonville in St. Mary parish, a small interior community whose name reflects the colonial estate-naming conventions of the plantation era. The church has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations, functioning as a small mission chapel within the St. Mary deanery network. Clarksonville sits in the hilly interior of St. Mary, in the agricultural landscape between the coastal towns and the higher ridge country.

## History
Clarksonville developed as a small settlement in the interior of St. Mary, its name preserving the memory of a colonial proprietor in the post-Emancipation landscape of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul’s as part of its mission outreach across the interior communities of St. Mary, serving the freed families and later generations of small farmers who settled in the area. The church’s history mirrors the broader transformation of St. Mary’s interior from plantation agriculture to independent smallholder farming.

## Architecture
St. Paul’s is a small masonry chapel characteristic of Anglican mission buildings in the St. Mary interior. The building is set in the hilly terrain of the Clarksonville district, in a landscape subject to the heavy rainfall that characterises interior St. Mary throughout the year and especially during hurricane season.

## Clergy
Clarksonville has been served within the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements with other interior congregations. Lay leaders sustain regular worship between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
Clarksonville is one of the many small interior communities of St. Mary whose history and name are rooted in the plantation era. The area’s inland position means storm surge is not a primary risk, but the heavy orographic rainfall of interior St. Mary creates significant landslide and road-closure risk during hurricane passages. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought serious rain impacts to interior St. Mary; the Clarksonville community was in the affected zone.
