# St. Mary's, Rose Hill
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Mary's Anglican Church serves Rose Hill, a community in the rural interior of Portland parish. The chapel is one of a network of small mission stations the Diocese of Jamaica established across the Portland interior in the late nineteenth century to serve the parish's small-farming and banana-economy population.

## History
Portland was created as a parish in 1723 under the Duke of Portland (then Governor of Jamaica) and named in his honour. The parish's economy was transformed in 1871 when the American Captain Lorenzo Dow Baker exported the first commercial shipment of Jamaican bananas from Port Antonio to Boston, beginning a banana boom that defined Portland's late-nineteenth- and early-twentieth-century economy. The dispersed banana workforce settled across the parish's hill country, and the Diocese of Jamaica responded by extending its mission network into the interior — chapels at Comfort Castle, Sherwood Forest, Birnamwood, Rose Hill, Maidstone, and other small districts each grew up to serve the surrounding communities.

The dedication to St. Mary the Virgin — mother of Jesus, commemorated on multiple feast days in the Anglican calendar including the Annunciation (25 March) and the Assumption / "Falling Asleep" (15 August) — is a common Anglican dedication. (St. Mary the Virgin gives her name to Jamaica's neighbouring St. Mary parish.) No substantive foundational date or architectural history for St. Mary's Rose Hill has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed.

## Architecture
St. Mary's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across rural Portland. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge, but the heavy rainfall typical of Portland's interior makes drainage and roof maintenance significant ongoing concerns.

## Clergy
St. Mary's Rose Hill is served within the Eastern Jamaica Region's Portland Deanery, grouped with the cure churches and missions of the Portland Anglican network.
