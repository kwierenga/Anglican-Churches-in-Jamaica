## History
Pimento Walk sits in the interior of St. Elizabeth, in the agricultural landscape of the parish. The name 'pimento walk' describes a plantation's pimento cultivation area — in Jamaican agricultural terminology, a 'walk' is a dedicated cultivation ground, particularly for tree crops. Allspice/pimento trees were one of the most valuable exports of colonial Jamaica, and pimento walks were significant economic assets. ## Notable Facts
Allspice (pimento) is the only major spice native to the New World, and Jamaica is the world's largest producer. The berry of the Pimenta dioica tree combines the flavours of cloves, nutmeg, and cinnamon — a quality that led the Spanish to name it 'allspice.' Pimento cultivation has been a cornerstone of the St. Elizabeth economy for centuries, and the community name Pimento Walk is a living record of this agricultural heritage.
