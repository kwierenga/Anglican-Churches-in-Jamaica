# St. Paul's Church (Old), Pimento Walk
**St. Elizabeth** · Church

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves Pimento Walk in St. Elizabeth, a small farming community in the parish's interior. The "(Old)" suffix in the church name suggests an older mission building, possibly distinguishing it from a newer or relocated congregation in the area.

## History
Pimento Walk takes its name from the pimento (allspice) tree — Pimenta dioica, native to Jamaica and one of the country's signature spice crops. A "walk" in Jamaican vernacular is a cultivated grove or planted area on an estate; "Pimento Walk" therefore identifies a settlement that grew up around an allspice plantation. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's as a small mission station to serve this farming community during the late-nineteenth-century rural church-planting expansion.

The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, which is consistent with a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Clergy
St. Paul's Pimento Walk has been administered within the network of St. Elizabeth Anglican mission churches grouped under the parish's cure churches in the Montego Bay Region.
