# St. Mary's, Southfield
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · unknown

St. Mary's Anglican Church serves the Southfield area of St. Elizabeth and is listed as a cure in the Southfield circuit alongside St. Mark's Mayfield, St. David's Morningside, and St. Alban's Stanmore, under the Southfield rectory. This is distinct from St. Mark's Mayfield (confirmed at on the Southfield Kinkead Main Road), which is also part of the same cure. St. Mary's and St. Mark's Mayfield are separate churches serving different parts of the Southfield/Southfield-Kinkead district.

## History
Southfield is a community in the coastal hills of southern St. Elizabeth. The Southfield cure is served by Olando Gayle from the Southfield rectory (tel: 963-1404) with Carol Vaughns (SM) as assistant, covering four churches across this part of the parish.

## Architecture
St. Mary's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Elizabeth in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The Southfield cure covering St. Mary's, St. Mark's Mayfield, St. David's Morningside, and St. Alban's Stanmore is served by Olando Gayle.

## Notable Facts
The Southfield area of St. Elizabeth is in the coastal hill country of the parish's southern edge. Mary, the mother of Jesus, is the most commonly dedicated saint in Jamaica's Anglican churches — reflecting the central place of Marian devotion in the Anglican tradition as it came to Jamaica in the 17th century. The feast of the Nativity of Mary (8 September) falls at the peak of hurricane season.
