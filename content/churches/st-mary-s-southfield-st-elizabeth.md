# St. Mary's, Southfield
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Mary's Anglican Church serves Southfield, a community in the upland country of southern St. Elizabeth.

## History
Southfield is a hill-country market community on the Santa Cruz Mountain plateau in southern St. Elizabeth, on the road between Junction and the south coast. The Anglican Diocese established St. Mary's as a mission chapel to serve this community as part of the wider rural church-planting expansion that the diocese carried out across St. Elizabeth from the late nineteenth century onward.

The dedication to St. Mary the Virgin — mother of Jesus, commemorated on multiple Anglican feast days including the Annunciation (25 March) and the "Falling Asleep" / Assumption (15 August) — is one of the most common Anglican dedications and gives the church a Marian character. (St. Mary the Virgin is the namesake of Jamaica's neighbouring St. Mary parish.)

No substantive foundational date or detailed architectural history specifically for St. Mary's Southfield has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed; the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, suggesting a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Architecture
St. Mary's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Elizabeth uplands. The Santa Cruz Mountain plateau position creates wind exposure during storm passages.

## Clergy
St. Mary's Southfield is served within the Montego Bay Region's St. Elizabeth deanery network.
