# St. James', New Roads (Westmoreland)
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. James' Anglican Church at New Roads is a small rural mission church within the Westmoreland Deanery of the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands. No substantive historical information about its foundation, architecture, or clergy has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed. The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica's Anglican returns — which recorded Lucea, Green Island, St. Saviour's, Church Hill, Dalmally, Chester Castle, and Hopewell as the main Hanover and border churches of the day — indicating that St. James' New Roads was established after that handbook was compiled.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Across the Diocese, some 50 church buildings were destroyed or significantly damaged. The specific status of St. James' New Roads after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
