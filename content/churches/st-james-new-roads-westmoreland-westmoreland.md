# St. James', New Roads (Westmoreland)
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. James' Anglican Church serves New Roads, a small community in the southern reaches of Westmoreland, in an area where the parish borders the south coast and the dry limestone country that characterises the south-western corner of Jamaica. The community is modest in size, and St. James' has served as its Anglican presence across many generations, providing worship, baptisms, burials, and community support to the families of the district.

## History
New Roads is one of the smaller communities scattered across the inland and southern fringe of Westmoreland, an area whose economic history is tied to small-scale farming and fishing rather than the great sugar estates of the central plain. The Anglican Church maintained a network of mission chapels across this southern fringe, reaching communities that had little access to the parish church at Savanna-la-Mar. St. James' represents this mission tradition, founded in the post-Emancipation period to serve a community of small farmers and their descendants.

## Architecture
St. James' is a modest masonry chapel of simple construction, typical of the smaller Anglican buildings erected across Westmoreland's southern communities in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The church is set within a small churchyard. The southern Westmoreland landscape, with its lower rainfall and limestone terrain, has distinct characteristics compared to the wetter coastal plain, and the building reflects the more austere conditions of this part of the parish.

## Clergy
New Roads has been served within the Westmoreland southern circuit, with shared clergy arrangements connecting it to other small churches in the region. Lay leaders have been essential to sustaining regular worship and community life. The church's role as a venue for funerals and family occasions confirms its active status as a meaningful institution for New Roads residents.

## Notable Facts
The southern coast of Westmoreland, while somewhat sheltered from the prevailing north-east trade winds, faces exposure to storms approaching from the south and south-west. Hurricane Charlie (1951) affected this coast, as did Hurricane Ivan (2004), which brought surge and high seas to the south-western shore. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) made landfall in western Westmoreland and the storm's path exposed the southern part of the parish to damaging winds and wave action along the coast; the New Roads community, being inland from the direct coast, was primarily affected by high winds and heavy rain.
