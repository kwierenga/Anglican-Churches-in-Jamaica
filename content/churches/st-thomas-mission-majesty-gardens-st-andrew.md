# St. Thomas Mission, Majesty Gardens
**St. Andrew** · Mission · active

St. Thomas Mission serves the **Majesty Gardens** community in St. Andrew, a densely populated inner-city neighbourhood in the greater Kingston metropolitan area. Majesty Gardens — known locally as "Back-to" — has been the site of the **St. Andrew Settlement** outreach by St. Andrew Parish Church since 1965.

## History
**English conquest (1655) and St. Andrew parish (1664).** Penn and Venables's English expedition captured Jamaica from Spain in May **1655**. **St. Andrew parish was established in 1664** with a 600-acre glebe granted in 1667 by Charles II.

**Port Royal, the 1692 earthquake, and the founding of Kingston (1693).** The **Great Earthquake of 7 June 1692** destroyed two-thirds of Port Royal; the new city of **Kingston** was formally established on **22 July 1692**, with the Assembly constituting Kingston as a parish in **1693**.

**Slavery, Diocese, Sam Sharpe, Emancipation, disestablishment.** Until **1870**, the Church of England in Jamaica was the established state church. The **Diocese of Jamaica** was established by Letters Patent on **24 July 1824**; **Christopher Lipscomb** was consecrated first Bishop in 1824. During the **1831 Sam Sharpe (Christmas) Rebellion**, planters and Anglican clergy formed the **Colonial Church Union**; **Sam Sharpe** was hanged on **23 May 1832** and is today a National Hero of Jamaica. Emancipation came in 1834, with full freedom on **1 August 1838**. Bishop Lipscomb (1824–1843) extended Anglican schools — 142 schools with 8,500 scholars by 1835. After the Morant Bay Rebellion of 1865 and Crown Colony rule (1866), the Anglican Church was disestablished in **1870**. Bishop **Enos Nuttall** (1880–1916) extended the rural mission network from 26 stations in 1880 to **122 by 1911**.

**1872 capital, 1907 earthquake, 1923 KSAC.** The capital moved from Spanish Town to **Kingston in 1872**. The **14 January 1907 earthquake** destroyed much of Kingston; under Nuttall's £50,000 reconstruction programme, ten churches were consecrated within a single month in **January 1911**. The **Kingston and St. Andrew Corporation (KSAC)** unified the two parishes for civic administration in **1923**.

**Foundation of St. Thomas Mission.** Majesty Gardens developed as an inner-city community of Kingston's urban expansion in the mid-20th century, growing rapidly as rural migration into the capital intensified. The **St. Andrew Settlement (SAS)**, founded in 1965 by the Anglican Diocese as an outreach project of **St. Andrew Parish Church**, serves the Majesty Gardens community off Spanish Town Road — home to over 4,000 residents, the majority living below the poverty line. SAS facilities include a community centre, mission house, medical centre, dental clinic, and two early childhood educational facilities. **In 2005, SAS received the *Jamaica Gleaner* "Man of the Year" Award** for its community work. St. Thomas Mission is the worship point at the heart of this outreach.

**Twentieth century and Independence.** Jamaica's independence on **6 August 1962** found St. Andrew Settlement about to be founded as a deliberate Anglican commitment to inner-city ministry. Bishop **Percival Gibson** (1957–1967), the first native-born Bishop of Jamaica, encouraged this kind of engagement.

**Hurricane Melissa (October 2025).** Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on **28 October 2025** — the strongest hurricane to strike Jamaica in recorded meteorological history. Hurricane Melissa brought wind and rain to Kingston, with low-lying inner-city communities particularly vulnerable to flooding.

## Architecture
St. Thomas Mission is an urban church in the Majesty Gardens community, in the low-lying coastal urban landscape of southern St. Andrew. The community's position in the greater Kingston metropolitan area creates particular vulnerability to flooding during heavy rainfall events.

## Clergy
Majesty Gardens has been served within the **St. Andrew urban deanery circuit**, anchored by St. Andrew Parish Church at Half-Way-Tree. The mission character of the church means it is closely supported by the wider Diocese's urban ministry resources through SAS.

## Notable Facts
The **St. Andrew Settlement** is one of the most established Anglican social-outreach projects in Jamaica — founded in **1965**, and recognised by the *Jamaica Gleaner* as "Man of the Year" in **2005**. The dedication to **St. Thomas the Apostle** — who doubted then proclaimed "My Lord and my God" (feast day **21 December**; 3 July in some traditions) — carries a resonance in a community where faith is tested by daily circumstances.
