# St. Thomas Mission, Majesty Gardens
**St. Andrew** · Mission · active

St. Thomas Mission serves the Majesty Gardens community in St. Andrew, a densely populated urban neighbourhood in the greater Kingston metropolitan area. Majesty Gardens is one of several inner-city communities in the parish with significant social challenges, and St. Thomas Mission represents the Anglican Diocese's commitment to pastoral presence in urban communities that most need it. The mission status reflects the church's position within a larger parish structure.

## History
Majesty Gardens developed as one of the inner-city communities of Kingston's urban expansion in the mid-20th century, growing rapidly as rural migration into the capital intensified. The Anglican Diocese established St. Thomas Mission to provide a pastoral presence in this community, serving families in a neighbourhood characterised by dense housing, limited economic opportunity, and ongoing social pressures. The church's mission status places it within the broader urban ministry network of the St. Andrew deanery.

## Architecture
St. Thomas Mission is an urban church in the Majesty Gardens community, in the low-lying coastal urban landscape of southern St. Andrew. The community's position in the greater Kingston metropolitan area creates particular vulnerability to flooding during heavy rainfall events, as the coastal plain drainage system is stressed during major storms.

## Clergy
Majesty Gardens has been served within the St. Andrew urban deanery circuit. The mission character of the church means it is closely supported by the wider Diocese's urban ministry resources.

## Notable Facts
Urban mission churches like St. Thomas Mission play a vital role in communities that face persistent social and economic challenges. The Anglican Diocese's presence in inner-city communities across Kingston and St. Andrew represents a tradition of urban ministry that has continued through the post-independence era. The church's dedication to St. Thomas the Apostle — known for his questioning faith — carries a resonance in a community where faith is tested by daily circumstances.
