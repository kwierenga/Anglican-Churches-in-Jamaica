# St. John's, Palmers Cross
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. John's Anglican Church serves Palmers Cross, a community on the south coast of Clarendon near the coastal fishing communities and the wetlands of the southern plain. Palmers Cross is a small coastal community and St. John's has served it for several generations.

## History
Palmers Cross sits on the south coast of Clarendon, in the flat coastal plain near the Caribbean Sea. The community has been shaped by coastal farming and fishing. The Anglican Diocese established St. John's to serve this community.

## Architecture
St. John's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Clarendon in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Palmers Cross has been served within the Clarendon deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Palmers Cross is one of the south coast communities of Clarendon on the Caribbean Sea, in an area of coastal wetlands and farming that has historically supported both agriculture and fishing.
