# St Alban's, Stanmore Hill
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · Active

St. Alban's Anglican Church is located at Stanmore Hill in St. Elizabeth parish. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica & The Cayman Islands directory and has a dedicated page on the diocesan website. A cemetery at the site is recorded on Find a Grave.

No verified historical information is currently available for this church.

## Recent coverage

In February 2026 St Alban's received J$1.3 million from the wound-up Mavis Fraser-Davis Trust to support pupils at the affiliated St Alban's Primary School and to establish a scholarship honouring the late educator ([Jamaica Gleaner, 19 Feb 2026](https://jamaica-gleaner.com/article/news/20260219/malvern-churches-get-26m-donation-late-educators-trust)).
