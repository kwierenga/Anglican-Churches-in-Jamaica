# St. Joseph's, Belvedere
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Joseph's Anglican Church serves Belvedere, a community on the Portland coast or coastal hills near Port Antonio. Belvedere is a residential and agricultural community in the Port Antonio area, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to local families for several generations. The dedication to St. Joseph, the foster father of Jesus, is relatively uncommon among Jamaican Anglican churches and gives this chapel a distinctive identity in the Portland network.

## History
Belvedere sits in the Port Antonio area of Portland, in the coastal and hillside community on the outskirts of the parish capital. The Anglican Diocese established St. Joseph's as a mission chapel to serve this community, dedicated to St. Joseph. The church has served the Belvedere community through the growth and transformation of the Port Antonio area across the 19th and 20th centuries.

## Architecture
St. Joseph's is a masonry chapel in the Belvedere community near Port Antonio. The coastal proximity of the Port Antonio area creates storm surge and wind exposure during hurricane passages, and the hillside terrain around Belvedere creates additional landslide risk during heavy rainfall events.

## Clergy
Belvedere has been served within the Port Antonio circuit of the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements, closely connected to Christ Church parish church.

## Notable Facts
The dedication to St. Joseph, while common in Roman Catholic churches worldwide, is unusual among Anglican foundations in Jamaica. The Belvedere community's proximity to Port Antonio has connected it to the wider fortunes of the parish capital, including the banana trade prosperity of the early 20th century. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) struck Port Antonio and its surrounding communities directly; Belvedere and St. Joseph's congregation were in the heart of the impact zone.
