## History
St. Alban’s Anglican Church was established as the local Church of England presence in Ashton, a district south of Bethel Town in Westmoreland. The region was a central part of Jamaica's sugar economy, reliant on enslaved African labour until Emancipation in the 1830s. Ashton later developed as a free village, similar to its neighbour Bethel Town. The church's long history is evidenced by Gleaner funeral notices from the early 2000s and earlier, which document services at St. Alban's followed by burial in local family plots. A 2025 feature article contrasts this past vitality—recalling well-attended Harvest services and rallies—with its current state of decline, illustrating the broader challenges facing traditional rural churches.
