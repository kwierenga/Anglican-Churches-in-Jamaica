# St. Paul's, Moore Town
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church at Moore Town serves one of Jamaica's most historically significant communities — a Maroon town in the Rio Grande Valley of Portland, officially known as one of the treaty Maroon communities recognised by the British Crown in 1739. Moore Town is the main town of the Windward Maroons of Portland, a community that maintained its freedom and distinct culture through resistance, treaty, and continuous habitation from the 17th century to the present. The Anglican church here has ministered to and alongside this remarkable community.

## History
Moore Town's Maroons are descendants of enslaved Africans who escaped from plantations and established free communities in the mountains of eastern Jamaica. The Windward Maroons, led by the legendary Nanny of the Maroons — now a Jamaican National Hero — negotiated a peace treaty with the British in 1739 that granted them freedom and land rights. Moore Town became the administrative centre of the Windward Maroon territory. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's to serve Moore Town's community, navigating the complex relationship between the established church and a community with its own independent religious and cultural traditions. Google Places confirms the church at its Moore Town location.

## Architecture
St. Paul's is a masonry church in the Rio Grande Valley. The valley setting between the Blue Mountains and John Crow Mountains creates extraordinary rainfall — Moore Town is one of the wettest inhabited places in Jamaica. The church's construction reflects the requirements of a building in a high-rainfall, high-humidity valley environment. The surrounding mountains create flooding and landslide risk during hurricane passages.

## Clergy
Moore Town has been served within the Portland deanery. The unique character of the Maroon community has shaped the relationship between the Anglican church and its Moore Town congregation across many generations, with the church operating within a community that has its own distinct spiritual and cultural traditions alongside Anglican worship.

## Notable Facts
Moore Town is the site of the grave and ceremonial ground associated with Nanny of the Maroons, Jamaica's only female National Hero, who led the Windward Maroons in their resistance to British colonial power. The community's Maroon heritage is protected under Jamaican law and the ongoing treaty relationships. St. Paul's Anglican church exists within this extraordinary historical context. The Rio Grande Valley — Portland's main river valley — is subject to catastrophic flooding during major rainfall events. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought extraordinary rainfall to Portland's interior; the Rio Grande Valley and Moore Town were severely affected.
