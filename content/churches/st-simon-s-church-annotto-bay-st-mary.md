# St. Simon's Church, Annotto Bay
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Simon’s Anglican Church serves the Annotto Bay district of St. Mary, one of several Anglican worship points in this north-coast town and its surrounding area. Annotto Bay is a modest coastal community at the mouth of the Pencar River with a history shaped by the banana trade and the fishing economy. St. Simon’s, dedicated to the apostle known as Simon the Zealot, serves part of the Anglican community in the town or its immediate surrounding district.

## History
Annotto Bay was one of the banana-shipping ports of St. Mary’s north coast, and the Anglican Diocese maintained multiple churches and mission points in the town and district to serve its varied population. St. Simon’s developed as part of this network, serving a specific community within the broader Annotto Bay area. Multiple Anglican dedications in the same town reflect the Diocese’s practice of planting mission chapels in different parts of larger communities or in nearby districts that fell within the same civic address.

## Architecture
St. Simon’s is a coastal church in the Annotto Bay area, directly exposed to the north-coast weather system. The church’s position near the river mouth creates compound storm risk from surge and flooding. Its construction is suited to this coastal environment.

## Clergy
St. Simon’s has been served within the Annotto Bay circuit of the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements with the other Anglican churches in the area.

## Notable Facts
The presence of multiple Anglican churches in Annotto Bay — St. James’, St. Margaret’s, and St. Simon’s — reflects the Diocese’s historically dense network of worship points in the coastal towns of St. Mary. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious damage along the St. Mary north coast. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along the north coast and Annotto Bay was directly in the storm’s path.
