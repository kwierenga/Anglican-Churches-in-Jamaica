# St. Mary's, Rural Hill
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Mary's Anglican Church serves Rural Hill, a small community in the interior of Portland parish in the hilly country south-east of Port Antonio. Rural Hill is a farming district in the transition zone between the coastal plain and the higher hill country of eastern Portland, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations.

## History
Rural Hill developed as a small farming settlement in the interior hills of Portland, part of the agricultural landscape that extended inland from the banana-producing coastal plain. The Anglican Diocese established St. Mary's as a mission chapel to serve the Rural Hill community, dedicated to the Virgin Mary and thus carrying the same dedication as the adjacent parish to the west. The church has served the community through the transformations of Portland's agricultural economy.

## Architecture
St. Mary's is a small masonry chapel in the interior hills of Portland, set in the Rural Hill farming community. The church's elevated position gives it some natural protection from coastal surge while exposing it to the heavy orographic rainfall that characterises Portland's interior — one of the wettest parishes in Jamaica. The area's hilly terrain creates landslide and road-closure risk during major storm events.

## Clergy
Rural Hill has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. The interior location means the church depends on lay leaders for its regular pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
Portland is consistently recorded as one of the wettest parishes in Jamaica, with the Blue Mountains and John Crow Mountains generating extraordinary annual rainfall totals in the interior. Rural Hill sits in this high-rainfall landscape. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious landslide damage across Portland's interior. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to Portland; the Rural Hill community and congregation were in the affected zone.
