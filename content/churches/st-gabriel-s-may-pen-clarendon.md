# St. Gabriel's, May Pen
**Clarendon** · Parish Church · active

St. Gabriel's Anglican Church is the principal confirmed Anglican church of May Pen, located at 10 Church Street, Reviewers describe it as a well-maintained church with a cemetery on the grounds, a spacious hall with cooking area and bathrooms suitable for events, 'traditional ancient architecture,' and a sincerely worshipping congregation. May Pen is the commercial hub of Clarendon, and St. Gabriel's serves as its primary Anglican church.

## History
May Pen is the largest town in Clarendon, situated on the plains of the Rio Minho valley on the main road between Kingston and the western parishes. St. Gabriel's at Church Street has served as the principal Anglican church of May Pen, providing the community's primary Anglican ministry. The church has served through the development of May Pen from a small market town to one of the most significant urban centres of central Jamaica.

## Architecture
St. Gabriel's is built in the tradition of Jamaican parish churches, with solid masonry walls and a nave proportioned to serve a substantial town congregation. The building carries the civic weight of its status as a mother church, with interior furnishings and memorial tablets accumulated over generations of parish life. The churchyard contains graves spanning the colonial and post-colonial periods, among the more historically layered burial grounds in Clarendon. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
May Pen's Anglican ministry is centred on St. Gabriel's. The church has maintained an active congregation and provides its hall for community events and functions. A reviewer notes the traditional Anglican form of worship — open-eyed prayer from a prayer book — as distinctive and sincere.

## Notable Facts
The archangel Gabriel's dedication is relatively uncommon in Jamaica's Anglican network — this is one of only a small number of churches dedicated to an archangel rather than an apostle or saint. Gabriel, the messenger angel who announced the Incarnation to Mary, is honoured on 29 September (Michaelmas) jointly with Michael and Raphael. The dedication gives May Pen's principal Anglican church a notably celestial character.
