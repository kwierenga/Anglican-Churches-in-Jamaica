# St. Stephen's, Arlington
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Stephen's Anglican Church serves Arlington, a community in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth parish.

## History
Arlington is one of the small upland farming districts of St. Elizabeth — one of several Jamaican community names transferred from English places by colonial-era estate proprietors (Arlington being a noted English country house and a London-area place name). The Anglican Diocese established St. Stephen's as a small mission chapel to serve this community as part of the wider rural church-planting expansion of the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

The dedication to St. Stephen — the first Christian martyr, deacon of the early Jerusalem church, stoned to death for his preaching as recorded in Acts 7 — is among the most ancient Christian dedications. His feast day on 26 December (the day after Christmas) makes "Boxing Day" a particularly significant day for Stephen-dedicated churches.

No substantive foundational date or architectural history for St. Stephen's Arlington has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed; the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, suggesting a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Architecture
St. Stephen's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Elizabeth interior. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge while remaining exposed to wind and rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. Stephen's Arlington is served within the Montego Bay Region's St. Elizabeth deanery network.
