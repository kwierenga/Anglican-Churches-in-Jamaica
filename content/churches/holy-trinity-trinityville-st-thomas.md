# Church of the Holy Trinity, Trinityville
**St. Thomas** · Church · active

The Church of the Holy Trinity serves Trinityville in St. Thomas parish — a community whose name derives directly from the church itself, in the wider Jamaican tradition where the Anglican mission station gave its name to the surrounding settlement.

## History
Trinityville is one of the upland communities of St. Thomas, in the rural farming country of the parish. The community's name preserves the practice — common in post-Emancipation Jamaica — by which a new free village or settlement took its name from the chapel that became the social and spiritual centre of community life. (Sligoville, Freetown, Faith's, and others fit the same pattern.) The Anglican Diocese established the Holy Trinity here as a mission chapel; the dedication then defined the name of the surrounding district, reflecting the church's foundational role.

St. Thomas Parish itself has a deep Anglican history: the parish church at Morant Bay was a brick building erected in 1865 after the original eighteenth-century parish church at Church Corner had fallen into dilapidation. The 1865 Morant Bay Rebellion, led by Paul Bogle, made the parish a focal point in Jamaica's nineteenth-century social and political history; its aftermath shaped the constitutional re-organisation of Jamaican governance and the redrawing of the island's parishes (1867, fourteen parishes from twenty-two).

The dedication to the Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — is one of the most traditional Anglican dedications, shared with the Diocese's Cathedral on North Street, Kingston (Holy Trinity Cathedral, 1911) and with the Holy Trinity at Old Harbour and Holy Trinity at Westgate, Montego Bay.

## Architecture
The Church of the Holy Trinity at Trinityville is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Thomas interior. The hill-country position creates exposure to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Holy Trinity Trinityville is served within the Diocese's Eastern Jamaica Region.
