# Church of the Holy Spirit, Pepper
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · unknown

The Church of the Holy Spirit serves Pepper in St. Elizabeth, listed as a cure in the Santa Cruz circuit alongside St. Matthew's Santa Cruz, St. Andrew's Gilnock, St. Stephen's Nain, The Transfiguration Leeds, and St. James' Mount Hermon mission. Pepper is a community in the interior of St. Elizabeth near the Santa Cruz area. The Holy Spirit dedication — one of only a handful of such dedications in Jamaica — gives this church a distinctive Pentecostal-flavoured name within the Anglican liturgical tradition.

## History
Pepper sits in the interior of St. Elizabeth in the Santa Cruz circuit area. The church is part of the cure served by Hilda Vaughan from Main Street, Santa Cruz P.O. (tel: 966-4520) with Teddy Blake (SM) as assistant.

## Architecture
Church of the Holy Spirit is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Elizabeth in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The Santa Cruz cure covering Pepper is served by Hilda Vaughan from the Santa Cruz rectory.

## Notable Facts
Dedicating a church to the Holy Spirit rather than to a specific saint or to Christ is relatively uncommon in Anglican tradition — the Spirit is more often honoured in Pentecost Sunday observances than in permanent church dedications. The name Pepper suggests a community associated with pepper cultivation, or alternatively preserves the family name of a colonial proprietor. St. Elizabeth has historically been a significant pepper-growing area alongside its yam and pimento production.
