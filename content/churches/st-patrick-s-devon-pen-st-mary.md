# St. Patrick's, Devon Pen
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Patrick’s Anglican Church serves Devon Pen, a community in the interior of St. Mary parish. The dedication to St. Patrick, the patron of Ireland, reflects the significant Irish presence among Jamaica’s colonial plantation class, many of whom came from Ireland or were of Irish descent and gave Irish names to their estates and communities across the island. Devon Pen is a farming district, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations.

## History
Devon Pen is a small interior community of St. Mary whose name preserves the memory of the plantation era in the parish. Many pen names in Jamaica denote livestock-raising estates rather than sugar plantations — a ‘pen’ was a stock farm. The Devon Pen community developed from such an estate landscape, and the Anglican Diocese established St. Patrick’s as part of its mission outreach into the interior communities of the parish after Emancipation.

## Architecture
St. Patrick's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Mary in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Mary. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Devon Pen has been served within the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church’s regular pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
The Irish connection in Jamaican place names — reflected in dedications like St. Patrick’s, and in estate names like Clonmel elsewhere in the parish — testifies to the significant role of Irish settlers and overseers in the plantation economy of Jamaica. St. Mary has a notable cluster of Irish-associated place names. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to interior St. Mary; the Devon Pen community and congregation were in the affected zone.
