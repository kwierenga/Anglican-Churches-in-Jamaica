# St. Boniface, Harbour View
**Kingston** · Church · unknown

St. Boniface Anglican Church serves Harbour View in the eastern extremity of Kingston, at the point where the city meets the Palisadoes peninsula and the road to Port Royal. St. Boniface is the main cure of a circuit that includes St. Peter's Port Royal and the mission of St. Martin's Bull Bay. The church office is at St. Thomas Main Road, Harbour View, Kingston 17, tel: 938-6712/938-0712. It is one of the most geographically exposed Anglican churches in Jamaica, at the edge of Kingston Harbour.

## History
Harbour View occupies the eastern end of the Palisadoes strip — the narrow tombolo connecting Port Royal to the mainland. The area is at high hurricane surge and flood risk from both Kingston Harbour to the north and the Caribbean Sea to the south. St. Boniface serves Harbour View alongside St. Peter's Port Royal (the historic parish church of the old pirate capital) and the Bull Bay mission. Sunday services include Holy Eucharist at 7:30am on the 1st, 3rd and 4th Sundays, with Sunday School at 8am and the Anglican Youth Fellowship at 10am.

## Architecture
St. Boniface is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Kingston in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the coastal landscape of Kingston. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
The cure is served by Kirk Brown. The circuit encompasses three distinct communities: the modern residential area of Harbour View, the historic Port Royal (former pirate capital and now a fishing village), and the coastal community of Bull Bay.

## Notable Facts
St. Boniface of Crediton (c. 675–754) was an Anglo-Saxon missionary who evangelised the Germanic peoples and is sometimes called the Apostle of Germany. His dedication in Harbour View, at the eastern gateway to Kingston Harbour, connects this exposed coastal community to one of the great missionary saints of the early Church. The Harbour View cure was damaged by Hurricane Melissa in October 2025 along with numerous other Diocese churches in the Kingston area.
