# St. James', Hayes
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. James' Anglican Church serves Hayes, a community in southern Clarendon near the coast and the areas associated with bauxite mining and industrial processing. Hayes is near the Jamalco alumina processing plant, one of the major industrial facilities on the south coast of Clarendon. The church has served the Hayes community through the industrial transformation of southern Clarendon.

## History
Hayes sits in the southern coastal plain of Clarendon, in an area that has been significantly shaped by the bauxite and alumina industry. The Jamalco facility processes bauxite from the interior parishes and has been a major employer in the area. The Anglican Diocese established St. James' to serve the Hayes community.

## Architecture
St. James' is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Clarendon in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Hayes has been served within the Clarendon deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Hayes is one of the communities closest to the Jamalco alumina processing facility on the south coast of Clarendon. The bauxite and alumina industry has been a defining feature of the Clarendon south coast landscape for several decades, and the communities here reflect the mix of agricultural and industrial employment that characterises this part of Jamaica.
