# St. Matthew's, Burnt Savanna
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Burnt Savanna in St. Elizabeth, a community in the parish's southern plain. The chapel is one of a network of small mission stations the Diocese of Jamaica established across the southern districts of St. Elizabeth.

## History
Burnt Savanna is one of the small farming settlements of southern St. Elizabeth, in the agricultural country between the Black River Morass and the Santa Cruz Mountains. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew's as a mission chapel during the late-nineteenth-century rural church-planting expansion under Bishop Enos Nuttall. The dedication to Matthew the evangelist (feast day 21 September) is a common one for late-nineteenth-century mission foundations.

The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, which is consistent with a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Clergy
St. Matthew's Burnt Savanna has been administered within the network of St. Elizabeth Anglican mission churches grouped under the parish's cure churches in the Montego Bay Region.
