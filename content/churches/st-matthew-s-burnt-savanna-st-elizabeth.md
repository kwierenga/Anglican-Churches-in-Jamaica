# St. Matthew's, Burnt Savanna
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Burnt Savanna, a community in St. Elizabeth parish. Burnt Savanna is a community in the interior of the parish, its name evoking the characteristic dry savanna landscape of parts of St. Elizabeth — a landscape shaped by the rain shadow of the Santa Cruz Mountains that gives the southern plain one of the driest climates in Jamaica. The church has served this community for several generations.

## History
Burnt Savanna sits in St. Elizabeth, in the agricultural landscape of the parish. The name 'burnt savanna' refers to the practice of burning the dry savanna grassland at the end of the dry season — a land management technique used across Jamaica's drier districts to clear land and stimulate new growth. St. Elizabeth is one of Jamaica's driest parishes, and savanna burning was a regular part of the agricultural calendar.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Elizabeth. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Burnt Savanna has been served within the St. Elizabeth deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
St. Elizabeth's relative dryness — caused by the rain shadow of the Santa Cruz Mountains on the north side of the parish — makes it distinctive among Jamaica's parishes. The parish is famous for its yam cultivation (it is sometimes called 'the breadbasket of Jamaica'), with the sandy-loam soils and dry climate producing the finest yams on the island. Communities named for savanna features, like Burnt Savanna, reflect this dry-landscape character.
