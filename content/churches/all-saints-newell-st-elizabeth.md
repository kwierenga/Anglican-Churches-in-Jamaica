# All Saints', Newell
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · unknown

All Saints' Anglican Church serves Newell in St. Elizabeth, listed as a mission under the Pedro Plains cure. Newell is a community in the southern part of St. Elizabeth near Pedro Plains, in the coastal plain of the parish. The church extends the Pedro Plains cure's ministry across this southern coastal farming district.

## History
Newell sits in the southern coastal plain of St. Elizabeth near Pedro Plains. The Pedro Plains cure is served by Marjorie Downer and covers St. Peter's Pedro Plains, St. Augustine's Mountainside, St. Matthew's Orange Grove, St. Paul's Barbary Hall, All Saints' Newell, and the Epiphany mission at Big Woods.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Elizabeth in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The Pedro Plains cure covering Newell is served by Marjorie Downer with Milton Russell (SM) and Beulah Rowe (SM) as assistants.

## Notable Facts
Newell is a family name common among English and Irish colonial settlers. The Pedro Plains area is one of the driest parts of St. Elizabeth, on the south coastal plain. The 'Pedros' refer to the Pedro Bluffs and Pedro Cays offshore — named from the Spanish era. All Saints' dedication, commemorating the full company of the faithful on 1 November, gives this coastal farming community a liturgical identity connected to the entire community of faith.
