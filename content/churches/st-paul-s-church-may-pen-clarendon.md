# St. Paul's Church, May Pen
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves May Pen, the largest town in Clarendon and the commercial hub of the parish, located on the plains of southern Clarendon near the Rio Minho river. May Pen is one of the most significant urban centres of central Jamaica, and St. Paul's provides Anglican ministry in the town alongside St. Gabriel's, which is the confirmed Anglican church at Church Street, May Pen.

## History
May Pen developed as the principal commercial town of Clarendon on the plains of the Rio Minho valley, and the Anglican Diocese established multiple worship points to serve this growing urban community. St. Paul's and St. Gabriel's together provide Anglican coverage for different parts of the May Pen community.

## Architecture
St. Paul's Church is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Clarendon. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
May Pen has been served within the Clarendon deanery. The town's multiple Anglican churches reflect its size as the largest urban centre in the parish.

## Notable Facts
May Pen is the commercial capital of Clarendon and one of the most active market towns in central Jamaica. The Rio Minho, which flows through the May Pen area to the south coast, has historically created serious flooding problems during major storm events — a recurring challenge for the low-lying communities of southern Clarendon.
