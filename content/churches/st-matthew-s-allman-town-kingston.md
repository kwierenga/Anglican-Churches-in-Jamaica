# St. Matthew's, Allman Town
**Kingston** · Church · unknown

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves the Allman Town area of Kingston, located at 24–26 Gordon Street (formerly Hitchen Street), Reviewers describe it as one of the iconic Anglican churches of Jamaica, now 119 years old and located a short distance from the National Heroes Park, noting it has stories to tell about Jamaica's pre- and post-colonial history. The church retains a pipe organ, mahogany pews, stained glass windows over the altar, and a Flower Guild that provides fresh floral arrangements — preserving a tradition of Anglican choral worship in the heart of the city.

## History
St. Matthew's was established in the late 19th century and has stood for over 119 years at its Gordon Street location in Allman Town, near the National Heroes Park. The church has witnessed the full span of Kingston's modern history — from the 1907 earthquake that devastated much of the city, through independence in 1962, to the present day. It sits near the National Heroes Park, the principal commemorative space for Jamaica's national heroes, giving it a proximity to the nation's most symbolic public landscape. The church has served generations of Kingston families and maintained its choral and liturgical traditions across more than a century.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a substantial stone church on Gordon Street, Allman Town. Reviewers describe stained glass windows over the altar as 'stunning,' warm mahogany pews, and a magnificent pipe organ that 'still plays those stirring hymns.' The Flower Guild provides fresh floral arrangements at services. The downtown Kingston location creates flooding vulnerability during major storm events, as the low-lying coastal plain of Kingston is exposed to surge during powerful hurricanes.

## Clergy
St. Matthew's has maintained an active clerical and lay ministry in central Kingston for over a century. The church's proximity to the National Heroes Park and its central location in the city have made it a venue for memorial services, funerals, and community events of significance. The congregation is described as warm and family-oriented, with long-standing family connections to the church.

## Notable Facts
The National Heroes Park, immediately adjacent to St. Matthew's, is the principal national memorial space of Jamaica, containing the tombs of several National Heroes including Norman Manley and Alexander Bustamante. The church's 119-year presence beside this national space gives it a unique connection to Jamaica's commemorative landscape. A reviewer records that a funeral service at St. Matthew's was 'a beautiful celebration' of life, with the stained glass, mahogany pews, and pipe organ creating a setting that many Kingstonians associate with the most significant moments of their family lives.
