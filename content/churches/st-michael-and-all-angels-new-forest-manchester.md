# St. Michael and All Angels, New Forest
**Manchester** · Church · active

St. Michael and All Angels Anglican Church serves New Forest, a community on the south coast of Manchester parish. New Forest is a coastal community near the southern edge of the parish, and the church has served this community for several generations. The full dedication — Michael and All Angels — is one of the grandest Anglican church names, encompassing the entire angelic company.

## History
New Forest sits in the southern coastal area of Manchester, near the Caribbean Sea. The community has developed in the relatively flat coastal plain at the base of the Manchester hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Michael and All Angels to serve this south coast community.

## Architecture
St. Michael and All Angels is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Manchester in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Manchester. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
New Forest has been served within the Manchester deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
The feast of Michael and All Angels — Michaelmas, 29 September — is one of the most ancient and important feasts in the Christian calendar. It falls at the autumn equinox and has been associated with the harvest season across centuries of English tradition. In Jamaica, September is in the height of hurricane season, giving Michaelmas churches a particular seasonal resonance on a south coast where storms are a living reality.
