# St. Mark's, Mayfield
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Mark's Anglican Church serves Mayfield, a community in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth parish.

## History
Mayfield is one of the small farming districts of St. Elizabeth — the community name preserves a colonial-era estate name (Mayfield being a recurring English place name, found in Sussex and other counties). The Anglican Diocese established St. Mark's as a small mission chapel to serve this community as part of the rural church-planting expansion that the diocese carried out across St. Elizabeth from the late nineteenth century onward.

The dedication to St. Mark the Evangelist — author of the second Gospel, traditionally associated with Alexandria, with a feast day on 25 April — is shared with several other Jamaican Anglican churches, including the Manchester Parish Church (St. Mark's Mandeville, consecrated 1820) and St. Mark's at Siloah in St. Elizabeth itself. The Diocese of Jamaica records a "St Mark Anglican, St Elizabeth" entry in the Jamaican Ancestral Records cemetery index.

No substantive foundational date or architectural history specifically for St. Mark's Mayfield has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed; the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, suggesting a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Architecture
St. Mark's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Elizabeth interior. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge while remaining exposed to wind and rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. Mark's Mayfield is served within the Montego Bay Region's St. Elizabeth deanery network.
