# St. Martin's Mission, Bull Bay
**St. Thomas** · Church · active

St. Martin's Anglican Mission serves the Bull Bay community on the south coast of Jamaica, ten miles east of Kingston on the boundary between St. Andrew and St. Thomas. The mission has been ministering in the Bull Bay community since the 1800s.

## History
Bull Bay is a coastal village on the highway east from Kingston toward Yallahs and Morant Bay, in the narrow coastal strip between the southern slopes of the Blue Mountains and the Caribbean Sea. The community sits administratively on the boundary line between St. Andrew (Kingston Region) and St. Thomas; St. Martin's Mission belongs in our inventory and in the diocese's Eastern Jamaica Region directory under St. Thomas.

The Anglican Diocese established St. Martin's as a mission chapel during the nineteenth century to serve this coastal community. The mission's own public communications confirm the nineteenth-century founding ("ministering in the Bull Bay community since the 1800s"). St. Thomas Parish itself has a deep Anglican history; the parish church at Morant Bay was a brick building erected in 1865 after the original eighteenth-century parish church at Church Corner had become dilapidated. The wider parish was the site of the 1865 Morant Bay Rebellion, in which Paul Bogle led the protest that became one of the foundational events in Jamaica's road to political and social reform.

The dedication to St. Martin of Tours — fourth-century soldier-turned-bishop of Tours in Gaul, famous for the legend of cutting his cloak in half to share with a beggar, with a feast day on 11 November — is one of the more theologically distinctive Anglican dedications in the Jamaican network. Martin's association with charity and care for the poor gives the mission an apt patron.

## Architecture
St. Martin's is a coastal mission building situated in the Bull Bay community. The exposed coastal position on the south coast creates direct vulnerability to storm surge during major hurricanes.

## Clergy
St. Martin's Mission, Bull Bay is served within the Diocese's Eastern Jamaica Region.
