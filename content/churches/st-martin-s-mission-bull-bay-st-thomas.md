# St. Martin's Mission, Bull Bay
**St. Thomas** · Mission · inactive

St. Martin's Mission is the Anglican church serving Bull Bay, a community on the coast of St. Thomas parish at the westernmost edge of the parish near the St. Andrew boundary. Reviewers describe it as 'Bull Bay's first Anglican Church' with a warm and welcoming congregation and a non-traditional worship style.

## History
Bull Bay developed as a coastal community on the south-east coast of Jamaica at the boundary between St. Thomas and St. Andrew, growing as a fishing community and later expanding as a suburban settlement within reach of Kingston. The Anglican Diocese established St. Martin's Mission to serve Bull Bay's community, dedicated to Martin of Tours — the soldier-saint associated with generosity and the sharing of resources, a fitting patron for a mission church in a fishing community. The mission status reflects the church's position within a larger parish structure rather than as an independent parish.

## Architecture
St. Martin's Mission is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Thomas. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the coastal landscape of St. Thomas. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
St. Martin's Mission has been served within the St. Thomas deanery, with its mission status linking it to a larger parish circuit. The church has developed a distinctive congregational identity, described by reviewers as 'not the ordinary/traditional Anglican church.'

## Notable Facts
A reviewer's description of St. Martin's as 'Bull Bay first Anglican Church' places it as a historically pioneering Anglican presence in a community that was relatively late to be reached by the Diocese's mission network. Bull Bay's coastal position between Kingston and the St. Thomas south coast gives it a character shaped by both urban proximity and rural fishing tradition. Hurricane Ivan (2004) struck this part of the south coast severely. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought impacts to the Bull Bay coastline.
