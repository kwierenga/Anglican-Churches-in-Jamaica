# St. Philip's, Mount Felix
**St. Thomas** · Mission · active

St. Philip's Anglican Church provides a second worship point in the Mount Felix area of St. Thomas, alongside St. Augustine's also recorded at this community. Mount Felix is a small farming district in the interior of St. Thomas, and the presence of two Anglican entries in the area reflects either a dispersed community served by separate chapels in different parts of the Mount Felix district, or historical uncertainty about the precise location of worship points in this part of the parish.

## History
The Mount Felix district of St. Thomas sits in the interior hill country between Port Morant and Morant Bay. Two Anglican worship points in the same community name suggest either a geographically dispersed settlement served by two chapels, or a historical recording duplication. St. Philip's serves its part of the Mount Felix community, dedicated to the apostle Philip who brought others to Christ.

## Architecture
St. Philip's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Thomas. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the hilly interior of St. Thomas. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Mount Felix has been served within the St. Thomas deanery under shared clergy arrangements. The two worship points in the area likely share the same circuit clergy.
