# St. Matthew's, Jackson Town
**Trelawny** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Jackson Town, a small rural community in central Trelawny. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory within the Trelawny Deanery of the Montego Bay Region, and is documented in the National Library of Jamaica's March 1931 photographic survey of Diocesan churches. No substantive foundational or architectural history has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed. The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica's Anglican returns — indicating a post-1891 foundation, most likely during Bishop Enos Nuttall's rural expansion of mission stations.

## History
Trelawny was created as a parish in 1770 out of St. James. Its economy was built on slavery, and after Emancipation (1834) and full freedom (1 August 1838), freed people established inland settlements across the parish. Jackson Town is one of several small communities whose Anglican churches were founded in the post-disestablishment decades to serve the emerging free-village landscape. The 1891 Handbook does not record a Jackson Town station; by 1931, the Diocese's photographic survey documents St. Matthew's as an existing church.

The dedication is to Matthew the Evangelist — apostle, tax collector, and traditional author of the first Gospel. His feast day is 21 September.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a rural masonry chapel. No detailed architectural description has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed, though the 1931 National Library of Jamaica image records the building as it stood early in the twentieth century.

## Clergy
St. Matthew's is served within the Trelawny Deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Trelawny was among the five most affected parishes. The specific status of St. Matthew's Jackson Town after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
