# St. Stephen's Church, Malvern
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Stephen's Anglican Church serves Malvern, a community in the hill country of St. Elizabeth. Malvern is a notable community in the interior hills of the parish, known as the location of several educational institutions including Munro College, one of Jamaica's foremost secondary schools. The church has served the Malvern community for several generations in this elevated, cool-climate environment.

## History
Malvern sits in the hills of St. Elizabeth at significant elevation, with a climate notably cooler than the coastal plain below. The area became associated with education from an early period — Munro College, founded in 1856, and the Hampton School are both located near Malvern, giving the area a particular character as an educational centre in the hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Stephen's to serve the Malvern community.

## Architecture
St. Stephen's Church is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Elizabeth. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Malvern has been served within the St. Elizabeth deanery. The proximity of Munro College and Hampton School has given Malvern's churches a connection to the educational life of these schools and their students.

## Notable Facts
Munro College, situated near Malvern, is one of Jamaica's most celebrated secondary schools, consistently producing prominent alumni in law, medicine, politics, and public life. The school was founded in 1856 under the will of Hugh Munro, a Scottish plantation owner who endowed the institution. Hampton School for girls is also in the area. The combination of educational institutions and the cool climate has given Malvern a distinct character in St. Elizabeth.
