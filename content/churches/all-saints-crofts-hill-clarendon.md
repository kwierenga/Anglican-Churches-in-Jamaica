# All Saints, Crofts Hill
**Clarendon** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church at Crofts Hill is the cure church for a group of rural Anglican congregations in interior northern Clarendon. Together with its mission stations — St. Bartholomew's at Good Hope, St. Peter's at Rhoden Hall, and St. Michael's at Arthur's Seat — All Saints' serves the upland farming districts north of the parish capital. The churchyard contains one of the larger inscribed cemeteries in the deanery, with 187 catalogued memorials.

## History
Crofts Hill is an interior community in the hill country of northern Clarendon, settled in the post-Emancipation period as freed people moved into the parish's upland districts in search of land for provision farming. The Anglican Diocese established All Saints' to serve this community as a cure parish, with mission chapels founded across the surrounding districts. The All Saints' dedication — commemorating the full company of saints on 1 November — is consistent with the late-nineteenth-century pattern of dedicating new rural churches to general categories of holiness rather than to single named patrons.

The cure is listed as **VACANT** in the most recent diocesan directory, with the parsonage at Croft's Hill P.O. (telephone 966-7609). That address serves as the contact point for All Saints' itself and for the missions of the cure.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a substantial rural masonry church, larger than the surrounding mission chapels in keeping with its role as cure church. The hillside position gives some defensive value against coastal flooding but increases exposure to wind during major storms. The Diocese of Jamaica maintains a directory image of the church among its online photographs.

## Clergy
All Saints' Crofts Hill is the parent church of a four-church cure with mission stations at Good Hope, Rhoden Hall, and Arthur's Seat. The cure is currently vacant; pastoral oversight has historically rotated among neighbouring Clarendon clergy during vacancies.
