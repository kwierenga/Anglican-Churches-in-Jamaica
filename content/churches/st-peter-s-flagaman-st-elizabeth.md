# St. Peter's, Flagaman
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Peter's Anglican Church serves Flagaman, a community in the southern coastal area of St. Elizabeth. Flagaman is a coastal community near the southern edge of the parish, and the church has served this community for several generations. The dedication to St. Peter — the rock on which Christ said he would build his church — is one of the most common in Jamaica's Anglican network.

## History
Flagaman sits in the southern coastal area of St. Elizabeth, in the agricultural landscape near the south coast. The Anglican Diocese established St. Peter's to serve this community. The church has maintained an Anglican presence in this southern district alongside St. Peter's at Pedro Plains, also recorded in the southern part of the parish.

## Architecture
St. Peter's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Elizabeth. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Elizabeth. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Flagaman has been served within the St. Elizabeth deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Flagaman is in the southern coastal area of St. Elizabeth, in the part of the parish that faces the Caribbean Sea. The south coast of St. Elizabeth and neighbouring Westmoreland are among the areas most directly exposed to hurricanes tracking on the southern track through the Caribbean. The community of Flagaman sits in the low coastal landscape that faces this exposure.
