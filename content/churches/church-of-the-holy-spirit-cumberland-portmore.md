# Church of the Holy Spirit, Cumberland
**St. Catherine** · Church · unknown

The Church of the Holy Spirit serves Cumberland, Gregory Park in the Portmore deanery. Cumberland is one of the major housing scheme areas of Portmore, and the Anglican church here is served alongside the mission of St. Andrew's Caymanas. The Portmore deanery covers Jamaica's most densely settled dormitory district, and the Church of the Holy Spirit represents the Anglican presence in the eastern part of the deanery.

## History
Cumberland Road, Gregory Park is in the eastern part of the Portmore deanery. The church serves the Cumberland and Caymanas communities. The deanery address is 2 Cumberland Road, Gregory Park P.O., tel: 939-2569.

## Architecture
Church of the Holy Spirit is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The cure is served by Paul Sharpe with Anglican Evangelist assistants O'Dayne Plummer and Dawn Ledgister.

## Notable Facts
The Caymanas area served by this cure's mission church was historically the site of Caymanas Estate, one of the largest sugar plantations in St. Catherine. The estate name survives in several community names in this part of the Portmore/St. Catherine area. The shift from plantation estate to Anglican mission to Anglican church mirrors the broader transformation of Jamaican society from the sugar economy to the urban housing schemes of the late 20th century.
