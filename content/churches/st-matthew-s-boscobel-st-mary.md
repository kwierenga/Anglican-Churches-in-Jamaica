# St. Matthew's, Boscobel
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church in Boscobel, founded around 1900, serves as a daughter church of Holy Trinity Retreat. The church shares its campus with Boscobel Primary School, creating an educational and spiritual hub. During Hurricane Melissa (2025), the school served as a damaged shelter while the community faced widespread power and travel disruptions.

## History
Established around 1900 as one of Holy Trinity's four daughter churches, St. Matthew's has ministered to Boscobel's coastal community through colonial rule, independence, and tourism development. The church maintains its Anglican lineage while serving predominantly Jamaican families whose ancestors worked local estates.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's features traditional country church architecture with an adjacent cemetery. The shared church-school campus creates an integrated community space, though the aging building faces maintenance challenges including roof repairs and termite damage addressed through community fundraising events.

## Clergy
As part of a shared pastoral group, St. Matthew's is served by rotating clergy while maintaining active worship and sacramental life. The congregation maintains local traditions and diaspora connections, with overseas partners supporting the adjacent school through regular donations.

## Notable Facts
The church-school campus hosts musical fundraisers for building maintenance. During Hurricane Melissa, Boscobel Primary served as a storm shelter and sustained damage, with the wider community experiencing prolonged outages. While specific church damage wasn't documented, the storm continued St. Matthew's historical role through climate challenges.
