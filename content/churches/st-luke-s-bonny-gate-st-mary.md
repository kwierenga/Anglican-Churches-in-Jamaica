# St. Luke's, Bonny Gate
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Luke’s Anglican Church serves Bonny Gate, a small community in the interior of St. Mary parish between Port Maria and Gayle, in the rolling hill country of the central parish. Bonny Gate is a quiet agricultural community, and St. Luke’s has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations. The church functions as a small mission chapel within the Port Maria deanery circuit.

## History
Bonny Gate sits in the interior hill country of St. Mary, in the agricultural landscape connecting the coastal capital at Port Maria with the interior market towns further south. The Anglican Diocese established St. Luke’s as part of its mission network across the post-Emancipation interior communities. The church has served successive generations of the Bonny Gate farming community through the transformations of the 19th and 20th centuries.

## Architecture
St. Luke’s is a small masonry chapel in the hill country of central St. Mary. Its inland position above the coast gives it some protection from direct surge, while the rolling terrain creates localised flooding and road-closure risk during heavy rainfall.

## Clergy
Bonny Gate has been served within the Port Maria circuit of the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church’s regular worship and pastoral life.

## Notable Facts
Bonny Gate’s interior position reflects the Anglican Diocese’s historically thorough coverage of St. Mary’s dispersed hill communities. The church’s pastoral role in connecting the farming families of the central parish interior to the wider Diocese has been consistent across its history. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to interior St. Mary; the Bonny Gate community was in the affected zone.
