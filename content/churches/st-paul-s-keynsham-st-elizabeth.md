# St. Paul's, Keynsham
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · Active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves Keynsham in St. Elizabeth, a community in the elevated farming landscape of the parish near the Manchester border. The diocesan directory records this church and its parent cure: it is administered within the parish's mission network served from one of the St. Elizabeth cure rectories. (Note: an earlier diocesan reference associated "St. Paul's, Keynsham" with the **Manchester** Deanery; the parish field of this row should be cross-checked against the current diocesan listing.)

The dedication to St. Paul — the apostle whose missionary journeys took the gospel from Jerusalem to Rome — is found on a number of Jamaican Anglican churches, particularly in cure churches and mission stations of the second half of the nineteenth century.

## History
Keynsham takes its name from Keynsham in Somerset, England — a transferred place name characteristic of Jamaica's colonial-era plantation landscape, where estate proprietors often named their properties after their English home towns. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's to serve this community as one of the network of mission stations the diocese created during Bishop Enos Nuttall's late-nineteenth-century rural expansion.

The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, which is consistent with a foundation date after that.

## Clergy
St. Paul's Keynsham has been served within the network of small St. Elizabeth/Manchester border missions under shared clergy arrangements typical of rural Anglican administration in Jamaica.
