# St. Stephen's Chapel, Lime Hall
**St. Ann** · Chapel · active

St. Saviour's Anglican Church serves the rural community of Lime Hall in the hills above St. Ann's Bay. Founded in the early 20th century, its establishment—with land purchased in 1903 and construction beginning in 1910 under the guidance of Archbishop Enos Nuttall—reflects the steady expansion of Anglicanism in the post-Emancipation period. The church occupies a strategically central position at a key road junction beyond Lime Hall Square, making it a simple but prominent village landmark. Today, it remains an active mission within the St. Ann's Bay pastoral cure under the Diocese of Jamaica's Montego Bay Region.

## History
The Anglican presence in the agricultural district of Lime Hall matured in the early 20th century. The community's development is marked by the acquisition of the church lot in 1903 and the commencement of building work in September 1910. A historical entry in the National Library of Jamaica commemorating the chapel's 61st anniversary preserves the local memory of its founding and subsequent community celebrations. St. Saviour's continues to be listed in contemporary diocesan and parish communications, maintaining its historic link to the wider St. Ann's Bay cure.

## Architecture
Based on surviving photographs and typical mission-church construction practices in St. Ann, St. Saviour's is a modest rural chapel characterized by masonry walls, a timber roof, and simple lancet windows, scaled appropriately for its village congregation. While no dedicated Jamaica National Heritage Trust monument card is published online, its ongoing function is confirmed by its noted location at the junction near the square and its appearance in current funeral listings. The building exemplifies the practical, vernacular ecclesiastical architecture of its era.

## Clergy
Within the current diocesan structure, St. Saviour's is pastorally linked to St. Ann's Bay Parish Church and falls under the Montego Bay Region. The clerical assignment for this cure covers St. Ann's Bay and its associated missions, including St. Saviour's in Lime Hall and nearby chapels such as St. Mark's in Chester. This arrangement of shared clergy and lay leadership across the cure is reflected in regular parish newsletters and deanery notices.

## Notable Facts
A modern Gleaner feature on Lime Hall's landmarks identifies St. Saviour's at the crucial turn-off beyond the square, cementing its role as an orienting point for the community. The church's 61st anniversary, recorded by the National Library of Jamaica, stands as a documented local milestone. Together, these sources illustrate a small but resilient Anglican presence that connects the area's plantation-era history, post-Emancipation settlement, and its place in the modern deanery system post-Independence.
