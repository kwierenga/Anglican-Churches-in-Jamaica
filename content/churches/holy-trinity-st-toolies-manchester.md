# Holy Trinity, St. Toolies
**Manchester** · Church · active

Holy Trinity Anglican Church serves St. Toolies, a community in the rural interior of Manchester parish. The church is administered as a mission of the St. Augustine's, Porus cure, alongside St. Andrew's Harmons, St. Stephen's Chantilly, and St. James' Toll Gate. The cure rectory is at Porus P.O., Manchester (cellular 349-2271).

## History
St. Toolies is a small farming district in the Porus hinterland of Manchester. The Porus area is itself notable in Jamaican history as a free village founded for ex-slaves following Emancipation in 1838: missionaries purchased tracts of land and divided them into parcels for sale to ex-slaves who came to grow coffee and other crops. The Anglican Diocese established the St. Augustine's Porus cure as the parish church for that free-village landscape, and extended its ministry into surrounding districts including St. Toolies through small mission stations like Holy Trinity.

The dedication to the Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — is one of the most traditional Anglican dedications, shared with the Diocese's Cathedral on North Street in Kingston (Holy Trinity Cathedral, built 1911). The choice for a rural mission station gives the small church a theologically central name.

## Architecture
Holy Trinity is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the Manchester interior. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge while remaining exposed to wind and rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Holy Trinity, St. Toolies is served as a mission of the St. Augustine's, Porus cure within the Mandeville Region's Manchester Deanery.
