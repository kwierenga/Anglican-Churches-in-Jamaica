# St. Matthew's, Yallahs
**St. Thomas** · Church · inactive

St. Matthew's Anglican Church provides a second Anglican worship point in the Yallahs area of St. Thomas, alongside St. David's also recorded in this community. Yallahs is one of the larger coastal communities in western St. Thomas at the mouth of the Yallahs River, and the presence of two Anglican entries in the area reflects either a geographically dispersed community served by separate chapels, or two worship points that served different parts of the Yallahs district.

## History
Yallahs's coastal position at the St. Thomas/St. Andrew boundary and its status as one of the larger communities on the south-east coast of Jamaica have historically supported a more substantial Anglican presence than smaller rural communities. St. Matthew's serves part of the Yallahs community, dedicated to the apostle and tax collector who became an evangelist.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Thomas. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the coastal landscape of St. Thomas. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
St. Matthew's Yallahs has been served within the St. Thomas deanery coast circuit, likely sharing clergy with St. David's in the same community.

## Notable Facts
The two Anglican churches in Yallahs reflect the Diocese's pattern of maintaining multiple worship points in larger coastal communities. Yallahs's position near the St. Thomas/St. Andrew boundary has given it a dual parish character in some administrative arrangements. Hurricane Ivan (2004) caused severe flooding across the Yallahs area. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain to the south coast of St. Thomas.
