# St. Michael's, Mavis Bank
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

St. Michael's Anglican Church in this second Mavis Bank entry represents either a distinct second Anglican presence in the Mavis Bank district, or a data recording reflecting the upper and lower parts of this extended Blue Mountain valley community. The Mavis Bank area sits in the upper Rio Cobre valley on the approach to the Blue Mountains in eastern St. Andrew, at significantly higher elevation than the city below. Note that St. Michael's Mission (entry [9]) is also recorded at Mavis Bank at the confirmed Google Places location.

## History
Mavis Bank is a community in the upper reaches of the St. Andrew interior, in the river valley that leads up into the Blue Mountains. The area is most famous for the JABLUM coffee factory, which processes Jamaica Blue Mountain coffee from the surrounding highland farms. The Anglican presence in Mavis Bank serves a community that has maintained its agricultural character despite its proximity to the city.

## Architecture
St. Michael's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Andrew in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Andrew. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The upper Mavis Bank area has been served within the eastern St. Andrew deanery circuit, shared with the mission church below.

## Notable Facts
The Mavis Bank valley community is known for its connection to Jamaica Blue Mountain coffee, produced on the surrounding mountainsides. The JABLUM factory at Mavis Bank has been processing and exporting Blue Mountain coffee for decades, making the community famous among coffee enthusiasts worldwide. The Anglican churches here serve a farming community with a proud agricultural tradition.
