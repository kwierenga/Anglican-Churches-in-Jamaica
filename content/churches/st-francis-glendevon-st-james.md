# St. Francis', Glendevon
**St. James** · Mission · active

St. Francis Mission is the Anglican church in Glendevon, a densely populated and often-challenged inner-city community above Montego Bay. Founded as an outreach mission of St. James Parish Church, it provides a critical anchor of stability, offering not only worship but also vital youth programs, a basic school, and community support. The mission holds international renown as the spiritual home of Rose Hudson-Wilkin, who grew up there before becoming a chaplain to the Queen and a bishop in the Church of England.

## History
St. Francis Mission was established by St. James Parish Church to serve the rapidly expanding hillside communities like Glendevon, which grew throughout the late 20th century as people moved from rural areas. Over time, Glendevon became known as one of St. James's most socially stressed areas, frequently cited in national reports on urban poverty and violence. Within this context, St. Francis was planted as a mission church; funeral notices from the 1980s confirm its early presence, documenting services at the church with burials at Pye River cemetery. This work continues the long Anglican story in Montego Bay, refocusing it on the needs of a modern urban settlement.

## Architecture
No formal architectural survey of St. Francis exists in public heritage records. However, community and media reports consistently place the church and its hall on Sun Valley Road, describing it as a venue for league presentations, trade training, and meetings—confirming its role as a multi-use community complex. Based on similar post-war urban missions in Jamaica, St. Francis is likely a modest, functional structure built from concrete block, with a low-pitched roof and an attached hall for its basic school and community programs, rather than a traditional, ornate church building.

## Clergy
St. Francis is listed under the Montego Bay Region as a mission of St. James Parish Church, with the rector, such as the Very Rev. Justin Nembhard, serving as the priest in charge. The mission has a strong tradition of lay and ordained leadership, most notably Rose Hudson-Wilkin, who often credits her upbringing and early ministry at St. Francis for nurturing her vocation. The church's life extends beyond Sunday worship; it hosts funeral services for local residents and operates the St. Francis Basic School on its premises, underlining its deep commitment to early childhood education and family support.

## Notable Facts
A Hub for Community Uplift: The church is directly involved in socio-economic development. A 2011 Gleaner report quoted its priest championing training programs for young men and mothers, and supporting childcare to enable adult education, demonstrating a commitment to holistic community transformation. The Cradle of a Bishop: St. Francis is internationally recognized as the childhood church of The Rt. Rev. Rose Hudson-Wilkin. Her journey from Sunday school at the mission to serving as a Queen’s Chaplain and participating in the coronation of King Charles III highlights the global impact of this local congregation. Stability in a Challenged Environment: While social surveys note Glendevon's struggles with violence and crime, they also acknowledge the presence of strong institutions like St. Francis. The church, with its hall and school, provides a crucial pillar of stability and hope for families amidst these significant pressures.
