# St. Mary's Church, Kellits
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. Mary's Anglican Church serves Kellits, a community in the high northern interior of Clarendon parish near the Manchester and St. Elizabeth boundaries. Kellits is a farming community in elevated terrain, and the church has served this remote hill community for several generations.

## History
Kellits sits in the high interior of Clarendon, in the hill country of the north-western part of the parish approaching the Manchester border. The Anglican Diocese established St. Mary's to provide worship for this elevated farming community. The church has served Kellits through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Mary's Church is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Clarendon. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Kellits has been served within the Clarendon deanery under shared clergy arrangements. The remote elevated location means the church depends significantly on lay leaders.

## Notable Facts
Kellits is in the high country of northern Clarendon, in a landscape of small farms and hill communities that developed after Emancipation. The area's elevation and remoteness have preserved a distinct community character.
