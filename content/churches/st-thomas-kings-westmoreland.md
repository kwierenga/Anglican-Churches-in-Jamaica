# St. Thomas', Kings
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Thomas' Anglican Church at Kings is a rural mission church in the Westmoreland Deanery of the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands. No substantive historical information about the church's foundation, architecture, or clergy has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed. The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica's Anglican returns — indicating that St. Thomas' Kings was established after 1891, most likely during Bishop Enos Nuttall's great expansion of rural mission stations (the Diocese grew from 26 stations in 1880 to 122 by 1911).

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Across the Diocese, some 50 church buildings were destroyed or significantly damaged. The specific status of St. Thomas' Kings after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
