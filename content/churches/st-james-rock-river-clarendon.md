# St. James', Rock River
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. James' Anglican Church provides a second Anglican worship point in the Rock River area of Clarendon, alongside St. Stephen's also recorded at Rock River. The two Rock River entries are approximately 14 km apart, suggesting either a very large district sharing the Rock River name or a coordinate error in one entry. St. James' serves its part of the Rock River community.

## History
The Rock River district of Clarendon supports two Anglican entries. St. James' serves its particular community in the interior farming landscape of the parish.

## Architecture
St. James' is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Clarendon in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. James' Rock River is administered as a mission of the St. Paul's Chapelton cure (Clarendon Deanery), grouped with St. Mark's Beckford Kraal, All Saints' Mt. Providence, and St. Luke's Wood Hall. The cure rectory is at Chapelton P.O., Clarendon.

## Notable Facts
The significant distance between the two Rock River entries in the dataset suggests that the Rock River name may apply to a broad valley or watershed district rather than a single compact community, with multiple settlements along the river's course each maintaining their own Anglican worship point.
