# St. Augustine's, Watermount
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Augustine's Anglican Church serves Watermount, a community in the interior of St. Catherine parish. Watermount is a small farming district, and the church has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The dedication to St. Augustine of Hippo — the great 4th-century theologian whose writings shaped Western Christianity — is found in only a small number of Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Watermount sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country between the main road corridor and the western hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Augustine's to serve this community. The church has served the Watermount farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Augustine's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the hilly interior of St. Catherine. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Watermount has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Augustine of Hippo's Confessions and City of God are among the most read theological texts in Christian history. The dedication to him in a small Jamaican interior chapel connects this farming community to one of the most intellectually influential figures in the history of Western Christianity. Watermount's name may derive from a local spring or stream feature that gave the community a reliable water source.
