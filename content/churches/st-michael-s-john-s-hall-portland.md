# St. Michael's, John's Hall
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Michael’s Anglican Church serves the small hillside community of John’s Hall in eastern Portland, situated just inland from Fellowship and Port Antonio. The Diocese of Jamaica & the Cayman Islands officially lists 'St. Michael's - John's Hall' among Portland churches within the Eastern Jamaica Region, confirming its status as an active mission. Multiple gazetteer and mapping sources identify John's Hall as a distinct settlement near Bellevue and Garland Grove, differentiating it from several other Jamaican localities sharing the same name. These references collectively establish the identity and context of this rural Anglican mission within Portland's network of interior chapels.

## History
Within the diocesan organizational structure, St. Michael's at John's Hall appears in the 'Churches by Deanery' tables under Portland's Eastern Jamaica Region grouping, alongside other small interior missions such as St. John's at Cooper's Hill and St. Jude's at Bourbon. This placement reflects Portland's long-established pattern of maintaining rural chapels that extend Anglican ministry into the parish's interior communities while remaining connected to the regional deanery centered around Port Antonio. The church represents the historical expansion of Anglican presence into Portland's eastern valleys during the post-emancipation period.

## Clergy
St. Michael's operates under the administrative oversight of the Eastern Jamaica Region's Portland deanery, as confirmed by its listing in diocesan 'churches by parish' directories. The church's designation as 'C/E' (Church of England) on regional indices identifies it among Portland's outstation missions. All clerical assignments, worship schedules, and pastoral coordination for this chapel are managed through the Eastern Jamaica Region's administrative channels, maintaining its connection to the broader diocesan network while serving its specific community.

## Notable Facts
The church's location requires careful geographical distinction, as several Jamaican settlements share the name John's Hall, though map sources precisely place this community in Portland near Fellowship and Bellevue. This distinction is particularly important for accurate mapping and visitor orientation. The chapel, like others in Portland's interior, faced operational challenges during Hurricane Melissa (October 2025), when the parish's eastern regions experienced significant landslides, road closures, and utility disruptions.
