# St. Peter's, Galina
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Peter's Anglican Church serves Galina, a coastal community on the north coast of St. Mary parish near Port Maria.

## History
Galina lies on the north coast of St. Mary, near Galina Point — a navigational landmark that gave the community its name. The St. Mary coastline was an early-settled part of British colonial Jamaica, and the parish has a long Anglican tradition anchored at the parish church of St. Mary's at Port Maria (the original parish church dates from the eighteenth century, with the current building bearing later modifications) and at the historic Holy Trinity Anglican Church in Retreat — a Georgian building of 1828 erected on land donated by Henry Rigg, recognised by the Jamaica National Heritage Trust as one of the parish's important heritage churches.

The Anglican Diocese established St. Peter's as a mission chapel to serve the Galina community as part of its broader north-coast and rural mission network. The dedication to St. Peter the apostle — chief of the twelve, traditionally identified as the first bishop of Rome, with a feast day on 29 June (shared with St. Paul) — is one of the most common Anglican dedications across the diocese.

No substantive foundational date or detailed architectural history specifically for St. Peter's Galina has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed.

## Architecture
St. Peter's is a small coastal masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations along the St. Mary north coast. The coastal position creates direct vulnerability to storm surge and salt-air corrosion during major hurricanes.

## Clergy
St. Peter's Galina is served within the St. Mary deanery of the diocesan structure.
