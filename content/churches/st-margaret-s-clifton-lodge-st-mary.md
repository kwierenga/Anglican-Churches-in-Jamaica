# St. Margaret's, Clifton Lodge
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Margaret’s Anglican Church at Clifton Lodge serves a community in the interior of St. Mary parish, one of two churches with this dedication in the parish. Clifton Lodge is a small agricultural community in the hill country of St. Mary, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations. This entry’s content was written to match the format of the surrounding parish entries.

## History
Clifton Lodge developed as a small interior community of St. Mary, its lodge-associated name reflecting the colonial estate landscape of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Margaret’s at Clifton Lodge as part of its mission network, serving the post-Emancipation farming communities of this part of the parish interior. The church has served the Clifton Lodge community across the generations since its foundation.

## Architecture
St. Margaret’s Clifton Lodge is a small masonry chapel in the interior hills of St. Mary. The building sits in the hilly terrain characteristic of this part of the parish, subject to heavy rainfall and the landslide and road-closure risks that accompany major storm events in St. Mary’s interior.

## Clergy
Clifton Lodge has been served within the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church’s regular pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
The two St. Margaret’s churches in St. Mary — at Long Road and Clifton Lodge — reflect the Diocese’s use of repeated dedications across a parish network. Both serve small interior farming communities in different parts of the parish hill country. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to interior St. Mary; the Clifton Lodge community and congregation were in the affected zone.
