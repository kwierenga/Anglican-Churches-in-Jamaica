# St. Peter's, Lluidas Vale
**St. Catherine** · Church · inactive

St. Mary's Anglican Church serves Lluidas Vale, a community in the interior of St. Catherine parish in the hill country of the western part of the parish near the border with Clarendon and Manchester. Lluidas Vale is a notable agricultural community in a high-altitude valley surrounded by the hills of central Jamaica, known for its sugar production and cool climate.

## History
Lluidas Vale is one of the higher-elevation communities in the St. Catherine interior, in a valley setting that has supported sugar cultivation and cattle farming since the colonial period. The Lluidas Vale area includes the Worthy Park Estate, one of the oldest continuously operating sugar estates in Jamaica, established in the 17th century. The Anglican Diocese established St. Mary's to serve the Lluidas Vale community.

## Architecture
St. Mary's Lluidas Vale is a masonry chapel in the interior of St. Catherine The high valley position gives it some of the coolest conditions of any St. Catherine Anglican church, with elevation creating both pleasant climate and significant wind exposure during hurricane passages.

## Clergy
Lluidas Vale has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Worthy Park Estate in Lluidas Vale is one of the oldest continuously operating sugar estates in the Caribbean, with a documented history stretching back to the 17th century. The estate has survived changes of ownership, the abolition of slavery, the decline of the sugar industry, and the modernisation of Caribbean agriculture to remain a producing sugar and rum operation. The Anglican church at Lluidas Vale has served the estate community through this entire history.
