# St. Helena's, Sheffield
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Helena's Anglican Church serves the Sheffield District of Westmoreland, an interior area near Darliston and the Westmoreland Hopewell. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory under the Westmoreland Deanery. No substantive foundational or architectural history has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed, and the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica — indicating a post-1891 foundation, most likely during Bishop Enos Nuttall's rural expansion of mission stations.

The dedication is to St. Helena (c. 246/248 – c. 330 AD), mother of the Emperor Constantine and traditionally credited with the discovery of the True Cross. Her feast day is 18 August (in the Anglican tradition).

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. The Sheffield District, in interior Westmoreland, experienced destructive winds. The specific status of St. Helena's after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
