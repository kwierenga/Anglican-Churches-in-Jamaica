# All Saints', Mt. Providence
**Clarendon** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church serves Mount Providence, a community in the upland farming country of central Clarendon north of the parish capital. The church is administered as a mission of the St. Paul's, Chapelton cure, alongside St. Mark's Beckford Kraal, St. James' Rock River, and St. Luke's Wood Hall. The cure rectory is at Chapelton P.O., Clarendon (telephone 785-0782).

## History
Mount Providence lies in the hill country between Chapelton and the southern parish boundary. The "Providence" element of the name belongs to a wider Jamaican post-Emancipation tradition of giving aspirational, scripturally resonant names to new free settlements — names like Providence, New Hope, Refuge, and Mount Pleasant express the hope of divine care that defined the founding generation of free villages after 1838. The Anglican Diocese established All Saints' as a small mission station of the St. Paul's Chapelton cure to serve this community within the diocese's late-nineteenth-century rural church-planting expansion.

The dedication to All Saints — commemorating the full company of recognised and unrecognised saints on 1 November — is the most general dedication in the Anglican tradition and is a common choice for mission chapels intended to gather a scattered farming community without claim to a single saintly patron.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the Clarendon interior. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge while remaining exposed to the wind and heavy rainfall that pass over central Clarendon during major hurricanes.

## Clergy
All Saints' Mt. Providence is served as a mission of the St. Paul's, Chapelton cure within the Mandeville Region's Clarendon Deanery.
