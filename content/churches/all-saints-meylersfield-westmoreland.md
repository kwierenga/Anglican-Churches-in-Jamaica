# All Saints', Meylersfield
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church serves the community at Meylersfield in the Westmoreland plain, a site with deep roots in the parish's plantation past. Meylersfield was one of the larger Westmoreland sugar estates: in 1823 it held 506 enslaved Africans, placing it among the most populous properties on the plain. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory under the Westmoreland Deanery; its specific foundation date has not been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed, and it does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica — indicating a post-1891 foundation.

## History
Westmoreland was one of Jamaica's largest sugar parishes, its economy built on the enslaved labour of properties like Meylersfield. Until 1870 the Church of England in Jamaica was the established state church. After full freedom on 1 August 1838, freed people settled across the plain, and the Anglican Church established mission stations across the interior in the post-disestablishment decades. The dedication to All Saints — the feast of all the faithful departed, celebrated on 1 November — is a common Anglican choice and is sometimes taken as a church-name response to the collective memory of the enslaved ancestors whose labour built the estate landscape that surrounded the building.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. The Westmoreland plain, including Meylersfield, was in the direct impact zone. The specific status of All Saints' after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
