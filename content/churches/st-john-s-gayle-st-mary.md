# St. John's, Gayle
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. John's Anglican Church in Gayle serves as a daughter church of Holy Trinity Retreat, established post-Emancipation to serve St. Mary's inland farming communities. It continues to function as an active worship center and burial ground for surrounding districts. During Hurricane Melissa (2025), Gayle experienced significant storm impact with urgent appeals for assistance and official relief visits highlighting the area's vulnerability.

## History
Established as part of Holy Trinity Retreat's expansion, St. John's represents the spread of Anglicanism into St. Mary's interior following Emancipation. It served communities transitioning from plantation labor to small-scale farming. Despite infrastructure challenges documented in recent years, including road damage from heavy rains, the church has maintained its role as spiritual center for Gayle, Labyrinth, and Lucky Hill residents.
