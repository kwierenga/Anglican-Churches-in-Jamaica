# St. Agnes', The Grove
**St. Andrew** · Church · unknown

St. Agnes' Anglican Church serves The Grove, a community in St. Andrew in the residential area of eastern Half-Way-Tree. The Grove is a neighbourhood in the middle-class residential zone between Half-Way-Tree and Liguanea, and St. Agnes' provides a local Anglican worship point in this eastern St. Andrew community. The dedication to St. Agnes, the young Roman martyr associated with purity and courage, is found in several Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
The Grove is a residential community in eastern St. Andrew, part of the upmarket suburban zone that developed in the 20th century along the Hope Road corridor. The Anglican Diocese established St. Agnes' to serve this community, providing a local place of worship for the residential neighbourhood.

## Architecture
St. Agnes' is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Andrew. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Andrew. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The Grove has been served within the eastern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
St. Agnes' feast day (21 January) is one of the early winter feasts of the Anglican calendar, associated with the tradition of blessing lambs on her feast day in some European communities. The dedication to Agnes is found in churches across Jamaica, reflecting the breadth of saints' names drawn upon by the Anglican Diocese in naming its churches.
