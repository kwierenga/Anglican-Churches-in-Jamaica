# St. Paul's, Little London
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves Little London, a small coastal community on the north-western shore of Westmoreland, close to the Cabarita River and the boundary with Hanover. The settlement grew as a modest port and fishing community in the shadow of the larger sugar economy and has a distinctive character shaped by its estuary location. St. Paul's has been the Anglican presence in Little London for well over a century, providing worship, pastoral care, and a community gathering point for the town and surrounding districts.

## History
Little London's name is a colonial-era designation, likely reflecting its role as a minor commercial centre during the plantation period. The surrounding area was dominated by sugar estates, and the post-Emancipation era saw many freed families settle in the town and along the coast. The Anglican Church established a presence here as part of its wider effort to serve Westmoreland's dispersed population following 1838. St. Paul's developed as a chapel-of-ease and later as an independent church within the Westmoreland deanery. The town's coastal and estuarine setting has made it vulnerable to both flooding and hurricane damage throughout its history.

## Architecture
St. Paul's is a modest masonry church typical of the smaller Anglican buildings erected in Westmoreland's coastal communities during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Its construction reflects both local materials and the practical need for storm resilience in a low-lying, exposed coastal location. The church is set within the compact townscape of Little London, with the sea and the Cabarita estuary close by. The surrounding churchyard serves as the local burial ground for families of the town and nearby districts.

## Clergy
St. Paul's has been served within the Westmoreland Anglican circuit, sharing clergy with St. George's in Savanna-la-Mar and other churches in the north-western end of the parish. As a smaller church, its day-to-day life has depended heavily on lay leaders — wardens, Mothers' Union members, and lay readers — who maintain worship and community activities between clerical visits. The church remains an active part of the Montego Bay Region's ministry network.

## Notable Facts
Little London's estuarine location on the Cabarita River makes it particularly vulnerable to flooding during heavy rain events, and the town has been affected by most major storms to strike Westmoreland. Hurricane Charlie (1951) caused widespread damage along this coast. Hurricane Ivan (2004) brought serious storm surge to the north-west coast. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) made landfall in Westmoreland and the surrounding area sustained significant damage; Little London, lying close to the landfall zone, was among the communities affected, and St. Paul's congregation was directly in the path of the storm.
