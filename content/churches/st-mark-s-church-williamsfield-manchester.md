# St. Mark's Church, Williamsfield
**Manchester** · Church · active

St. Mark's Anglican Church serves Williamsfield, a community in the southern part of Manchester parish near the border with Clarendon. Williamsfield is associated with the Williamsfield bauxite operations and the industrial landscape of the Manchester-Clarendon border area. The church has served the Williamsfield community for several generations.

## History
Williamsfield sits in the southern part of Manchester, near the flat lands approaching the Clarendon border. The area has been shaped by both agricultural and industrial development, including the bauxite industry that has transformed parts of the Manchester landscape. The Anglican Diocese established St. Mark's to serve this community.

## Architecture
St. Mark's Church is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Manchester. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of Manchester. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Williamsfield has been served within the Manchester deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
The Manchester landscape has been significantly shaped by bauxite mining, the red-soil ore that Jamaica exports for alumina and aluminium production. Manchester is one of the key bauxite parishes, and the industry's presence in the landscape around Williamsfield has defined the economic character of this part of the parish for several decades.
