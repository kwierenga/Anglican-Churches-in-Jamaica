# All Saints', Fellowship
**Portland** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church serves the community of Fellowship in Portland parish, a small farming district in the interior of the parish. The dedication to All Saints — commemorating the full company of saints on 1 November — is one of the traditional Anglican dedications found across Jamaica. Fellowship is a community in the southern interior of Portland, and the church has served the local families here for several generations.

## History
Fellowship sits in the interior of Portland, south of Port Antonio in the hill country approaching the Blue Mountains. The Anglican Diocese established All Saints' as a mission chapel to serve this interior community. The community name 'Fellowship' reflects a Jamaican naming tradition of aspirational or devotional community names, particularly common in post-Emancipation settlements.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a small masonry chapel in the southern interior of Portland, in the high-rainfall hill country near the Blue Mountains. The building sits in a landscape of extraordinary rainfall and steep terrain, with the associated landslide and flooding risk that characterises Portland's interior valleys.

## Clergy
Fellowship has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. The interior location means the church depends significantly on lay leaders for its regular pastoral life.

## Notable Facts
Community names like Fellowship are found throughout Jamaica's interior, particularly in communities founded by freed people after Emancipation who expressed their aspirations for their new communities in the names they chose. Portland's interior communities south of Port Antonio are in the approach country to the Blue Mountains wilderness. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought serious rain impacts to this part of Portland.
