# St. Barnabas', Crescent
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Barnabas' Anglican Church serves Crescent, a community in St. Catherine parish in the hill country of the interior. Crescent is a small farming district, and the church has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The dedication to St. Barnabas — the 'son of encouragement' who supported Paul's early missionary journeys — is found in several Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Crescent sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country north of Spanish Town. The Anglican Diocese established St. Barnabas' to serve this community. The church has served the Crescent farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Barnabas' is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. Barnabas' Crescent is administered as a mission of the Cathedral of St. Jago de la Vega cure (Spanish Town); the cure rectory at Red Church Street, Spanish Town P.O. (telephone 984-2535), is the contact address.

## Notable Facts
Barnabas' feast day (11 June) falls in the mid-year, and his association with encouragement and practical generosity has made him a popular patron for community mission churches across Jamaica. St. Barnabas is one of the more frequently occurring church dedications in the island's Anglican network.
