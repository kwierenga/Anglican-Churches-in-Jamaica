# St. Christopher's, Stony Hill / St Christopher's
**St. Andrew** · Church · unknown

St. Christopher’s Anglican Church at Stony Hill (distinct from St. Christopher’s at Cavaliers) provides a second Anglican presence in the Stony Hill area of northern St. Andrew. The Stony Hill community is substantial enough to support multiple Anglican worship points, and this St. Christopher’s serves a part of the hill-town community alongside St. Jude’s, which is the principal confirmed Anglican church at Stony Hill.

## History
Stony Hill is one of the more substantial communities on the road into the Blue Mountains from Kingston, and its population is sufficient to support multiple Anglican churches. St. Christopher’s represents a second worship point in the community, providing local access to Anglican ministry for a part of the Stony Hill area.

## Architecture
St. Christopher's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Andrew. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the hilly interior of St. Andrew. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
This Stony Hill church has been served within the northern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements with St. Jude’s.

## Notable Facts
The presence of two St. Christopher’s churches in northern St. Andrew — one at Cavaliers and one at Stony Hill — reflects the Diocese’s dense coverage of the hill-country communities north of Kingston. Both communities look to the patron of travellers, apt for congregations whose daily lives involve navigating winding mountain roads.
