# St. James', Guanoboa Vale
**St. Catherine** · Unknown · 

St. James' Anglican Church provides a second worship point in the Guanoboa Vale (also spelled Guanaboa Vale) area of St. Catherine, alongside St. John's OPC also recorded in this district. The two entries are approximately 4 km apart, consistent with a dispersed valley community served by multiple chapels. St. James' serves its part of the Guanoboa Vale community.

## History
The Guanoboa/Guanaboa Vale district of St. Catherine supports two Anglican worship points, reflecting a dispersed community across the valley. St. James' serves its part of this farming community, dedicated to James the Apostle, patron of pilgrims.

## Architecture
St. James' is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Catherine. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the hilly interior of St. Catherine. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Guanoboa Vale has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements, with the two valley churches likely sharing circuit clergy.

## Notable Facts
The alternate spellings Guanoboa and Guanaboa Vale in the dataset reflect the inconsistent recording of this place name across different historical sources. This kind of spelling variation is common in Jamaican place names, particularly for interior communities whose names have Arawak or Spanish origins that were anglicised over time.
