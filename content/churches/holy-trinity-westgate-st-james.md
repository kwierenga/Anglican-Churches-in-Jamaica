# Holy Trinity, Westgate
**St. James** · Church · active

Holy Trinity Anglican Church at Westgate serves a community in the western urban fringe of Montego Bay, in the district between the city centre and the expanding western suburbs. Westgate — named for its position at the western approach to the town — developed as a mixed residential and commercial area as Montego Bay grew through the 20th century. Holy Trinity has provided Anglican ministry to this community, serving a congregation drawn from the established and newer residential areas of the western city. As a Trinitarian dedication, it is one of several Holy Trinity churches in the Diocese’s western region.

## History
The Westgate area of Montego Bay grew as the town expanded westward beyond its colonial core, and the Anglican Diocese responded by planting mission churches in the new residential districts. Holy Trinity was established to serve the Westgate community, providing Anglican worship and pastoral care to families who found themselves beyond the immediate catchment of St. James Parish Church in the town centre. The church’s history mirrors the rapid growth of Montego Bay as Jamaica’s second city through the 20th century, its congregation shaped by the social and economic forces that drove urban expansion along the north-west coast.

## Architecture
Holy Trinity is a functional urban church building in the Westgate district of Montego Bay. Its location on the western approach to the city gives it a prominent position in the urban streetscape. The building serves both as a place of worship and as a community facility, with the church and its associated spaces used for a range of pastoral and community activities consistent with Anglican urban ministry. The coastal proximity of the greater Montego Bay area means all churches in the city’s western corridor share exposure to hurricane conditions.

## Clergy
Holy Trinity Westgate has been served within the Montego Bay Region clergy cluster, grouped with other western Montego Bay congregations. The church’s urban setting means it draws on both a regular resident congregation and families connected to the wider western city. Lay leaders and wardens have been central to the church’s community life, sustaining worship and ministry alongside visiting or shared clergy. The church is confirmed active in diocesan records.

## Notable Facts
Westgate’s position on the western approach to Montego Bay places it at the interface between the city’s urban core and the communities stretching westward toward Hanover. The north-west coast of Jamaica, of which Montego Bay is the focal point, has experienced multiple major hurricane impacts. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) struck the north-west coast with extreme severity, causing extensive damage across Montego Bay and the surrounding area. Hurricane Ivan (2004) again affected the region. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) made direct landfall in western Jamaica; the western corridor of Montego Bay, including Westgate, experienced damaging winds and coastal conditions, and Holy Trinity’s congregation was in the storm’s impact zone.
