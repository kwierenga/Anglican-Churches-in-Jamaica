# St. James', Birnamwood
**Portland** · Church · active

St. James' Anglican Church at Birnamwood serves a community in the western interior of Portland parish, near the boundary with St. Mary. Birnamwood (also spelled Birnam Wood in some sources) is a small farming community in the hill country of western Portland, and the church has served this interior community for several generations. St. James' at Birnamwood was specifically named in Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) coverage, with a landslide reported near the church during the storm.

## History
Birnamwood sits in the western interior of Portland, in the hill country that transitions towards the St. Mary border. The community's name echoes the Shakespearean forest of Birnam Wood — one of many literary and English landscape names transplanted to Jamaican estate communities during the colonial period. The Anglican Diocese established St. James' as a mission chapel to serve this interior farming community, and the church has been the centre of religious life in Birnamwood for well over a century.

## Architecture
St. James' is a masonry chapel in the western interior hills of Portland. The building is set in hilly terrain that is particularly susceptible to landslides during heavy rainfall — a fact confirmed by the landslide reported near the church during Hurricane Melissa. Portland's interior receives some of the highest rainfall in Jamaica, and the steep slopes of western Portland amplify this risk.

## Clergy
Birnamwood has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements, connected to the Buff Bay circuit given its western Portland location. Lay leaders sustain the church's regular pastoral life.

## Notable Facts
St. James' Birnamwood gained specific mention in Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) coverage when a landslide was reported in the immediate vicinity of the church during the storm. This direct documentation makes it one of the better-evidenced storm-impact records for a Portland Anglican church in the 2025 season. The incident also illustrates the particular landslide risk for hill-country churches in western Portland during major hurricane passages.
