# Holy Trinity, Old Harbour
**St. Catherine** · Church · inactive

Holy Trinity Anglican Church provides a second Anglican worship point in the Old Harbour area of St. Catherine, alongside St. Dorothy's which is the historic principal Anglican church of Old Harbour. Confirmed at 2 Darlington Drive, Old Harbour, the church is described by reviewers as warm and welcoming with a feeling of family. Holy Trinity serves its part of the Old Harbour community.

## History
Old Harbour is one of the larger coastal communities on the south coast of St. Catherine, with sufficient population to support multiple Anglican worship points. Holy Trinity was established as a second Anglican church in the town, providing additional ministry for a different part of the community.

## Architecture
Holy Trinity is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Catherine. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the coastal landscape of St. Catherine. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
Holy Trinity Old Harbour has been served within the St. Catherine deanery, sharing the Old Harbour circuit with St. Dorothy's.

## Notable Facts
Old Harbour's dual Anglican churches — the historic St. Dorothy's and the more recently established Holy Trinity — reflect the town's growth and the Diocese's layered approach to urban ministry. The two churches serve different parts of the Old Harbour community and together provide comprehensive Anglican coverage of one of the south coast's larger towns.
