# St. Bartholomew's, Frankfield
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. Bartholomew's Anglican Church serves Frankfield, Frankfield is the main town of the interior highlands of Clarendon, and St. Bartholomew's provides a second Anglican presence alongside St. John's also recorded here. The church serves the Frankfield community in the upper Rio Minho valley.

## History
Frankfield developed as the principal town of the interior highlands of Clarendon, and the Anglican Diocese established St. Bartholomew's alongside St. John's to serve this substantial community. The upper Rio Minho valley around Frankfield has been a farming area for the surrounding hill communities for well over a century.

## Architecture
St. Bartholomew's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Clarendon in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. Bartholomew's Frankfield is the cure church for a circuit covering Frankfield, St. Luke's Sanguinetti, St. George's Red Hills, St. Matthew's Aenon Town, Holy Trinity Park Hall, and Peckham — the diocese rectory address is The Rectory, Frankfield P.O., Clarendon (telephone 904-4565). The cure has recently been listed as **VACANT**.

## Notable Facts
St. Bartholomew's feast day (24 August) falls in the height of hurricane season. Frankfield's position in the upper Rio Minho valley makes it central to the agricultural economy of interior Clarendon, and the town has historically served as the market hub for the surrounding hill communities.
