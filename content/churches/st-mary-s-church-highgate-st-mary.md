# St. Mary's Church, Highgate
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Mary’s Anglican Church serves Highgate, one of the larger interior market towns of St. Mary parish, situated in the hill country south of Annotto Bay and Port Maria. Highgate developed as an agricultural service town for the coffee and later banana-growing districts of interior St. Mary, and the church has been part of its community life for well over a century. The dedication to St. Mary gives the church the same name as the parish it serves, a distinction reflecting the importance of this particular Anglican presence within the Highgate community.

## History
Highgate grew as a market and service centre for the interior agricultural districts of St. Mary, an area whose hills and valleys were extensively cultivated for coffee and, later, bananas during the 19th and early 20th centuries. The Anglican Diocese established a church at Highgate to serve the community of farmers, traders, and their families who made the town their commercial and social centre. St. Mary’s has served successive generations of Highgate’s population through the economic transformations of the parish interior, from the plantation era through the banana trade to the smallholder farming economy of the present day.

## Architecture
St. Mary’s is a masonry church in the interior hill country of St. Mary, set in the Highgate community. The church building is consistent with Anglican hill-country construction in Jamaica — durable masonry, suited to a community that lies inland and at elevation, away from direct coastal storm surge but exposed to the heavy rainfall and wind that the Blue Mountain ridge system channels through interior St. Mary. The town of Highgate is at moderate elevation, giving it some natural protection from coastal hazards while creating its own risks from landslide and road disruption during major storms.

## Clergy
St. Mary’s Highgate has been served within the St. Mary deanery of the Anglican Diocese, grouped with other interior and coastal St. Mary congregations under shared clergy arrangements. As a market town, Highgate has maintained a more substantial congregation than many rural mission chapels, supporting a regular cycle of worship and community events. Lay leaders — wardens, Mothers’ Union members, and readers — have been central to the church’s life.

## Notable Facts
Highgate is one of St. Mary’s more significant interior towns, serving as a hub for communities across the central and southern parts of the parish. The town’s interior position has made it a gathering point for agricultural workers and smallholders from across a wide district. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious damage to interior St. Mary, with landslides affecting the hill roads that connect Highgate to the rest of the parish. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked across northern Jamaica; interior St. Mary experienced heavy rain and wind, and the Highgate community and its congregation were affected.
