# St. John the Baptist's, Coleyville
**Manchester** · Church · active

St. John the Baptist's Anglican Church serves Coleyville, a community in the interior of Manchester parish. The dedication to John the Baptist — the forerunner of Christ who baptised Jesus in the Jordan — is one of the most significant in the Anglican calendar, and one of only a small number of 'John the Baptist' dedications in Jamaica's Anglican network, distinct from the many churches simply dedicated to 'St. John.'

## History
Coleyville sits in the interior of Manchester, in the farming landscape of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. John the Baptist's to serve this community. The church has served the Coleyville farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. John the Baptist's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Manchester in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Manchester. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Coleyville has been served within the Manchester deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
The feast of the Nativity of St. John the Baptist (24 June) is one of the oldest feasts in the Christian calendar, predating many apostolic feast days. It falls at the summer solstice — the longest day — which in the northern hemisphere has been a day of particular religious and cultural significance since ancient times. In Jamaica, 24 June falls in the early part of hurricane season. The dedication specifically to 'John the Baptist' rather than simply 'St. John' gives this church a more precise theological identity.
