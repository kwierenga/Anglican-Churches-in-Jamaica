# St. George's Church, 83 East Street
**Kingston** · Parish Church · active

St. George's Anglican Church at 83 East Street is a historic parish in the heart of downtown Kingston, known as the 'Oasis in Kingston' for its community outreach in a dense urban area. Originally established in 1830 as a Chapel-of-Ease to Kingston Parish Church, it became an independent parish and has served the surrounding community for nearly two centuries. The current Georgian building, rebuilt after the 1907 earthquake and consecrated in 1911, remains an active centre of worship, education, and social support.

## History
St. George's was formally consecrated on August 12, 1830, originally as a Chapel-of-Ease to accommodate the overflow congregation from Kingston Parish Church. It gradually developed into its own independent parish serving the residents of lower Kingston. The 1907 earthquake caused tremendous damage to the building, which was subsequently abandoned and then rebuilt. The present structure was consecrated on January 18, 1911. The church suffered significant damage again during Hurricane Charlie in 1951 but was repaired. Over its nearly 200-year history it has evolved from a colonial overflow chapel into a front-line community church in one of Kingston's most challenged inner-city districts.

## Architecture
The current St. George's is a Georgian-style building constructed after the 1907 earthquake, consecrated in 1911. It is built on a modest but dignified scale suited to its downtown location. Stained glass features in its design. The property forms a small campus in the urban streetscape of East Street, providing space not only for worship but for the church's extensive outreach programmes. Its solid masonry construction has allowed it to withstand the repeated storms and seismic events that have affected Kingston over the past century.

## Clergy
St. George's has been led by Canon Densil Barnes in recent years, under whose leadership the church has expanded its community outreach identity as the 'Oasis in Kingston.' The parish provides back-to-school supplies, a homework-assistance programme, and various other ministries to local families. It falls under the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands. The church's mission, as articulated by its leadership, combines liturgical worship with active service to the surrounding neighbourhood, reflecting the Anglican Diocese's broader commitment to social engagement.

## Notable Facts
St. George's 189th anniversary in 2019 highlighted its remarkable longevity as a downtown Kingston institution. The church's history spans the full arc of modern Jamaican life — from the era of slavery through emancipation, the 1907 earthquake, Independence, and into the 21st century. Its survival of the 1907 earthquake (requiring a full rebuild) and Hurricane Charlie (1951) speaks to the congregation's resilience. During Hurricane Melissa (October 2025), Kingston was spared the worst of the storm, which made direct landfall in western Jamaica; no major structural damage to St. George's has been reported.
