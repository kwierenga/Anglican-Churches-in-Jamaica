# Christ Church, Vineyard Town
**Kingston** · Church · inactive

Christ Church, Vineyard Town is one of the Anglican churches of Kingston, serving the Vineyard Town community in eastern Kingston. Located at 6 Antrim Road, Kingston 3, it is one of the working-class residential communities of the capital. The church has been served by clergy including Sean Major-Campbell and forms part of the Kingston deanery.

## History
Vineyard Town is an inner-city community of eastern Kingston. The name preserves the memory of market gardening that once characterised the flat land east of the city centre. Christ Church has served this community through the development of Kingston's eastern residential areas.

## Architecture
Christ Church is a masonry church in the urban landscape of eastern Kingston, serving the Vineyard Town community at 6 Antrim Road, Kingston 3.

## Clergy
The cure has been served by Sean Major-Campbell within the Kingston deanery.

## Notable Facts
Vineyard Town is one of several inner-city Kingston communities with a strong Anglican presence. The area has been the subject of community development work and the church plays a role in the social fabric of one of Kingston's more established working-class residential districts.
