# St. Thomas-ye-Vale, Bog Walk
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Thomas-ye-Vale Anglican Church serves Bog Walk, a community in the Rio Cobre gorge on the main road between Spanish Town and the north coast of Jamaica. Confirmed at its Bog Walk Church Road location, the church is described by reviewers as a warm and welcoming congregation with a fantastic family feel. Bog Walk is a community defined by the spectacular Rio Cobre gorge that the main road passes through — one of the most dramatic landscapes on any Jamaican main road.

## History
St. Thomas-Ye-Vale Anglican Church was established in 1839 — the year following full Emancipation — to serve the Bog Walk community at the entrance to the Rio Cobre gorge, a natural bottleneck on the main road between the Spanish Town lowlands and the north coast. The church was destroyed by the 14 January 1907 Kingston earthquake and restored in 1909. In 2016 the church celebrated its 177th anniversary by honouring community members in an event reported in the *Jamaica Gleaner*. The "Ye Vale" element in the name preserves an archaic English form of "the vale" — a memory of the Spanish-era valley name "Vale of St. Thomas in the East" that was rendered in pre-modern English as "St. Thomas ye Vale."

Bog Walk developed as a community at this gorge, with the church serving travellers and local residents for nearly two centuries.

## Clergy
Bog Walk has been served within the St. Catherine deanery. The church's 'great family history' noted by a reviewer suggests a congregation with deep multigenerational roots in the community.

## Notable Facts
The Rio Cobre gorge at Bog Walk is one of Jamaica's most dramatic inland landscapes, where the river has cut through the limestone hills to create a narrow gorge that the main road follows closely. Flooding in the gorge during hurricanes regularly closes the main road, isolating north St. Catherine and St. Ann from Spanish Town and Kingston. The church at Bog Walk has witnessed many such flood events across its history. The unusual 'Ye Vale' in the church's name preserves an archaic English form that gives St. Thomas-ye-Vale a distinctively antique identity among Jamaican Anglican church names.
