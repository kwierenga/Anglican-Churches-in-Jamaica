# St. Luke's Church, Highgate
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Luke’s Anglican Church is one of the Anglican churches serving the Highgate area of St. Mary, in the interior hill country of the parish. Highgate is a market town at moderate elevation, and the presence of multiple Anglican churches in the area reflects the Diocese’s network of mission chapels serving different communities within the broader Highgate district. St. Luke’s, dedicated to the physician apostle and evangelist, has served a congregation in this part of interior St. Mary for several generations.

## History
The Highgate district of St. Mary has been served by multiple Anglican worship points reflecting the dispersed population of the surrounding hill communities. St. Luke’s developed as part of the Diocese’s mission network in interior St. Mary, serving a community within or adjacent to Highgate. The area’s agricultural economy — coffee and bananas historically, smallholder farming today — shaped the character of the congregation across the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Luke’s is a masonry chapel in the Highgate area, set in the hill country of interior St. Mary. Its elevated position gives it natural drainage and some protection from direct coastal storm surge, while the rolling terrain creates landslide and access risk during heavy rainfall events.

## Clergy
St. Luke’s has been served within the Highgate circuit of the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders have been central to sustaining regular worship in this interior community.

## Notable Facts
The Highgate district’s multiple Anglican churches reflect the historically dense mission network the Diocese maintained across the interior communities of St. Mary. Hurricane impacts on interior St. Mary have periodically isolated Highgate and surrounding communities through road damage and landslides. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to interior St. Mary; the Highgate district was in the affected zone.
