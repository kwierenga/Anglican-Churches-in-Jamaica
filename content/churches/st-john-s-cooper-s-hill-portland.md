# St. John's, Cooper's Hill
**Portland** · Church · active

St. John's Anglican Church serves the small community of Cooper's Hill in Portland's upper Rio Grande valley, situated among the hillside settlements near Unity Valley and Berridale. The Diocese lists it among Portland churches in the Eastern Jamaica Region, grouping it with neighboring missions including St. Paul's in Moore Town and St. Luke's in Comfort Castle. Gazetteers confirm Cooper's Hill as a distinct Portland locality, separate from its namesake in St. Andrew, with local wayfinding references indicating an active community hub consistent with the Diocese's pattern of small rural chapels serving valley settlements.

## History
Cooper's Hill developed as one of many settlements along Portland's interior river valleys, appearing in parish records and local histories as part of the Rio Grande corridor's community network. Diocesan records consistently place St. John's, Cooper's Hill among Portland churches within the Eastern Jamaica Region, demonstrating a long-standing Anglican presence alongside other historic missions in Moore Town and Comfort Castle. This placement reflects the Church's strategic establishment of missions throughout Portland's interior to serve remote communities during the post-emancipation period and beyond.

All clerical assignments, service schedules, and pastoral oversight for these missions are coordinated through the regional deanery network, reflecting the diocesan approach to managing multiple small congregations across Portland's challenging terrain with limited clergy resources.

## Notable Facts
