# St. Mark's (Parish Church), Mandeville
**Manchester** · Parish Church · active

St. Mark's Anglican Parish Church is the principal Anglican church of Mandeville, the capital of Manchester parish and the main urban centre of Jamaica's central highlands. Confirmed at its Mandeville location, the church is described by reviewers as consecrated in 1820 in Gothic style, built from stone, with 'stunning architecture — stone walls, pointed arch doors and windows.' It is described as a 'Mandeville historical treasure' and is 'centrally located — easy to access,' a 'solid refuge welcoming the lost, the faithful, the hopeful' that has withstood the test of time.

## History
Mandeville was established as the capital of Manchester parish in 1816 when Manchester was carved out of the older parishes. St. Mark's Parish Church was consecrated in 1820, just four years after the town's founding, and has been the religious and civic heart of Mandeville ever since. The church was built in the Gothic revival style that was fashionable in the early 19th century, with local stone giving it a distinctive appearance and durability. Mandeville is the highest-elevation parish capital in Jamaica, set at approximately 600 metres above sea level, and its cool climate attracted a substantial European and later North American residential community.

## Architecture
St. Mark's (Parish Church) is built in the tradition of Jamaican parish churches, with solid masonry walls and a nave proportioned to serve a substantial town congregation. The building carries the civic weight of its status as a mother church, with interior furnishings and memorial tablets accumulated over generations of parish life. The churchyard contains graves spanning the colonial and post-colonial periods, among the more historically layered burial grounds in Manchester. Set in the rural interior of Manchester. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. Mark's Parish Church has been the seat of the Manchester deanery and one of the most important Anglican churches in central Jamaica. As the principal church of the parish capital, its clergy have served as leaders of the Manchester circuit and prominent figures in Jamaican Anglican life.

## Notable Facts
Mandeville's status as the highest parish capital in Jamaica has given it a distinct character — the town attracted British and later North American retirees and residents drawn by its cool climate, and it has a more 'English' feel than most Jamaican towns. The church, consecrated only four years after the town's founding in 1816, has been at the centre of Mandeville's community life for over two centuries. St. Mark's Day (25 April) falls in the spring — a time of year when Mandeville's weather is at its most pleasant.
