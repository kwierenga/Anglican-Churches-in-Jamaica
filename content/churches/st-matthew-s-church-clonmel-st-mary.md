# St. Matthew's Church, Clonmel
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Matthew’s Anglican Church serves the community of Clonmel in the interior hill country of St. Mary parish. Clonmel is a small agricultural community whose name, like many in St. Mary, preserves the memory of an Irish or British estate owner from the plantation era. The church has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations, functioning as a small mission chapel within the broader St. Mary deanery network.

## History
The Clonmel district developed from the plantation landscape of interior St. Mary, with the estate name surviving long after the sugar and coffee economy transformed into the smallholder farming communities of the post-Emancipation period. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew’s to serve the freed families and their descendants who settled in the Clonmel area, dedicated to the apostle and evangelist Matthew. The church’s history runs parallel to the broader transformation of St. Mary’s interior from plantation agriculture to independent small farming.

## Architecture
St. Matthew’s is a small masonry chapel of modest proportions characteristic of Anglican mission buildings in the St. Mary interior. The building sits in the hilly terrain of the Clonmel district, in a landscape of small farms and wooded ridges. The interior hill-country position creates landslide and road-closure risk during the heavy rainfall events that characterise St. Mary’s interior, particularly during hurricane passages.

## Clergy
Clonmel has been served within the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements with other interior congregations. As a small mission chapel, its pastoral life has depended heavily on lay leaders who sustain regular worship between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
St. Mary parish is known for its high rainfall, driven by the orographic effect of the Blue Mountains trapping moisture from the northeast trade winds. Communities like Clonmel in the interior hills experience some of the heaviest rainfall in Jamaica, making road conditions a constant challenge and landslide risk a recurring concern during hurricane season. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious damage to interior St. Mary hill communities. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to this region; the Clonmel area was affected and St. Matthew’s congregation required pastoral support in the aftermath.
