# St. Andrew's, Labyrinth
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Andrew's Anglican Church in Labyrinth, western St. Mary, serves as a daughter church of Holy Trinity Retreat. This countryside church ministers to farming communities near the historic White River bridge. During Hurricane Melissa (2025), western St. Mary suffered severe infrastructure damage, leaving the congregation facing prolonged outages and access challenges.

## History
Established as part of Holy Trinity's expansion, St. Andrew's likely dates to the late 19th century, serving post-emancipation communities in the White River valley. The area combines natural beauty at Old Spanish Bridge with documented poverty, creating the complex social context where this Anglican church has ministered from colonial times through independence.

## Architecture
Photographs depict St. Andrew's as a traditional Jamaican country church with masonry walls and zinc roof, typical of late 19th-century rural Anglican architecture. The active churchyard cemetery and hillside location above the White River make it vulnerable to erosion and landslide risks during severe weather events.

## Clergy
St. Andrew's shares clergy with Holy Trinity Retreat and other daughter churches, supported by local lay leadership. The church remains active in hosting funerals and community events, maintaining strong ties with local schools and farms while supporting families through agricultural challenges and hurricane seasons.

## Notable Facts
As one of Holy Trinity's four daughter churches, St. Andrew's ministers in a district famous for the Old Spanish Bridge swimming spot. The area has historical storm vulnerability, with Labyrinth Primary losing its roof in 1988's Hurricane Gilbert. Hurricane Melissa (2025) caused catastrophic damage across western St. Mary, likely affecting the historic church and continuing its story through climate challenges.
