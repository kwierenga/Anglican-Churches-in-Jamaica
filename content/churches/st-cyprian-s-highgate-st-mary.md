# St. Cyprian's, Highgate
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Cyprian’s Anglican Church is the confirmed principal Anglican church of Highgate, one of the largest interior market towns of St. Mary parish. Set on the main road through Highgate, the church serves the town’s market community and the surrounding farming districts. Highgate occupies a strategic position in interior St. Mary as a commercial hub for the hill communities of the central parish, and St. Cyprian’s has been its established Anglican presence for well over a century.

## History
Highgate developed as the main market town for the interior communities of central St. Mary, growing in commercial importance as coffee and banana cultivation spread across the parish hills in the 19th century. The Anglican Diocese established St. Cyprian’s as the principal church for this interior hub, dedicated to the 3rd-century bishop of Carthage who is revered for his writings on church unity and his courage as a martyr. The church has served Highgate’s market families, traders, and the surrounding farming communities through the economic transformations of the 19th and 20th centuries.

## Architecture
Reviewer descriptions confirm St. Cyprian’s as a stone-built church in a ‘rustic setting’, characterised as an ‘old style country church’ with an authentic historical character. The building sits in the Highgate townscape, accessible from the main road, and is set within a churchyard. The church’s moderate elevation in the interior hills gives it some natural protection from direct coastal storm surge while exposing it to the heavy orographic rainfall of interior St. Mary and wind during hurricane passages.

## Clergy
St. Cyprian’s serves as the hub church of the Highgate circuit of the St. Mary deanery, with its clergy responsible for the network of smaller mission chapels in the surrounding interior communities. The church has maintained a more substantial congregation than the smaller mission chapels, given Highgate’s market-town character and population.

## Notable Facts
St. Cyprian's is identified in Satchell's *Parish History of St. Mary* as one of the four principal Anglican churches of the parish, alongside the Port Maria parish church, St. James' Annotto Bay, and Holy Trinity Retreat — noted for its "beautiful and inspiring" stained-glass windows. Highgate gave its name to a widespread Jamaican soil series — the Highgate Clay — identified during the national soil survey. The Anglican Church also established St. Cyprian's Preparatory School in the town. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious damage to interior St. Mary, with landslides affecting the roads into Highgate. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind impacts to interior St. Mary; Highgate and St. Cyprian's congregation were directly affected.
