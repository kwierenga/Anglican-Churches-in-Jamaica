# St. Jude's, Bourbon
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Jude's Anglican Church serves the community of Bourbon in Portland parish, a small farming district in the hill country of western Portland. Bourbon is an interior community, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to local farming families for several generations. The dedication to St. Jude — the patron saint of lost causes and desperate situations — gives this chapel a distinctive spiritual identity, particularly apt for a small rural community maintaining its faith in the face of isolation and periodic storm damage.

## History
Bourbon sits in the interior hill country of Portland, in the farming communities of the western parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Jude's as a mission chapel to serve this area, as part of its comprehensive mission network across Portland's interior. The community's name, like many Jamaican estate names, preserves a connection to a French-derived colonial naming tradition.

## Architecture
St. Jude's is a small masonry chapel in the interior hills of Portland. The building sits in the hilly terrain of the Bourbon district, exposed to Portland's extraordinary annual rainfall and the landslide risk associated with steep terrain during major storm events.

## Clergy
Bourbon has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. As a small interior chapel, it depends on lay leaders for its regular pastoral life.

## Notable Facts
St. Jude is invoked in Catholic and Anglican tradition as the patron of difficult and desperate causes, a dimension of the saint's intercessory role that resonates across Jamaica's rural communities who have faced repeated hurricane devastation across the centuries. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to Portland's interior; the Bourbon community and congregation were in the affected zone.
