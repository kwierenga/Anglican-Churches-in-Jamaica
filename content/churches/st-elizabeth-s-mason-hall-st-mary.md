# St. Elizabeth's, Mason Hall
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Elizabeth’s Anglican Church serves Mason Hall, a community on the north coast of St. Mary parish, near Galina. The area sits close to the coast with the interior hills of St. Mary rising to the south. St. Elizabeth’s has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations. The dedication to St. Elizabeth, the mother of John the Baptist, is a relatively unusual choice for a Jamaican Anglican church and gives this chapel a distinctive identity.

## History
Mason Hall sits in the northern coastal district of St. Mary, near Galina on the island’s north shore. The Anglican Diocese established St. Elizabeth’s as part of its mission outreach into the coastal communities of St. Mary. The church has served the Mason Hall district across the generations since its foundation.

## Architecture
St. Elizabeth’s is a small masonry chapel on the north coast of St. Mary. The coastal position exposes the building to wind and storm surge risk during hurricane seasons, a recurring challenge for churches along Jamaica’s north shore.

## Clergy
Mason Hall has been served within the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements, grouped with nearby north coast congregations including St. Peter’s, Galina.

## Notable Facts
St. Elizabeth’s unusual dedication — one of very few churches of this name in the Jamaican Anglican network — marks it out among the many small mission chapels of St. Mary. Its position near Galina on the north coast places it in one of the more scenically striking parts of the parish. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought serious wind and coastal impacts to this part of St. Mary.
