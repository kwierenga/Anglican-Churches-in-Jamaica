# St. Matthew's, Jack's Hill
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Jack's Hill, a residential community in the hills of northern St. Andrew above the New Kingston commercial district. Jack's Hill is one of the premier hillside residential addresses in the Kingston area, with large properties, cool temperatures, and panoramic views over the city and harbour. The church has served this elevated community for several generations.

## History
Jack's Hill developed as an upscale residential neighbourhood in the hills above Kingston, attracting professional and business families who sought the cooler climate and larger land plots of the northern St. Andrew hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew's to serve this elevated community, dedicated to the apostle and tax collector who became an evangelist.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a hillside church in the northern St. Andrew hills, at a position that gives it panoramic views over Kingston and the Kingston Harbour. The elevated position creates wind exposure during hurricane passages and the hilly terrain creates landslide risk during heavy rainfall.

## Clergy
Jack's Hill has been served within the northern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements with the other hillside communities.

## Notable Facts
Jack's Hill is one of the most sought-after residential addresses in Jamaica, favoured for its climate, views, and sense of distance from the urban pressures of Kingston below. The community has been home to many prominent Jamaicans in politics, business, and the arts. The church here serves a congregation with a distinctly different social character from the urban mission churches of the southern metropolitan area, reflecting the social diversity of the St. Andrew deanery.
