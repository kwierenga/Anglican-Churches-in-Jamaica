# St. Luke's Church, Chapelton
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. Luke's Anglican Church serves Chapelton, the administrative capital of Clarendon parish, a market town in the interior north of May Pen. Chapelton is the parish capital in name, though May Pen is the larger commercial centre. St. Luke's provides Anglican ministry in the capital town alongside St. Paul's, the church confirmed as the oldest in Chapelton.

## History
Chapelton's status as the administrative capital of Clarendon gave it an Anglican church presence from the early colonial period. St. Luke's has served the Chapelton community alongside the older St. Paul's, providing ministry for different parts of the town. Chapelton developed as a market town serving the interior farming communities of Clarendon.

## Clergy
Chapelton has been served within the Clarendon deanery. St. Luke's and St. Paul's together provide Anglican coverage for Clarendon's parish capital.

## Notable Facts
Chapelton's designation as the parish capital of Clarendon is somewhat paradoxical, as May Pen is the much larger commercial centre and is more widely recognised as Clarendon's main town. The distinction between administrative and commercial capitals is a legacy of colonial-era parish organisation.
