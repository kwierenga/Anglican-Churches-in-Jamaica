# St. Andrew's, Hope Bay
**Portland** · Church · unknown

St. Andrew's Anglican Church serves Hope Bay, a community on the north coast of Portland parish between St. Margaret's Bay and Buff Bay. Hope Bay is a small coastal community in western Portland with a fishing tradition, and St. Andrew's — named for the patron saint of Scotland and the fisherman apostle — serves its congregation along this stretch of the Portland north coast.

## History
Hope Bay sits on the coastal plain of western Portland, on the main coastal road between the hills of St. Margaret's Bay and the more substantial town of Buff Bay. The Anglican Diocese established St. Andrew's to serve the Hope Bay fishing and farming community, with the dedication to the fisherman apostle carrying particular resonance for a coastal congregation. The church has served the community through the transformations of Portland's coastal economy.

## Architecture
St. Andrew's is a coastal church directly exposed to the Caribbean Sea on Portland's north coast. The flat coastal position at Hope Bay creates storm surge vulnerability during hurricane passages, and the proximity of hills creates compound risk from heavy rainfall and landslides.

## Clergy
Hope Bay has been served within the Portland deanery coast circuit. The community maintains its congregation through the local fishing and farming families.

## Notable Facts
The dedication to St. Andrew is particularly fitting for a fishing community — Andrew was a fisherman on the Sea of Galilee before becoming an apostle, and has been invoked as patron by fishing communities across the Christian world. Hope Bay's coastal position makes it one of the more surge-exposed communities on Portland's north coast. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along this coast; Hope Bay was in the storm's direct path.
