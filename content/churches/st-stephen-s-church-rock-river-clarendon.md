# St. Stephen's Church, Rock River
**Clarendon** · Church · active

St. Stephen's Anglican Church serves Rock River, a community in the interior of Clarendon parish. Rock River is a small farming district in the interior, and St. Stephen's has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. A second Anglican church, St. James', is also recorded at Rock River approximately 14 km away, suggesting the Rock River name covers a broad district or that there is a coordinate error.

## History
Rock River sits in the interior of Clarendon, in the farming landscape between the south coast plains and the northern hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Stephen's to serve this community. The church has served the local farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Stephen's Church is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Clarendon. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Rock River has been served within the Clarendon deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
St. Stephen's Day (26 December) is one of the most significant feast days in the Anglican calendar — the feast of the first Christian martyr falls on the day after Christmas, giving churches with this dedication a special liturgical prominence in the Christmas season.
