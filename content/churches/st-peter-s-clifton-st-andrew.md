# St. Peter's, Clifton
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

St. Peter's Anglican Church serves Clifton, a community in St. Andrew in the residential and agricultural zone north-east of the urban centre. Clifton is a small district in the eastern hills of St. Andrew, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations.

## History
Clifton sits in the eastern hill country of St. Andrew, in the residential and agricultural landscape that extends from the suburban fringe toward the Blue Mountain foothills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Peter's as part of its ministry network in eastern St. Andrew, dedicated to the prince of the apostles.

## Architecture
St. Peter's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Andrew in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Andrew. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Clifton has been served within the eastern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
The eastern hill country of St. Andrew forms the transition zone between the urban metropolitan area and the rural Blue Mountain communities. Communities like Clifton occupy this in-between landscape, with residents who may commute to the city while maintaining ties to the agricultural character of the hills.
