# St. John's, Ocho Rios
**St. Ann** · Church · active

St. John’s Anglican Church in Ocho Rios serves the coastal town of Ocho Rios in St. Ann. The church dates back to the 19th century and has roots in Jamaica’s colonial past when slavery shaped many communities. After Emancipation in 1838, the church became important for freed people and their descendants. Today the church remains active, welcoming worshippers and visitors in this tourist-well known region.

## History
Ocho Rios and the surrounding area were influenced by the plantation economy during slavery. After Emancipation (1838), freed families moved into the area and established farms, trade and small villages. The Anglican Diocese grew its presence, and St. John’s Church was built for the community’s spiritual and educational needs. It appears in old photographs showing graves and churchyard, and is listed in the national library’s digital collection.

## Architecture
The church is built of stone, with strong walls and simple but dignified design. It has pointed-arch windows, a nave for the congregation and a chancel toward the front. The roof is pitched to handle tropical weather and the interior appears to have wooden fittings and pews. The churchyard includes old graves which reflect many generations of local families.

## Clergy
St. John’s belongs to the Anglican Diocese and is part of the St. Ann Deanery (north coast). It has served as a parish church for many years, supporting Sunday schools, youth gatherings, and community outreach. After Independence in 1962, the church continued its work with lay readers and the Mothers’ Union to reach local people, tourists, and workers in the region.

## Notable Facts
Over the years St. John’s has faced challenges from storms, population changes and tourism growth. It has been maintained, and regular services still take place. Today the church remains a focal point for worship, heritage tours, and community events in Ocho Rios, linking Jamaica’s history of slavery, emancipation and independence with its modern life.
