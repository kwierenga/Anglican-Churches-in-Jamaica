# St. Alban's, Albion Mountain
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Alban’s Anglican Church serves Albion Mountain, a community in the hills above Port Maria in St. Mary parish. Albion Mountain is a residential and agricultural district on the elevated ground above the parish capital, with views over Port Maria harbour. The church provides Anglican ministry to the hill community above the coast, serving a population distinct from but connected to the coastal town of Port Maria below.

## History
Albion Mountain developed as a hill community above Port Maria as the parish capital grew, with families settling on the elevated ground for residential and agricultural purposes. The Anglican Diocese established St. Alban’s to serve this hilltop community, dedicated to the first British Christian martyr, St. Alban. The church has served Albion Mountain’s community for several generations, providing a distinct Anglican presence above the coast.

## Architecture
St. Alban’s is a hilltop church set on the elevated ground above Port Maria, giving it both a commanding position and some natural protection from direct coastal storm surge. However, the hillside terrain creates landslide risk during heavy rainfall, and the elevated position exposes the church to higher winds than the sheltered coastal position of the Parish Church below. The building is consistent with modest Anglican hill-country construction in St. Mary.

## Clergy
Albion Mountain has been served within the Port Maria circuit of the St. Mary deanery, linked closely to the parish church below. Shared clergy arrangements connect it to the broader Anglican network of the parish capital area.

## Notable Facts
Albion Mountain’s elevated position above Port Maria creates a different storm risk profile from the beachfront parish church below. While it avoids direct surge, wind exposure on the hillside can be severe during hurricane passages. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious wind and landslide damage to the hills above Port Maria. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) struck the north coast of St. Mary; Albion Mountain and its congregation were in the storm’s impact zone.
