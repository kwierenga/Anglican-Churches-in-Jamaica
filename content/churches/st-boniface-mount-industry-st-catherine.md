# St. Boniface, Mount Industry
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Boniface Anglican Church serves Mount Industry, a community in St. Catherine parish. The dedication to St. Boniface — the 8th-century English monk who Christianised Germany and became the Apostle of the Germans — is found in only one other entry in this dataset (the Stony Gut church in St. Thomas), making it a notably uncommon choice. Mount Industry is a small farming community in the interior of St. Catherine.

## History
Mount Industry sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the farming communities between Spanish Town and the Bog Walk area. The Anglican Diocese established St. Boniface to serve this community, choosing the unusual dedication to the missionary monk Winfrid (Boniface) who organised the German church in the 8th century. The church has served the Mount Industry farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Boniface is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the hilly interior of St. Catherine. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Mount Industry has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
St. Boniface's feast day (5 June) commemorates the English missionary who reorganised the Frankish church and extended Christianity into Germany and Frisia, eventually being martyred in 754. His dedication in a Jamaican interior farming community is one of the more historically evocative naming choices in the island's Anglican network — connecting Mount Industry to the 8th-century missionary tradition of the English church.
