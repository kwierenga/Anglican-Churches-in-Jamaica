# St. Alban's, Kingston
**Kingston** · Mission · unknown

St. Alban's Anglican Church serves the downtown Kingston area, recorded St. Alban's is one of the downtown Kingston Anglican churches serving the residential and commercial communities of the city centre, dedicated to Alban — the first Christian martyr of Britain, executed during the Roman period for sheltering a Christian priest. The church sits in the heart of one of the Caribbean's most historically significant urban landscapes.

## History
Downtown Kingston developed as the commercial and residential heart of Jamaica's capital following the establishment of the city after the 1692 Port Royal earthquake. The Anglican Diocese established multiple churches in the city centre to serve the dense population of merchants, tradespeople, government officials, and their families. St. Alban's serves the downtown Kingston community in the historic urban core, maintaining an Anglican presence in a neighbourhood that has undergone significant social and economic transformation across the post-independence period.

## Clergy
Downtown Kingston has been served within the Kingston deanery, with St. Alban's part of the cluster of Anglican churches that maintain the Diocese's presence in the historic city centre.

## Notable Facts
The dedication to St. Alban — Britain's first Christian martyr — carries a particular resonance in a country whose entire Anglican heritage derives from the English church tradition. Alban sheltered and was converted by a Christian priest during the Roman persecution, and was executed when he refused to recant; his example of faith under persecution has been commemorated in the English church for over 1,500 years. Downtown Kingston's Anglican churches collectively represent one of the oldest concentrations of continuous Christian worship in the western hemisphere.
