# St. Gabriel's, Brandon Hill
**St. Andrew** · Church · inactive

St. Gabriel's Anglican Church serves Brandon Hill, a community in southern St. Andrew in the residential zone adjoining the Kingston metropolitan area. Brandon Hill is a neighbourhood in the urban fringe of southern St. Andrew, and St. Gabriel's has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The dedication to the archangel Gabriel — the divine messenger of the Annunciation — gives this church a celestial patron shared with few other Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Brandon Hill developed as a residential community in the southern urban fringe of St. Andrew. The Anglican Diocese established St. Gabriel's to serve this community, providing a local place of worship in the urban southern zone.

## Architecture
St. Gabriel's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Andrew. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the hilly interior of St. Andrew. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Brandon Hill has been served within the southern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Gabriel the Archangel is celebrated in the Anglican calendar (along with Michael and Raphael) on 29 September — Michaelmas. The Annunciation — Gabriel's message to Mary — is commemorated on 25 March. Churches dedicated to Gabriel are relatively rare in Jamaica, giving St. Gabriel's Brandon Hill a distinctive celestial patron in the local Anglican network.
