# St. John's Church (Old), Leith Hall
**St. Thomas** · Church · ruin

The Old St. John's Church at Leith Hall is a historic Anglican building — possibly a ruin or disused structure — at Leith Hall, a community on the eastern coast of St. Thomas parish near Port Morant. Leith Hall sits on the coastal plain of eastern St. Thomas, near the mouth of the Plantain Garden River, in a landscape shaped by centuries of sugar cultivation. The 'Old' designation suggests this is a historic or possibly ruinous building, distinct from the active St. Barnabas' Anglican Church also in the Port Morant/Leith Hall locality.

## History
Leith Hall is a community on the eastern coast of St. Thomas, in the sugar-producing district around Port Morant and the Plantain Garden River valley. The Anglican church here is one of the oldest ecclesiastical sites in this part of St. Thomas, predating the current St. Barnabas' church in the area. The 'Old' designation in the dataset record suggests this building may have been superseded by a newer church, fallen into disuse, or partially ruined, while retaining its identity as a historical landmark. Its confirmed coordinates place it in the Leith Hall/Port Morant coastal area.

## Architecture
The Old St. John's is recorded as a historic structure in the coastal community of Leith Hall. The building's age and condition are indicated by the 'Old' designation. The coastal plain of eastern St. Thomas around Port Morant is directly exposed to the Caribbean Sea, making it one of the more hurricane-vulnerable stretches of the St. Thomas coast. The Plantain Garden River valley behind Leith Hall also creates flooding risk during heavy rainfall.

## Clergy
As an old or ruined building, St. John's Leith Hall is no longer served by active clergy. In its operational years it was served within the St. Thomas deanery, likely linked to the Port Morant circuit.

## Notable Facts
The Leith Hall and Port Morant area of eastern St. Thomas was historically one of the most productive sugar districts in Jamaica. The Plantain Garden River valley behind Port Morant supported extensive sugar cultivation, and the Anglican church here served the planter class and their estates. The area was devastated by Hurricane Ivan (2004), which struck the south-east coast of Jamaica with catastrophic force, causing severe damage across the coastal communities of St. Thomas.
