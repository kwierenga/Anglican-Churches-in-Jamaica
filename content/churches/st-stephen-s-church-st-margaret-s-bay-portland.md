# St. Stephen's Church, St. Margaret's Bay
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Stephen's Anglican Church serves St. Margaret's Bay, a small north-coast community in Portland parish situated between Port Antonio and Hope Bay. St. Margaret's Bay is a quiet coastal community with a fishing tradition, sheltered by headlands on the Portland north coast. The church has served this coastal community for well over a century, confirmed at its present location on the main coastal road through the Bay.

## History
St. Margaret's Bay developed as a small coastal settlement on the north shore of Portland, one of the series of fishing and farming communities that dot the coastline between Port Antonio and Buff Bay. The Anglican Diocese established St. Stephen's as part of its mission network along the Portland coast, serving the local fishing and farming families. The church is dedicated to the first Christian martyr, St. Stephen the Deacon, and has served the St. Margaret's Bay community through the transformations of the 19th and 20th centuries.

## Architecture
St. Stephen's is a coastal church on the north shore of Portland, directly exposed to the Caribbean Sea. Google Places confirms it at its location on the main coastal road. The church's coastal position creates storm surge and wind exposure during hurricane passages along the Portland north coast.

## Clergy
St. Stephen's has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements, linked to the Port Antonio circuit. The coastal community has maintained an active congregation through the fishing and farming families of the Bay.

## Notable Facts
St. Margaret's Bay is one of several small named bays along Portland's north coast, each with its own distinct community character. The Bay's name, like the parish of St. Mary, reflects the tradition of Marian dedications in Jamaica's colonial geography. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) struck Portland's north coast severely. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along Jamaica's north coast; St. Margaret's Bay was directly in the storm's path and the St. Stephen's congregation experienced direct coastal impact.
