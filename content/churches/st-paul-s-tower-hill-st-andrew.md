# St. Paul's, Tower Hill
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves Tower Hill, a community in southern St. Andrew in the urban residential zone between Half-Way-Tree and the Kingston waterfront. Tower Hill is a residential neighbourhood, and St. Paul's has served its community for several generations. The dedication to St. Paul the Apostle is among the most common in the Anglican network across Jamaica.

## History
Tower Hill developed as a residential neighbourhood in the urban expansion of St. Andrew toward the south, part of the broader metropolitan growth of Kingston and its environs. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's to serve this community, providing a local place of Anglican worship in the southern urban zone of the parish.

## Architecture
St. Paul's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Andrew in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the hilly interior of St. Andrew. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Tower Hill has been served within the southern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
St. Paul's dedication connects this urban community church to one of the most celebrated figures in Christian history — the apostle whose letters form a substantial portion of the New Testament and whose missionary journeys established Christian communities across the Mediterranean world. The dedication to Paul is found in Anglican churches from the earliest colonial period to the most recent urban mission establishments.
