# St. Augustine's, Coral Gardens
**St. James** · Church · active

St. Augustine's Anglican Church serves Coral Gardens, an established residential community in the Montego Bay area of St. James. The church stands at the corner of Sugar Mill Road and Melrose Terrace, Coral Gardens, Montego Bay.

## History
Coral Gardens is one of the residential and commercial districts that developed in Montego Bay during the parish's mid-twentieth-century tourism-and-suburban expansion, on the western flank of the city. The Anglican Diocese established St. Augustine's to serve this growing residential community, following the pattern of mission planting in new urban neighbourhoods that characterised the diocese's twentieth-century work in Montego Bay alongside Holy Trinity Westgate, Mary Magdalene Granville, St. Francis Glendevon, and Holy Cross Mount Salem.

Coral Gardens is also the name of a notorious incident in modern Jamaican history: the **Coral Gardens incident** of April 1963, when state security forces conducted a violent crackdown on the Rastafarian community in the area, an event recognised in 2017 by the Jamaican government with a formal apology to the Rastafarian community. Ministry in Coral Gardens has continued through that history, alongside the parallel Anglican presence in the surrounding Montego Bay communities.

The dedication to St. Augustine of Hippo — the great fourth- and fifth-century theologian whose *Confessions* and *City of God* shaped Western Christianity, with a feast day on 28 August — is one of the more theologically substantial Anglican dedications, distinct from St. Augustine of Canterbury (who is commemorated 26 May).

## Architecture
St. Augustine's is a functional Anglican church building in the Coral Gardens residential district. The Montego Bay area is exposed to the major hurricanes that periodically strike Jamaica's western coast, and the parish has been affected by Hurricane Gilbert (1988), Hurricane Ivan (2004), and most recently Hurricane Melissa (October 2025).

## Clergy
St. Augustine's Coral Gardens is served within the Montego Bay Region's St. James Deanery; the church maintains a public Facebook page under "St. Augustine's Church" Montego Bay.
