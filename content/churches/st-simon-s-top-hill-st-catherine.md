# St. Simon's, Top Hill
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Simon's Anglican Church serves Top Hill, a community in the upland country of St. Catherine parish.

## History
Top Hill is one of the small upland farming districts of St. Catherine, in the broken hill country of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Simon's as a small mission chapel to serve this community as part of the wider rural church-planting expansion that the diocese carried out from the late nineteenth century onward. After the disestablishment of the Anglican Church in 1870, the diocese was free to organise its mission network on its own canons and resources, founding small chapels in upland communities like Top Hill that had previously had no direct Anglican presence.

The dedication to St. Simon — Simon the Zealot, one of the twelve apostles, distinguished from Simon Peter by his political designation — is found in only a small number of Jamaican Anglican churches. Simon's feast day (28 October) is shared with St. Jude Thaddaeus; the joint feast appears in the dedication "SS Simon and Jude" at Ewarton in northern St. Catherine. No substantive foundational date or architectural history for St. Simon's Top Hill has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed.

## Architecture
St. Simon's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Catherine interior. The hill-country position creates wind exposure during storm passages.

## Clergy
St. Simon's Top Hill has been served within the Kingston Region's St. Catherine Deanery.
