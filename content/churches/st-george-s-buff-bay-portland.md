# St. George's, Buff Bay
**Portland** · Church · active

St. George's Anglican Church is the historic Anglican church of Buff Bay, a town on the north coast of Portland at the mouth of the Buff Bay River. Buff Bay is one of Portland's larger coastal communities, historically important as a commercial and transport hub for the western part of the parish. The church is confirmed at its location on St. George's Street in Buff Bay town, with reviewers praising its impressive stone architecture as giving the town 'a bit medieval' character that 'just stands out as something ancient but strong.'

## History
Buff Bay developed as one of the main towns of Portland's north coast, with the river providing fresh water and a natural focal point for settlement. The Anglican church here has a long history connected to the town's commercial and planting class. St. George's was established as a substantial church in Buff Bay — its stone architecture is notable enough to draw particular comment from visitors who describe it as awe-inspiring and a testament to the Anglican Diocese's heritage in the area. The church's name, dedicated to the patron saint of England, reflects the colonial cultural framework within which it was built.

## Architecture

It just stands out as something ancient but strong in the town.' Another describes the atmosphere as giving 'a bit medieval' feel. The church sits near the Buff Bay River mouth, with the river creating compound flooding risk alongside storm surge during hurricane passages.

## Clergy
St. George's serves as the principal Anglican church of Buff Bay and has been the hub of the western Portland deanery circuit, with clergy covering the mission network in the western part of the parish. The church has maintained an active congregation drawing from the town's community and the surrounding farming and fishing districts.

## Notable Facts
Buff Bay's position at the mouth of the Buff Bay River makes it one of the more flood-prone communities on Portland's north coast. The river can overflow during the extraordinary rainfall events that Portland experiences during hurricane passages. St. George's stone construction has given it durability across multiple hurricane impacts — Hurricane Gilbert (1988) struck Buff Bay severely, and the town has been repeatedly affected by storm surge and flooding. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along the north coast; Buff Bay was in the storm's path and the congregation experienced direct impact.
