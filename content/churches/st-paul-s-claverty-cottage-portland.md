# St. Paul's, Claverty Cottage
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves the Claverty Cottage community in Portland parish, a small farming district in the interior hills south-east of Port Antonio. Claverty Cottage is one of the more unusually named communities in Portland, its name presumably derived from a colonial estate designation. The church has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations, and a second Anglican worship point (St. Catherine's) is also recorded at Claverty Cottage, suggesting the district has historically supported more than one chapel.

## History
Claverty Cottage sits in the interior hill country of Portland south-east of Port Antonio, in the farming communities that developed in the post-Emancipation period. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's as a mission chapel for this area. The presence of two Anglican entries for Claverty Cottage suggests the district is geographically dispersed or has historically maintained two separate worship points for different parts of the community.

## Architecture
St. Paul's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Portland in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Portland. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Claverty Cottage has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church's pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
The name Claverty Cottage is among the more distinctive community names in Portland, one of many unusual estate-derived names preserved in the parish's human geography. Portland's interior communities south-east of Port Antonio are among the more remote in the parish, with limited road access that becomes challenging during major storm events. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought serious rain and wind impacts to this part of Portland.
