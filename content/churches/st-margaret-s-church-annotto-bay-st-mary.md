# St. Margaret's Church, Annotto Bay
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Margaret’s Anglican Church serves the Annotto Bay area of St. Mary, a north-coast town at the mouth of the Pencar River that was historically one of the main banana-shipping ports of the parish. Annotto Bay is a modest but historically significant coastal community, and St. Margaret’s has provided Anglican ministry to its district for several generations. The dedication to St. Margaret, the virgin martyr associated with the courage of faith, gives the church a distinctive patron among the several Anglican dedications in the Annotto Bay area.

## History
Annotto Bay developed as a port and service town on the north coast of St. Mary, with the Pencar River providing the natural harbour that made it useful for the banana trade in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The Anglican Diocese maintained a presence in Annotto Bay through multiple churches serving different parts of the community. St. Margaret’s was established to serve the local farming and fishing population, growing from the Diocese’s mission outreach into the coastal communities of St. Mary.

## Architecture
St. Margaret’s is a coastal church in a town directly exposed to the Caribbean Sea on the north coast of St. Mary. Annotto Bay’s position on the coast, at the mouth of a river, creates compound storm risk: coastal surge from the sea and flooding from the river during heavy rainfall. The church’s construction is suited to this environment, with the durability required for long-term survival in a coastal location subject to periodic hurricane impact.

## Clergy
St. Margaret’s has been served within the Annotto Bay circuit of the St. Mary deanery, grouped with other Anglican churches in the town and district under shared clergy arrangements. The church’s coastal community has maintained an active congregation through the fishing and farming families of the district.

## Notable Facts
Annotto Bay’s position on the north coast of St. Mary has made it one of the more hurricane-exposed communities in the parish. The combination of sea exposure and river flooding risk creates significant vulnerability during major storms. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) struck the north coast of St. Mary with great force. Hurricane Ivan (2004) again brought coastal damage. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along Jamaica’s north coast; Annotto Bay was directly in the storm’s path and the church’s congregation experienced direct storm impact.
