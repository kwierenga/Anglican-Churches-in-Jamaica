# St. Simon's, Clifton
**St. Andrew** · Church · unknown

This St. Simon's entry represents a second Clifton entry in St. Andrew, Peter's also recorded at Clifton. The distance between the two Clifton entries suggests either two genuinely distinct communities sharing the name Clifton in different parts of St. Andrew, or a significant coordinate error in one entry. St. Simon's serves its part of the Clifton district.

## History
Two Anglican churches are recorded at Clifton in St. Andrew, suggesting the Clifton name covers a dispersed area or that two communities in different parts of the parish share this name. The Anglican Diocese maintained worship points across both.

## Architecture
St. Simon's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Andrew. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Andrew. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Clifton has been served within the eastern St. Andrew deanery circuit under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Simon the Apostle — also known as Simon the Zealot — is one of the less-documented apostles in the New Testament, distinguished from Simon Peter by his political designation. Dedications to Simon the Zealot are relatively uncommon in Jamaica and give this church a distinctive apostolic patron.
