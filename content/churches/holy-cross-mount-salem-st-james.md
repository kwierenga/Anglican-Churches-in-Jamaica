# Holy Cross, Mount Salem
**St. James** · Church · active

Holy Cross Anglican Church serves Mount Salem, one of the larger inner-community areas of Montego Bay, situated on the hillside rising above the city's western districts. Mount Salem is a well-established residential community with a mixed social character — working families, small businesses, and a dense network of community institutions — and Holy Cross has provided the Anglican presence there for several decades. The church is grouped with other Montego Bay congregations within the diocese's Montego Bay Region.

## History
Mount Salem developed as a hillside residential community in the expanding western suburbs of Montego Bay, growing rapidly in the latter half of the twentieth century as internal migration brought families from rural Jamaica into the city. The Anglican Diocese established Holy Cross as a mission church to serve Mount Salem's growing population, following the pattern of church-planting in new urban communities across the Diocese. The dedication to the Holy Cross — a deeply traditional Anglican Christological dedication — reflects the church's character as a planted urban mission rather than an inherited colonial-period parish church.

Mount Salem has in more recent decades been identified in national security reporting as one of the communities of concern in the greater Montego Bay area; in September 2017 the Government declared it Jamaica's first Zone of Special Operations (ZOSO), a social reality that has shaped the pastoral challenges facing the church.

## Architecture
Holy Cross is a functional church building on the Mount Salem hillside, set within a community of dense residential development above the Montego Bay coastal plain. The elevated hillside position gives the church some protection from direct coastal storm surge, though the steep terrain creates landslide and flood risk during heavy rainfall. The building serves as both a place of worship and a community gathering point, with the church's presence on the hillside making it a visible landmark in the Mount Salem community.

## Clergy
Holy Cross has been served within the Montego Bay Region, grouped with other western Montego Bay congregations in shared clergy arrangements typical of urban Anglican administration in Jamaica. Lay leaders — wardens, Mothers' Union members, and youth workers — have been important to sustaining the church's life and its connections to the wider Mount Salem community.

## Notable Facts
Hurricane Melissa, which made Category 5 landfall in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025, brought heavy rainfall and damaging winds to the hillside communities of Montego Bay; Holy Cross and its congregation were in the affected zone, though specific damage to this church has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
