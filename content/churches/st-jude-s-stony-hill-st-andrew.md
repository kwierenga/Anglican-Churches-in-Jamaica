# St. Jude's, Stony Hill
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

St. Jude's Anglican Church is the principal Anglican church of Stony Hill, a town in northern St. Andrew on the main road leading up into the Blue Mountains. Confirmed by Google Places at its location on the Stony Hill Road, the church is described by reviewers as a welcoming community set in the cool hillside, with services of approximately 90 minutes, good parking, and a congregation that integrates technology, music, and art into its worship. The church has served Stony Hill for several decades.

## History
Stony Hill developed as a small town on the road from Kingston into the Blue Mountains, serving as a service and commercial centre for the hill communities above. It sits at a point where the road begins to climb steeply, creating a natural stopping point for travellers. A reviewer notes the church 'has been here for many years (50 something years I think),' placing its establishment in the mid-20th century, consistent with the suburban expansion of northern St. Andrew in the post-independence era.

## Architecture
St. Jude's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Andrew in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the hilly interior of St. Andrew. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
St. Jude's serves as the hub church of the Stony Hill circuit of the St. Andrew deanery, with its clergy responsible for the network of smaller churches in the surrounding hill communities. The current rector is noted by reviewers as providing 'everyday life lessons' in a preaching style appreciated by the congregation.

## Notable Facts
St. Jude is the patron saint of lost causes and desperate situations, and churches dedicated to him are found throughout the Anglican and Catholic world. The Stony Hill church's active social media presence and its integration of technology into worship reflects the contemporary character of a congregation that has evolved significantly from its mid-20th-century origins. Stony Hill is a key waypoint on the road to Jamaica's mountain communities and is exposed to the extreme orographic rainfall that the Blue Mountains generate.
