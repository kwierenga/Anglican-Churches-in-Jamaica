# St. Paul's, Tryall
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves Tryall, a community in St. Elizabeth parish.

## History
Tryall is one of the small farming districts of St. Elizabeth — its name preserves a colonial-era estate name (a number of Jamaican estates bore the name "Tryall" or "Trial," typically given by proprietors as an early reference to the experimental nature of new sugar cultivation). The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's as a small mission chapel to serve the Tryall community as part of the wider rural church-planting expansion that the diocese carried out across St. Elizabeth from the late nineteenth century onward.

The dedication to St. Paul the apostle — author of much of the New Testament, missionary across the Mediterranean world, with feast days on 25 January (his conversion) and 29 June (with St. Peter) — is one of the most common Anglican dedications.

No substantive foundational date or detailed architectural history for St. Paul's Tryall has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed; the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, suggesting a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Architecture
St. Paul's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Elizabeth interior. The position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge while remaining exposed to wind and rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
St. Paul's Tryall is served within the Montego Bay Region's St. Elizabeth deanery network.
