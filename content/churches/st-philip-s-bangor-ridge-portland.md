# St. Philip's, Bangor Ridge
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Philip's Anglican Church serves Bangor Ridge, a community in the interior hills of Portland parish. Bangor Ridge sits in the elevated hill country of Portland, with a name that preserves a Welsh or Irish colonial connection — Bangor is most famously a cathedral city in Wales. The church has served this interior community for several generations, functioning as a small mission chapel within the Portland deanery network.

## History
Bangor Ridge developed as an interior hill community of Portland, part of the agricultural landscape that extended through the hills of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Philip's as a mission chapel for this community, dedicated to the apostle Philip. The church's history is rooted in the post-Emancipation mission work of the Diocese in Portland's interior communities.

## Architecture
St. Philip's is a small masonry chapel in the elevated hill country of Portland. The building sits in the hilly terrain of Bangor Ridge, in a landscape subject to the heavy rainfall of Portland's interior and the landslide risk that accompanies major storm events in this high-rainfall zone.

## Clergy
Bangor Ridge has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church's regular pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
The name Bangor Ridge is one of several Welsh-associated place names in Jamaica, alongside many Irish and Scottish names, reflecting the diverse origins of the colonial settlers who named estates across the island. Portland's interior hill communities like Bangor Ridge are in some of the highest-rainfall terrain in the Caribbean. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought serious rain and wind impacts to Portland's interior; the Bangor Ridge community was in the affected zone.
