# St. Thomas', Manchioneal
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Thomas' Anglican Church serves Manchioneal, a fishing and farming community at the far eastern end of Portland parish's accessible coast. Manchioneal is a small but historically significant community on the north coast, at the point where Portland's coastal road reaches the edge of the John Crow Mountains wilderness. The church is confirmed at its Manchioneal location by Google Places, with reviewers noting stained glass windows and a serene ambiance in a community of warm and welcoming character.

## History
Manchioneal developed as a coastal community at the eastern extremity of the Portland coastal plain, with the John Crow Mountains pressing close to the sea at this point and limiting further coastal settlement to the east. The Anglican Diocese established St. Thomas' as part of its coastal mission network in Portland, named for the apostle known for his questioning faith. The community's fishing tradition and its position as the last significant settlement before the wilderness of the John Crow Mountains gave it a distinct character within the parish.

## Architecture
St. Thomas' is a coastal church at the eastern end of the Portland coast. Reviewers describe stained glass windows and a serene atmosphere, with the church set in the tranquil surroundings of Manchioneal. The coastal position creates storm surge vulnerability during hurricane passages, and the proximity of the John Crow Mountains means the community also faces heavy rainfall and potential landslide risk from the steep terrain immediately behind the coast.

## Clergy
Manchioneal has been served within the Portland deanery, connected to the Port Antonio circuit. The remote coastal location means the community depends significantly on local leadership to sustain its congregation.

## Notable Facts
Manchioneal sits at the point where Portland's accessible north coast effectively ends, with the John Crow Mountains creating a barrier to further coastal development to the east. The John Crow Mountains are one of Jamaica's most pristine and inaccessible wilderness areas, and Manchioneal is the gateway community to this landscape. Hurricane Ivan (2004) struck this part of the Portland coast with severe force. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) tracked along the north coast; Manchioneal was in the storm's direct path.
