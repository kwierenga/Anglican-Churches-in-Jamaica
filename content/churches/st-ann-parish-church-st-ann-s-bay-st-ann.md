# St. Ann Parish Church, St. Ann's Bay
**St. Ann** · Parish Church · active

St. Ann Parish Church, completed in 1871, is a historic Anglican church in one of Jamaica's oldest settlement areas. It was built after Emancipation in a region deeply shaped by the plantation era, serving communities in transition. The church has withstood over a century of social and environmental changes, including hurricanes. Today, it remains a key spiritual and community center for the parish of St. Ann.

## History
The history of the Anglican faith in St. Ann's Bay dates back to the 18th century, a period when Jamaica's economy was built on slavery. After Emancipation in 1838, the social landscape transformed as freed people established new communities and sought spiritual autonomy. The current St. Ann Parish Church was constructed to meet these changing needs and was completed in September 1871. It quickly became a central institution for the area, providing not only worship but also baptism, education, and community support during the post-slavery era.

## Architecture
St. Ann Parish Church is a solid stone building designed in the 19th-century Gothic Revival style common for important Anglican churches. It features a rectangular nave, a steeply pitched roof to handle tropical rain, and characteristic pointed-arch windows. The interior contains historical memorial tablets from the 1700s and 1800s, some of which make reference to enslaved Africans, offering a tangible link to the area's complex past. The well-kept churchyard and grounds contribute to the site's serene and historic atmosphere.

## Clergy
As the principal Anglican church in the region, St. Ann Parish Church has long been a hub for ministry across the north coast. Its clergy have historically played a vital role in education, overseeing Sunday schools and supporting local primary schools. Following Jamaica's Independence in 1962, the church continued its outreach through the Mothers’ Union, youth groups, and various community programs. This sustained activity has ensured its place as a cornerstone of community life in St. Ann’s Bay.

## Notable Facts
Throughout its long history, the church has endured numerous hurricanes and periods of social change. While specific damage from recent storms like Hurricane Melissa (2025) has not been reported, its sturdy stone construction has proven resilient over time. Today, St. Ann Parish Church remains fully active, hosting weekly services, weddings, funerals, and community events. It stands as a powerful, living monument to faith, connecting Jamaica's history from the era of slavery through Emancipation and into the modern day.
