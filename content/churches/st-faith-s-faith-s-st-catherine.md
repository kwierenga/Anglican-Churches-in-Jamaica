# St. Faith's, Faith's
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Faith's Anglican Church serves the community of Faith's in St. Catherine, a small farming district in the interior of the parish whose very name echoes the church dedication. Faith's is one of those Jamaican communities where the church name and the community name have become synonymous — the settlement taking its identity from the church that anchored it. St. Faith's has served this community for several generations.

## History
Faith's developed as a community in the interior of St. Catherine, and the co-identification of church and community in the name reflects the Anglican church's role as the founding institution of many Jamaican rural settlements. St. Faith — commemorated on 6 October — was a young Roman martyr whose cult was particularly strong in medieval France and England. The dedication here gives this interior community a connection to one of the more unusual saints in the Anglican calendar.

## Architecture
St. Faith's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Faith's has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements. The community's identity with its church creates a particularly tight pastoral relationship.

## Notable Facts
Faith's community name and the church's dedication creating a single identity — 'Faith's' — is a particularly evocative example of how Anglican churches shaped the naming of Jamaican communities. Many Jamaican villages take their name from a church, a spring, an estate, or a natural feature. Faith's belongs to the tradition of spiritually named communities that are common across Jamaica's interior.
