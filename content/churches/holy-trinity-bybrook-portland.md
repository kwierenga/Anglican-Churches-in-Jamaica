# Holy Trinity, Bybrook
**Portland** · Mission · unknown

Holy Trinity Anglican Church serves Bybrook, a community in the interior of Portland parish. Bybrook is a small farming district in the hill country of Portland, and Holy Trinity has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations. The dedication to the Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — is one of the most traditional Anglican church names and is found across Jamaica's diocese.

## History
Bybrook sits in the interior hills of Portland, part of the agricultural landscape of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established Holy Trinity as a mission chapel for this community, with the Trinity dedication reflecting the central doctrinal commitment of Anglican worship. The church has served the Bybrook community through the transformations of Portland's interior economy.

## Architecture
Holy Trinity is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Portland. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of Portland. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Bybrook has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church's pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
The Trinity dedication at Bybrook connects this small interior chapel to one of the oldest strands in Christian architecture — Trinity churches have been built since the early medieval period as expressions of the central Christian doctrine of the triune God. Portland's interior communities like Bybrook maintain a consistent Anglican presence through their lay leadership even when clerical visits are infrequent.
