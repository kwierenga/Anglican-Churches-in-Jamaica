# St. Margaret's, Middle Quarters
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Margaret's Anglican Church serves the vibrant fishing and farming community of Middle Quarters in southwest St. Elizabeth, situated between the iconic Holland Bamboo Avenue and the historic town of Black River. The church falls under the St. Thomas' Cure in Lacovia within the Diocese of Jamaica's Montego Bay Region, with all administrative and pastoral oversight coordinated through the Lacovia rectory. Located in low-lying terrain near the Black River Morass, the chapel continues the Anglican tradition of serving rural settlements in this agriculturally rich area. Recent events, including Hurricane Melissa's severe impact on the Holland Bamboo corridor and Black River in 2025, highlight the environmental vulnerability of both the community and its religious infrastructure.

## History
Middle Quarters has evolved as a distinctive settlement along the A2 highway, nationally celebrated as Jamaica's premier 'pepper shrimp' destination while serving as the eastern gateway to the magnificent Holland Bamboo Avenue. Within the Anglican diocesan structure, St. Margaret's (sometimes recorded as Middlesex/Middle Quarters) appears consistently among St. Elizabeth churches attached to the St. Thomas' Cure in Lacovia, reflecting generations of pastoral care extending from this central parish church. This connection underscores the historical pattern of Anglican mission work connecting larger parish centers with smaller rural communities across St. Elizabeth's southern plains.

## Clergy
St. Margaret's operates within the well-established diocesan framework that links smaller mission churches to larger parish centers. Current diocesan directories clearly position St. Margaret's - Middlesex/Middle Quarters under the St. Thomas' (Lacovia) Cure within the Montego Bay Region, with all clergy assignments, service schedules, and administrative functions managed through the Lacovia rectory. This organizational structure, reiterated across multiple diocesan publications, ensures pastoral continuity while maximizing limited clergy resources across the geographically dispersed St. Elizabeth parishes.

## Notable Facts
The church exists within a uniquely Jamaican cultural landscape, with Middle Quarters famed nationwide as the 'Peppa Shrimp' capital where roadside vendors create an iconic culinary experience for travelers. Immediately adjacent to the community lies the breathtaking Holland Bamboo Avenue, the historic canopy road between Lacovia and Middle Quarters that forms part of the church's immediate travel corridor and visual environment. Hurricane Melissa's catastrophic impact in 2025 caused severe regional disruption, including the prolonged blockage of Holland Bamboo Avenue and catastrophic flooding in Black River - events that undoubtedly affected St. Margaret's operations, access, and congregation while highlighting the climate vulnerability of this low-lying coastal region.
