# St. George's, Point Hill
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. George's Anglican Church serves Point Hill, a community in the interior of St. Catherine parish in the hill country. Point Hill is a small farming district, and the church has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The area around Point Hill is in the interior hill country of St. Catherine, distinct from the Point Hill stub entry at different coordinates.

## History
Point Hill sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country of the western part of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. George's to serve this community as part of its interior mission network. The church has served the Point Hill farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. George's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the hilly interior of St. Catherine. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.

## Clergy
Point Hill has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Point Hill's position in the interior hill country of St. Catherine reflects the pattern of Anglican mission churches established across the hill communities of the interior parishes in the post-Emancipation period. St. George's feast day (23 April) is one of the most celebrated in the Anglican calendar across the English-speaking world.
