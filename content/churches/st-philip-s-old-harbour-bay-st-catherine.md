# St. Christopher's, Old Harbour Bay
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Christopher's Anglican Church serves Old Harbour Bay, a fishing and coastal community on the south coast of St. Catherine, distinct from Old Harbour town further inland. Old Harbour Bay is a fishing village at the waterfront of the Old Harbour area, with a character shaped by its direct relationship with the sea. The dedication to St. Christopher, patron of travellers, is fitting for a coastal community where fishing voyages are part of daily life.

## History
Old Harbour Bay developed as a fishing community on the south coast of St. Catherine, at the actual bay from which Old Harbour takes its name. The community has maintained a fishing tradition alongside the agricultural economy of the surrounding area. The Anglican Diocese established St. Christopher's to serve this waterfront community.

## Architecture

## Clergy
Old Harbour Bay has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements, linked to the Old Harbour circuit.

## Notable Facts
Old Harbour Bay is the fishing village at the actual harbour that gave Old Harbour town its name. The separation of the town (further inland) from its associated bay community is a common pattern on Jamaica's south coast, where settlements developed both at the waterfront and slightly inland. St. Christopher's patron — the great saint of travellers and those who cross dangerous waters — carries a particular resonance for a fishing community whose members venture onto the Caribbean Sea.
