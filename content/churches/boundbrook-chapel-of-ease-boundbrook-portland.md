# Boundbrook Chapel-of-Ease, Boundbrook
**Portland** · Chapel · active

Boundbrook Chapel-of-Ease serves the Boundbrook community of Portland, a district adjacent to Port Antonio on the western edge of the parish capital's urban area. A Chapel-of-Ease is an Anglican church built to serve a community at some distance from the parish church, providing a more accessible place of worship without full parish status. Boundbrook's Chapel-of-Ease has served the residents of this Port Antonio suburb for well over a century, linked to Christ Church parish church as its parent congregation.

## History
Boundbrook developed as a residential and commercial area on the western approaches to Port Antonio, growing as the town expanded along the coastal plain. The Anglican Diocese established the Chapel-of-Ease to serve Boundbrook's population who would otherwise have had to travel to the parish church for worship. Chapels-of-Ease were a common feature of the Diocese's parish system in Jamaica, providing local accessibility while maintaining the administrative structure of the larger parish. Boundbrook's chapel has served the community through the growth and decline of Port Antonio's banana-trade prosperity.

## Architecture
The Boundbrook Chapel-of-Ease is a modest Anglican chapel in the coastal community west of Port Antonio town centre. Its position near the coast exposes it to storm surge during hurricane passages, and the flat coastal plain of Boundbrook is particularly vulnerable to flooding during major rainfall events.

## Clergy
As a Chapel-of-Ease, Boundbrook has been served by the clergy of Christ Church parish, Port Antonio, without independent parish status. The chapel's pastoral life is closely linked to the parish church and its deanery circuit.

## Notable Facts
The Chapel-of-Ease designation reflects a medieval English ecclesiastical tradition that was transplanted throughout the British colonial church system. In Jamaica, many communities that lacked full parish status were served by Chapels-of-Ease that provided regular worship within a larger parish structure. Boundbrook's chapel is one of several such chapels in the Portland deanery. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) struck Port Antonio and its immediate surroundings directly; Boundbrook's coastal position made it particularly vulnerable to surge and flooding.
