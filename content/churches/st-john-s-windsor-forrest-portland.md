# St. John's, Windsor Forrest
**Portland** · Church · active

St. John's Anglican Church serves Windsor Forest (recorded in the dataset as Windsor Forrest), a community in the interior of Portland parish. Windsor Forest is a small farming community in the hilly interior west of Port Antonio, in the agricultural landscape between the coast and the higher hill country. St. John's has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations.

## History
Windsor Forest developed as an interior agricultural community in Portland, its royal English name reflecting the colonial estate-naming tradition of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. John's as a mission chapel in this community, dedicated to the apostle and evangelist. The church has served successive generations of the Windsor Forest farming community through the transformations of Portland's agricultural economy.

## Architecture
St. John's is a small masonry chapel in the interior hills of Portland. The building is set in the Windsor Forest community, in a hilly landscape subject to the heavy rainfall that Portland's interior receives from the orographic effect of the Blue Mountains. Landslide and road-closure risk are recurring challenges during hurricane passages.

## Clergy
Windsor Forest has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church's pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
The name Windsor Forest links this community to one of England's most famous royal landscapes, though the Portland interior's dense tropical vegetation and steep topography could hardly be more different from the Windsor Great Park. This transplanting of English landscape names is a common feature of Jamaica's human geography. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind impacts to interior Portland; Windsor Forest was in the affected zone.
