# St. Matthew's, Mount Herman
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Mount Herman, a community in the interior hills of Portland parish. Mount Herman is a hill community in western Portland, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations. The dedication to St. Matthew the Evangelist is shared with several other Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Mount Herman sits in the interior hill country of Portland, a community in the higher ground of the western part of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew's as a mission chapel to serve this hill community. The name Mount Herman echoes the biblical Mount Hermon in a tradition of biblical naming found in Jamaica's interior communities.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a small masonry chapel in the interior hills of Portland. The elevated hill position creates wind exposure during hurricane passages and sits in the high-rainfall zone of Portland's interior, with associated landslide and road-closure risk.

## Clergy
Mount Herman has been served within the Portland deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church's pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
Mount Herman's biblical name places it in a tradition of religious naming found across Jamaica's interior communities — names like Mount Zion, Mount Sinai, and Mount Moriah appear throughout the island, often reflecting the deep biblical literacy of post-Emancipation Jamaican communities. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to Portland's interior; the Mount Herman community was in the affected zone.
