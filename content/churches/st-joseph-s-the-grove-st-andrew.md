# St. Joseph's, The Grove
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

St. Joseph's Anglican Church serves The Grove, a community in St. Andrew in the residential zone east of Half-Way-Tree and near Liguanea. The Grove is a neighbourhood in the middle-class residential area of eastern St. Andrew, and St. Joseph's has served its community for several generations. The dedication to St. Joseph, the foster father of Jesus, is relatively uncommon among Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
The Grove developed as part of the residential expansion of eastern St. Andrew in the 20th century, in the upmarket suburban zone between Half-Way-Tree and the University of the West Indies at Mona. The Anglican Diocese established St. Joseph's to serve this growing residential community.

## Architecture
St. Joseph's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Andrew in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Andrew. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
The Grove has been served within the eastern St. Andrew deanery circuit, connected to the Liguanea and Mona ministry cluster.

## Notable Facts
The Grove area of St. Andrew sits in the broader Liguanea Plain, a residential zone that extends from Half-Way-Tree eastward to the foothills of the Blue Mountains. This area has developed as one of Kingston's more prosperous residential districts, home to professional and business families, and the Anglican churches here serve a congregation with a middle-class suburban character.
