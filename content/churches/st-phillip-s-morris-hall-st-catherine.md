# St. Phillip's, Morris Hall
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Philip's Anglican Church serves Morris Hall, a community in the hill country of interior St. Catherine. Morris Hall is a small farming district, and St. Philip's has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The dedication to St. Philip the Apostle — who brought Nathanael to Christ and asked Jesus to 'show us the Father' — is found in several Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Morris Hall sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country between the main road communities and the upper reaches of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established St. Philip's to serve this community as part of its comprehensive mission coverage of the interior. The church has served the Morris Hall farming community through the transformations of the parish's agricultural economy.

## Architecture
St. Phillip's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Morris Hall has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Morris Hall's name preserves the memory of a colonial estate proprietor — a common naming convention in Jamaica's interior communities where plantation estates became the nucleus of post-Emancipation settlements. St. Philip's feast day (1 May, shared with James the Less) is one of the Apostles' days in the Anglican calendar.
