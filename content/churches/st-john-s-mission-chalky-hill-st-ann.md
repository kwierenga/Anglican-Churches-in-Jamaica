# St. John's Mission, Chalky Hill
**St. Ann** · Mission · active

St. John’s Mission serves the community of Chalky Hill, a rural district in St. Ann in the hilly interior south of Ocho Rios. Chalky Hill is a small agricultural community in the ridge country that characterises the interior of St. Ann between the north-coast tourist corridor and the southern agricultural plain. St. John’s was established as an Anglican mission chapel to serve this dispersed hill community, providing worship, pastoral care, and a centre for community life in a quiet corner of the parish.

## History
The Chalky Hill district sits in the hill country of interior St. Ann, an area shaped by the post-Emancipation settlement of freed families who moved into the interior highlands after 1838 and established small farms on lands that had previously been part of the plantation system. The Anglican Diocese extended its mission network into these interior communities, and St. John’s was planted as a mission chapel to serve Chalky Hill and the surrounding district. As a mission rather than a parish church, it has always operated within the ministry circuit of the larger St. Ann congregations, with its life sustained largely by dedicated lay leadership.

## Architecture
St. John’s Mission is a small chapel of simple construction typical of Anglican mission buildings in the St. Ann interior. The building is set in the hilly terrain of Chalky Hill, in a landscape of small farms and wooded ridges. The interior hill position gives the mission some shelter from direct coastal storm surge, but the ridge country of interior St. Ann is subject to landslides and road closures during heavy rainfall events associated with tropical storms and hurricanes.

## Clergy
As a mission chapel, St. John’s Chalky Hill has been served within the St. Ann’s Bay or Ocho Rios clergy cluster of the Diocese, grouped with other interior and coastal St. Ann congregations under shared ministry arrangements. The mission’s pastoral life has depended heavily on lay leaders — wardens, Mothers’ Union members, and readers — who maintain regular worship between clerical visits. The church is confirmed active in diocesan records.

## Notable Facts
The Chalky Hill area of St. Ann is part of the interior ridge landscape that lies between the tourist-facing north coast and the quieter agricultural south of the parish. Communities like Chalky Hill represent a St. Ann largely invisible to visitors who come for the beaches and attractions of Ocho Rios, yet these interior hill communities have deep roots in the post-Emancipation history of the parish and a long-standing connection to the Anglican Diocese’s mission network. Hurricane activity has periodically disrupted interior St. Ann, with landslides and road damage isolating hill communities. Hurricane Gilbert (1988) caused serious damage across the parish interior. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to interior St. Ann; Chalky Hill and the mission congregation were in the affected zone.
