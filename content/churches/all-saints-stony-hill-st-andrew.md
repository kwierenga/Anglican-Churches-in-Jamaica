# All Saints', Stony Hill
**St. Andrew** · Church · active

This All Saints' entry provides a third Anglican worship point associated with the Stony Hill area of northern St. Andrew, The Stony Hill community's size and the surrounding hill-country settlements create demand for multiple Anglican worship points in the area. All Saints' may serve a specific neighbourhood of Stony Hill or a nearby hill community accessible from the Stony Hill road.

## History
The Stony Hill area supports multiple Anglican churches reflecting both the town's own population and the dispersed hill communities surrounding it. All Saints' adds a third worship point to the Stony Hill cluster, serving its particular community within the broader hill-country network.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Andrew. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the hilly interior of St. Andrew. The elevated terrain channels wind during storm passages and makes good drainage essential to the building's longevity.
