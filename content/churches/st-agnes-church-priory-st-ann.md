# St. Agnes', Priory
**St. Ann** · Church · active

St. Agnes Anglican Church is a mission church in the seaside village of Priory, St. Ann, situated near the historic sites of Seville Heritage Park and Drax Hall. The church is part of the St. Ann's Bay Cure, serving a community that originated as a post-Emancipation free village for formerly enslaved people. Today, St. Agnes continues its role as a small but active spiritual centre for local families, hosting regular worship and community gatherings.

## History
St. Agnes Anglican Church is rooted in the transformative post-Emancipation era. The village of Priory was established on land purchased from the Seville estate by Protestant reformers to create a free settlement for formerly enslaved individuals. As this community grew into a permanent village, St. Agnes was founded as a mission of the St. Ann Parish Church in St. Ann's Bay. Historical records note that the St. Agnes Mission was restored during the 20th century, linking it directly to the long-term development of the Anglican Church in St. Ann from the colonial period into modern Jamaica.

## Architecture
While no formal architectural survey is available, St. Agnes is consistently listed in directories as a mission church on the Priory to Bamboo Road. Visual evidence from drive-through videos and photo sites depicts a modest, rectangular chapel built from masonry or blockwork. Its design, featuring a pitched roof, plain windows, and a low tower or porch, is characteristic of late 19th and early 20th-century Anglican mission churches in rural Jamaica. The church is set just inland from the coast, near Priory Primary and Infant School, with its location offering a blend of sea breezes and hillside winds.

## Clergy
As a mission within the St. Ann's Bay Cure, St. Agnes is served by the same rector and team that minister to St. Ann Parish Church and other outstations. The congregation benefits from active pastoral care, as evidenced by its inclusion in weekly parish intercessions. Funeral notices confirm the church's role in serving residents of Priory and surrounding areas, with burials often conducted at the St. Ann's Bay cemetery. The week-to-week liturgical and community life of the church is sustained by lay leaders, including wardens, readers, and Mothers’ Union members, who maintain regular worship and activities.

## Notable Facts
St. Agnes occupies a uniquely historic landscape. It is located near the Seville Heritage Park, which encompasses the ruins of the first Spanish settlement and later British sugar works. The church itself serves the community established on part of this estate after Emancipation. This places St. Agnes at the confluence of Jamaica's layered history, connecting the era of enslavement at Drax Hall and Seville with the post-Emancipation free villages and modern residential developments. The church's role as a respected local institution is underscored by its use for high-profile funerals of notable figures from the north coast's business and tourism sectors.
