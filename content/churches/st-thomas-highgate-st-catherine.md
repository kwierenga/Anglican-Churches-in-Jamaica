# St. Thomas', Highgate (Sligoville area)
**St. Catherine** · Church · unknown

St. Thomas' Anglican Church serves Highgate in the Sligoville area of St. Catherine, a community in the northern interior of the parish. This Highgate in St. Catherine should not be confused with the Highgate in St. Mary parish. The church has served this interior hill community for several generations.

## History
This Highgate community in the Sligoville area of St. Catherine sits in the northern interior of the parish, in the hill country near Sligoville. The Anglican Diocese established St. Thomas' to serve this community, named for the doubting apostle. The church has served the local farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Thomas' is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Catherine. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
This Highgate community has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements, connected to the Sligoville circuit.

## Notable Facts
The existence of Highgate communities in both St. Catherine and St. Mary is a reminder that Jamaican place names were frequently duplicated across different parishes — the same estate name or geographic description appearing independently in multiple locations. The Sligoville-area Highgate has its own distinct history rooted in the free village tradition of northern St. Catherine.
