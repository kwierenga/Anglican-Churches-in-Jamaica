# St. Michael's, Belfield
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Michael’s Anglican Church serves Belfield, a community in the interior of St. Mary parish in the hill country between the coastal towns and the southern parish boundary. Belfield is an agricultural district, and St. Michael’s has provided Anglican ministry to the local farming families for several generations, functioning as a small mission chapel within the St. Mary deanery.

## History
Belfield sits in the interior hill country of St. Mary, in the agricultural landscape that characterises the central parts of the parish away from the north coast. The Anglican Diocese established St. Michael’s as part of its mission network across the interior communities of St. Mary. The church has served the Belfield community through the transformations of the parish’s agricultural economy across the post-Emancipation era.

## Architecture
St. Michael’s is a small masonry chapel typical of Anglican interior churches in St. Mary. The building sits in the hilly terrain of the Belfield district, subject to the heavy rainfall that characterises interior St. Mary. The church’s construction is functional and durable, suited to a small rural congregation in a rainfall-intensive environment.

## Clergy
Belfield has been served within the St. Mary deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain regular worship between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
Belfield is one of many small interior communities of St. Mary whose history and character are shaped by the post-Emancipation agricultural landscape. The area’s heavy rainfall and hilly terrain mean road access can be disrupted during major storm events. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought rain and wind impacts to interior St. Mary; the Belfield community and congregation were in the affected zone.
