# St. Paul's, Spalding
**Manchester** · Church · active

St. Paul's Anglican Church serves Spalding, a community in the northern interior of Manchester parish near the Christiana area. Spalding is a farming community in the highland hills of Manchester, and the church has served the local community for several generations. Manchester's northern interior, of which Spalding is part, is among the highest-elevation farming landscapes of central Jamaica.

## History
Spalding sits in the northern interior of Manchester, in the highland farming communities above the main Mandeville plateau. The Anglican Diocese established St. Paul's to serve this community. The church has maintained an Anglican presence in this elevated farming community.

## Architecture
St. Paul's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Manchester in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Manchester. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Spalding has been served within the Manchester deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Spalding is one of the higher-elevation communities in Manchester's northern interior, in a landscape of cool-climate farming. The area around Spalding and Christiana is known for producing vegetables including Irish potatoes that thrive in the cooler highland temperatures. The apostle Paul's missionary journeys across the Mediterranean world have made his dedication a popular choice for churches in communities that see themselves as outposts of the faith in the wider landscape.
