# St. Silas', Mt. Airy
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Silas' Anglican Church serves the community of Mt. Airy in Westmoreland. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory under the Westmoreland Deanery; no substantive foundational or architectural history has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed, and the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica — indicating a post-1891 foundation, most likely during Bishop Enos Nuttall's rural expansion of mission stations.

The dedication is to Silas (also called Silvanus), the early Christian missionary and companion of the Apostle Paul — his name appears in the Acts of the Apostles and in the Pauline epistles. A dedication to a Pauline companion rather than to an apostle or major saint is characteristic of the smaller mission-station foundations of the later nineteenth century.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Westmoreland communities experienced destructive winds and heavy rain. The specific status of St. Silas' Mt. Airy after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
