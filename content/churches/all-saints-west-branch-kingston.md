# All Saints' (West Branch), Kingston (West Branch)
**Kingston** · Mission · unknown

All Saints' (West Branch) is an Anglican worship point in the King Street area of downtown Kingston, in the immediate vicinity of Kingston Parish Church. The 'West Branch' designation suggests this is a branch congregation or mission chapel operating as part of the Kingston Parish Church ministry, providing additional Anglican services for the western part of the parish church's catchment area in downtown Kingston.

## History
West Branch missions and branch congregations were a common feature of larger urban parishes in the Anglican Diocese, allowing the parish church to extend its pastoral reach beyond a single building. The All Saints' West Branch in the King Street area of Kingston operates within the administrative structure of Kingston Parish Church, serving the immediate downtown community alongside the main parish church.

## Architecture
All Saints' (West Branch) is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Kingston. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the coastal landscape of Kingston. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
The West Branch has been served by the clergy of Kingston Parish Church, operating as part of the same parish ministry structure.

## Notable Facts
Branch congregations in urban Kingston reflect the Diocese's approach to ensuring Anglican worship is accessible across the city's various neighbourhoods and communities. Downtown Kingston's concentration of Anglican churches — the Parish Church, St. George's, St. Michael's & All Angels, and branch congregations — reflects the area's historical importance as the colonial capital and commercial heart of the island.
