# St. Luke's, Comfort Castle
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Luke's Anglican Church serves Comfort Castle, a community in the Rio Grande Valley of Portland near Moore Town. The Comfort Castle area lies in the valley of the Rio Grande, one of Jamaica's most dramatic rivers, in a landscape of extraordinary natural beauty and extraordinary rainfall. The church is confirmed at its location in the valley, with a reviewer noting it is 'nestled within the lush landscapes of the Rio Grande Valley' and describing it as a spiritual haven reflecting the rich history and cultural heritage of the area.

## History
Comfort Castle sits in the Rio Grande Valley of Portland, in the mountain country south of Port Antonio. The valley is one of the most spectacular and isolated in Jamaica, with the Rio Grande cutting through the Blue Mountains and John Crow Mountains. The Anglican Diocese established St. Luke's to serve the Comfort Castle community, dedicated to the physician apostle and evangelist. The church has served successive generations of the valley community through the challenges of life in one of Jamaica's most remote inhabited landscapes.

The valley position creates compound flood risk — the Rio Grande is subject to flash flooding during the extraordinary rainfall events that Portland's interior receives — and the surrounding mountain slopes create landslide risk.

## Clergy
Comfort Castle has been served within the Portland deanery. The remote valley location means the church depends significantly on lay leaders for its regular pastoral life, with the difficult road access making regular clerical visits challenging.

## Notable Facts
The Rio Grande Valley is one of Jamaica's premier nature tourism destinations, with bamboo rafting on the Rio Grande offered as a visitor experience from Port Antonio since the 1940s — a tradition associated with Errol Flynn, who popularised the river rafting trip. Comfort Castle and the valley communities live alongside this tourism economy while maintaining their own deep-rooted agricultural and community traditions. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought extraordinary rainfall to the Rio Grande Valley; Comfort Castle was in the severely affected zone, with flooding and landslide risk across the valley.
