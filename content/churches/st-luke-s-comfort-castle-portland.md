# St. Luke's, Comfort Castle
**Portland** · Church · active

St. Luke's Anglican Church serves **Comfort Castle**, a community in the **Rio Grande Valley** of Portland near Moore Town. The Comfort Castle area lies in one of Jamaica's most dramatic river valleys, in a landscape of extraordinary natural beauty and extraordinary rainfall. A reviewer notes the church is "nestled within the lush landscapes of the Rio Grande Valley."

## History
**English rule (1655) and the founding of Portland (1723).** Under English rule from **1655**, the future Portland coast remained largely Maroon territory. **Portland parish was created in 1723** by order of the then Governor, **Henry Bentinck, 1st Duke of Portland** (Governor 1722–1726), and named in his honour. The British built **Fort George** at Port Antonio in 1729.

**The First Maroon War (1728–1739) and Nanny — and the Rio Grande valley.** The **Windward Maroons** under **Quao** and **Nanny** controlled the Blue Mountains and John Crow Mountains, with the **Rio Grande valley** as the heartland of their territory. The **1740 treaty** granted **Nanny a 500-acre land grant** that forms the present site of **Moore Town**, just up the Rio Grande from Comfort Castle. **Nanny of the Maroons** became Jamaica's only female National Hero in October 1975.

**Slavery and the planter church.** Sugar estates dominated the fertile coastal lowlands; coffee thrived in the hills. Until **1870**, the Church of England in Jamaica was the established state church, supported by colonial government funds.

**Sam Sharpe (1831), Emancipation (1838).** During the **1831 Sam Sharpe (Christmas) Rebellion**, planters and Anglican clergy formed the **Colonial Church Union** to "wipe non-conformist preachers off the face of the earth"; **Sam Sharpe** was hanged in Montego Bay on **23 May 1832** and is today a National Hero of Jamaica. Emancipation came in 1834, with full freedom on **1 August 1838**. **Christ Church Port Antonio** was completed around **1840** under Bishop **Christopher Lipscomb**.

**Disestablishment (1870), Nuttall, and the banana revolution.** After the Morant Bay Rebellion of 1865 and Crown Colony rule (1866), the Anglican Church was disestablished in **1870**. Bishop **Enos Nuttall** (1880–1916) extended the rural mission network from 26 stations in 1880 to **122 by 1911**. In **1870**, **Captain Lorenzo Dow Baker** purchased bananas in Port Antonio market; Baker's Boston Fruit Company became **United Fruit Company in 1899**, and the Rio Grande valley became a major banana-producing zone.

**Foundation of St. Luke's.** The Anglican Diocese established St. Luke's to serve the Comfort Castle community, dedicated to the physician apostle and evangelist. The church has served successive generations of the valley community through the challenges of life in one of Jamaica's most remote inhabited landscapes.

**Twentieth century, Errol Flynn, and Independence.** The Rio Grande Valley is one of Jamaica's premier nature tourism destinations, with **bamboo rafting** on the Rio Grande offered as a visitor experience from Port Antonio since the 1940s — a tradition associated with **Errol Flynn**, who popularised the river rafting trip from Navy Island. The **1903 hurricane** had earlier damaged Christ Church Port Antonio and devastated the banana industry. Jamaica's independence on **6 August 1962** found St. Luke's a settled valley mission.

**Hurricane Melissa (October 2025).** Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on **28 October 2025** — the strongest hurricane to strike Jamaica in recorded meteorological history. Hurricane Melissa brought extraordinary rainfall to the Rio Grande Valley; Comfort Castle was in the severely affected zone, with flooding and landslide risk across the valley.

## Architecture
St. Luke's is confirmed by Google Places. A reviewer describes the church as situated within the lush landscapes of the Rio Grande Valley, speaking to both the extraordinary natural setting and the isolation of the congregation. The valley position creates compound flood risk — the **Rio Grande** is subject to flash flooding during the extraordinary rainfall events that Portland's interior receives — and the surrounding mountain slopes create landslide risk.

## Clergy
Comfort Castle has been served within the **Portland deanery**. The remote valley location means the church depends significantly on lay leaders for its regular pastoral life, with the difficult road access making regular clerical visits challenging.

## Notable Facts
The dedication to **St. Luke** — physician and evangelist, whose feast day is **18 October** — falls in the calmer period after hurricane season and is a common Anglican choice for communities that have endured storm damage. Comfort Castle and the Rio Grande valley communities live alongside **Errol Flynn's bamboo-rafting** tourism economy while maintaining their own deep-rooted agricultural and Maroon-adjacent community traditions.
