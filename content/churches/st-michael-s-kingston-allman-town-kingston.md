# St. Michael's, Kingston (Allman Town)
**Kingston** · Church · unknown

St. Michael's Anglican Church serves the Allman Town community of Kingston, a residential neighbourhood in the lower St. Andrew/Kingston zone north of the downtown area. Allman Town is one of Kingston's older established residential communities, and St. Michael's provides Anglican ministry in this urban neighbourhood distinct from the confirmed St. Matthew's Anglican also recorded in the Allman Town area.

## History
Allman Town developed as a residential neighbourhood in the expansion of Kingston northward from the original colonial waterfront settlement. The Anglican Diocese established St. Michael's to serve this urban community, providing a local place of worship in a neighbourhood that has been part of Kingston's residential fabric for well over a century.

## Architecture
St. Michael's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in Kingston. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the coastal landscape of Kingston. Its coastal setting brings exposure to salt air and periodic storm surge, demanding robust masonry construction.

## Clergy
Allman Town has been served within the Kingston deanery under shared clergy arrangements, linked to the downtown Kingston circuit.

## Notable Facts
Allman Town is one of several historic residential neighbourhoods of inner Kingston that developed in the post-colonial period. The area has maintained its residential character alongside the commercial development of the surrounding city. Two Anglican churches are recorded in the Allman Town area — St. Michael's and St. Matthew's — reflecting the neighbourhood's size and the Diocese's layered urban ministry approach.
