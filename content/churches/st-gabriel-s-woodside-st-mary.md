# St. Gabriel's, Woodside
**St. Mary** · Church · active

St. Gabriel’s Anglican Church serves Woodside, a community in the south-eastern interior of St. Mary parish, in the hill country approaching the border with St. Thomas. Woodside is a small farming district, and St. Gabriel’s has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations. The dedication to the archangel Gabriel, messenger of divine announcement, gives this mission chapel a celestial patron shared with few other Jamaican Anglican churches.

## History
Woodside sits in the south-eastern interior of St. Mary, in the hilly transition zone between the parish’s agricultural interior and the St. Thomas border country. The area developed from the post-Emancipation settlement of freed families in the interior highlands, and the Anglican Diocese established St. Gabriel’s as part of its mission network. The church has served successive generations of the Woodside farming community.

## Architecture
St. Gabriel’s is a small masonry chapel in the south-eastern hills of St. Mary. The building sits in a hilly landscape of moderate elevation, subject to the heavy rainfall characteristic of this part of the island and to wind exposure during hurricane passages. The church’s construction is suited to a small rural congregation in a demanding physical environment.

## Clergy
Woodside has been served within the southern St. Mary circuit of the Diocese deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church’s pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
Woodside’s south-eastern position in St. Mary places it in one of the more remote parts of the parish, connecting it geographically more closely to the St. Thomas hill country than to the north-coast towns that characterise St. Mary’s public face. Hurricane impacts in this part of Jamaica tend to come from the south-east track of storms approaching from the Atlantic, creating a different risk profile from the north-coast communities. Hurricane Melissa (October 2025) brought heavy rain and wind to southern St. Mary; the Woodside community and congregation were in the affected zone.
