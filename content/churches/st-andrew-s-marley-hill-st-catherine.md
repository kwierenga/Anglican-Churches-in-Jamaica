# St. Andrew's, Marley Hill
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Andrew's Anglican Church serves Marley Hill, a community in the interior of St. Catherine parish in the hill country north of Spanish Town. Marley Hill is a farming community in the elevated terrain of the parish, and the church has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations.

## History
Marley Hill sits in the northern interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country between the Bog Walk corridor and the eastern hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Andrew's to serve this community. The name Marley Hill preserves the memory of a colonial estate with an English county name.

## Architecture
St. Andrew's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Catherine. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Marley Hill has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Marley Hill's name, like many Jamaican estate-derived community names, preserves a connection to the English county of Somerset. The transplanting of English county and landscape names to Jamaican estates was a common practice among colonial proprietors who named their properties after their home regions.
