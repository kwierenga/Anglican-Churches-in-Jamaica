# St. Bartholomew's, Woodside
**St. Catherine** · Mission · unknown

St. Bartholomew's Anglican Church serves Woodside in the interior of St. Catherine parish, a community in the high hill country of the western part of the parish near the Clarendon and Manchester borders. Woodside is a farming community in elevated terrain, and the church has served this community for several generations.

## History
Woodside sits in the high interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country that approaches the central ridge of Jamaica near the Clarendon and Manchester boundaries. The Anglican Diocese established St. Bartholomew's to serve this remote hill community. The church has served the Woodside farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
St. Bartholomew's is a masonry church of modest proportions, representative of Anglican rural church building in St. Catherine. The structure balances practicality with the dignity appropriate to a place of worship, featuring a straightforward nave with local stone or concrete block construction typical of Jamaica's interior church-building tradition. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Woodside has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements. The remote elevated location means the church depends significantly on lay leaders for its regular pastoral life.

## Notable Facts
Woodside's high elevation in the interior of St. Catherine places it in one of the cooler and more remote farming landscapes of the parish. The proximity to the Clarendon and Manchester borders means this community has connections to multiple parish traditions. St. Bartholomew's feast day (24 August) falls in the height of hurricane season.
