# St. Matthias, Kentucky
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Matthias Anglican Church serves the community of Kentucky, an interior district of Westmoreland south-east of Petersfield. The church is listed in the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands directory under the Westmoreland Deanery. No substantive historical information about its foundation, architecture, or clergy has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed, and the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica's Anglican returns — indicating that St. Matthias Kentucky was established after 1891, likely during Bishop Enos Nuttall's great expansion of rural mission stations (the Diocese grew from 26 rural stations in 1880 to 122 by 1911).

The dedication is to Matthias, the apostle chosen by lot to replace Judas Iscariot after the Resurrection (Acts 1:15–26). His feast day is 14 May.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made Category 5 landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025. Interior communities across Westmoreland experienced destructive winds, and the Diocese reported some 50 church buildings destroyed or significantly damaged. The specific status of St. Matthias Kentucky after Melissa has not been separately reported in public sources reviewed.
