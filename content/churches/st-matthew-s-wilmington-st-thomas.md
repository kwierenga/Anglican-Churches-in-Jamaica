# St. Matthew's, Wilmington
**St. Thomas** · Church · active

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Wilmington, a community in St. Thomas parish.

## History
Wilmington is one of the small communities of St. Thomas, in the parish whose colonial-era plantation landscape stretched from the south coast at Morant Bay up into the Blue Mountain foothills and the John Crow Mountain slopes. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew's as a small mission chapel to serve the Wilmington community as part of its rural network across St. Thomas. The wider parish's nineteenth-century history — including the 1865 Morant Bay Rebellion led by Paul Bogle and the subsequent constitutional re-organisation of the Jamaican parish system in 1867 — shaped the social context within which the diocese expanded its mission stations across the parish in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

The dedication to St. Matthew the Evangelist — apostle, former tax collector, and traditional author of the first Gospel, with a feast day on 21 September — is a common Anglican choice across the diocese.

No substantive foundational date or detailed architectural history specifically for St. Matthew's Wilmington has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed.

## Architecture
St. Matthew's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Thomas interior.

## Clergy
St. Matthew's Wilmington is served within the Diocese's Eastern Jamaica Region.
