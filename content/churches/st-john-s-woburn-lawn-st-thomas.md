# St. John's, Woburn Lawn
**St. Thomas** · Church · active

St. John's Anglican Church serves Woburn Lawn, a community in St. Thomas parish. Woburn Lawn is a small farming district in the interior of St. Thomas, and the church has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations. The dedication to St. John the Evangelist is one of the most common Anglican dedications in Jamaica.

## History
Woburn Lawn sits in the interior of St. Thomas parish, away from the coastal communities that characterise much of the parish's settled landscape. The Anglican Diocese established St. John's as a mission chapel to serve this interior community in the post-Emancipation period. The church has served successive generations of Woburn Lawn's farming community through the transformations of St. Thomas's agricultural economy.

## Architecture
St. John's is a small masonry chapel in the interior of St. Thomas. The building sits in a farming landscape away from the direct coastal exposure of the south-coast parishes, though St. Thomas's interior is subject to the heavy rainfall associated with the Blue Mountains to the north.

## Clergy
Woburn Lawn has been served within the St. Thomas deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Lay leaders sustain the church's pastoral life between clerical visits.

## Notable Facts
St. Thomas parish stretches from the south coast north to the Blue Mountains, creating a diverse landscape from coastal sugar estates to mountain farming communities. Woburn Lawn represents the interior agricultural landscape of the parish. Hurricane Ivan (2004) caused widespread damage across St. Thomas, affecting both coastal and interior communities.
