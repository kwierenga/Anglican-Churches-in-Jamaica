# St. Peter's, Petersfield
**Westmoreland** · Church · active

St. Peter's Anglican Church is the principal parish church of the Petersfield–Darliston Cure in the Westmoreland Deanery of the Diocese of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands. Built in 1843, it stands at the centre of Petersfield, a large interior community on the road between Savanna-la-Mar and Montego Bay. It was severely damaged by Hurricane Melissa on 28 October 2025 — only the walls remain standing — and the congregation currently worships in Petersfield Primary and Infant School under a tarpaulin.

## History
Westmoreland was one of the largest and most productive sugar parishes of the British Caribbean, its plain intensively cultivated by an enslaved labour force. Until 1870 the Church of England in Jamaica was the established state church, supported by colonial government funds, with St. George's at Savanna-la-Mar serving as the mother church of the parish. St. Peter's was built in 1843 — five years after full Emancipation on 1 August 1838 — to serve the inland communities of freed people and the small settlements that had grown up across the Westmoreland interior. It has been a mission centre for the surrounding rural parish ever since.

Since 1979 the Rev. Canon Hartley Perrin has been Rector of the Petersfield–Darliston Cure, a circuit of five Anglican churches in inland Westmoreland. He also serves as Custos Rotulorum of Westmoreland — the senior civic dignitary of the parish — a dual role that has placed him at the centre of Westmoreland's public life for more than four decades.

## Architecture
St. Peter's is a masonry parish church, its walls substantial enough to have survived Hurricane Melissa's Category 5 winds when the roof did not. After Hurricane Beryl in July 2024 damaged the roof, the congregation completed a full re-roofing at a reported cost of over J$8 million — work that was then entirely destroyed by Melissa the following year.

## Clergy
The Petersfield–Darliston Cure has been under the Rev. Canon Hartley Perrin's care since 1979. Lay leaders and wardens across the five churches of the cure sustain regular worship alongside the rector.

## Hurricane Melissa (October 2025)
Hurricane Melissa made landfall near New Hope in Westmoreland on 28 October 2025 as a Category 5 storm. St. Peter's Petersfield was in the direct impact zone. In Canon Perrin's words to the Caribbean National Weekly, "all of the roof and all the windows" were removed by the storm, leaving only the standing walls; restoration, he said, would require millions of dollars. Perrin also reported at least five deaths in Petersfield from the storm, with bodies remaining for days before undertakers could reach them. The congregation now worships at Petersfield Primary and Infant School under a tarpaulin that, in his words to the Jamaica Gleaner, "drains water sometimes." "It's a little disheartening at times. But we still do our best to worship."

## Notable Facts
Petersfield's 1843 foundation places it among the first generation of Anglican churches built in Westmoreland for the freed population after Emancipation. Canon Hartley Perrin's combined role as Rector and Custos — rare even by Jamaican standards — has made him the public face of Westmoreland's post-Melissa recovery.
