# St. Matthew's, Orange Grove
**St. Elizabeth** · Church

St. Matthew's Anglican Church serves Orange Grove in St. Elizabeth, one of six congregations grouped under the St. Peter's cure that anchors Anglican ministry across the southern districts of the parish. The other churches in the cure are All Saints' Newell, St. Augustine's Mountainside, the Epiphany at Bigwoods District, St. Paul's Barbary Hall, and the cure church St. Peter's. As of recent diocesan listings the cure has been led by the Reverend Marjorie Downer.

## History
Orange Grove is a small farming community in southern St. Elizabeth, named after the grove of orange trees that gave the district its identity. The Anglican Diocese established St. Matthew's as a small mission station of the St. Peter's cure during the late-nineteenth-century rural church-planting expansion under Bishop Enos Nuttall. The dedication to Matthew the evangelist (feast day 21 September) is a common one for late-nineteenth-century mission foundations.

The church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, which is consistent with a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Clergy
St. Matthew's Orange Grove is one of six congregations in the St. Peter's cure (Junction P.O., St. Elizabeth) and is served alongside the other missions of the cure under shared clergy arrangements.
