# St. Luke's, Juan-de-Bolas
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Luke's Anglican Church serves Juan-de-Bolas, a community in the upland Lluidas Vale area of central St. Catherine. The community takes its name from one of the most important early figures in Jamaican history, the seventeenth-century Maroon leader Juan de Bolas.

## History
The Juan-de-Bolas district preserves the memory of Juan Lubolo (known to the Spanish as **Juan Lubolo** and to the English as **Juan de Bolas**), a Black guerrilla leader and freedom fighter. He was an enslaved African during the Spanish occupation of Jamaica that ended in 1655, and in that year he led a group of formerly enslaved Africans into the hills of Lluidas Vale, St. Catherine, to fight against the new English regime. He later switched allegiance to the English, and the Lluidas Vale uplands where he established his stronghold became known as "Juan de Bolas country" — a name still attached to a hill, a district, and now a church in the area. The Jamaica National Heritage Trust maintains the Juan de Bolas Marker in the centre of Lluidas Vale town in commemoration.

The Anglican Diocese established St. Luke's as a mission chapel to serve the Juan-de-Bolas community within the wider Lluidas Vale upland landscape, where the Worthy Park Estate — one of the oldest continuously operating sugar estates in the Caribbean (17th-century origin) — has long been the dominant economic presence. The post-Emancipation settlement of the Lluidas Vale uplands by freed estate workers created the kind of small-farmer congregation that the diocese reached through chapels like this.

The dedication to St. Luke — physician and evangelist (feast day 18 October) — is a common Anglican choice; St. Luke is also the parish church dedication at nearby Sanguinetti and at Mitchell Town in Clarendon.

## Architecture
St. Luke's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Catherine uplands. The high valley position creates wind exposure during storm passages and brings cooler conditions than the parish's coastal districts.

## Clergy
St. Luke's Juan-de-Bolas is served within the Kingston Region's St. Catherine Deanery.
