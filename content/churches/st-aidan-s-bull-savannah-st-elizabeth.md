# St. Aidan's, Bull Savannah
**St. Elizabeth** · Church · active

St. Aidan's Anglican Church serves Bull Savannah, a community on the south coast of St. Elizabeth parish. The church maintains a public Facebook presence under the page "St. Aidan Anglican Church, Bull Savannah, St. Elizabeth."

## History
Bull Savannah lies on the south coast of St. Elizabeth, in the dry plains country between the Black River Morass to the west and the Santa Cruz Mountains to the east. The community is one of several "Savannah" districts of southern Jamaica — open grassland zones whose names ("Bull Savannah," "Burnt Savannah," "Lacovia Savannah") record the cattle-grazing economy that characterised these dry coastal flats during and after the plantation era.

The Anglican Diocese established St. Aidan's as a mission chapel to serve the Bull Savannah community as part of its rural network across St. Elizabeth. The dedication to St. Aidan — the seventh-century Irish monk who founded the great monastery of Lindisfarne off the Northumbrian coast and is regarded as the apostle of northern England — is one of the more theologically distinctive Anglican dedications in Jamaica. Aidan's choice for a small Caribbean farming community evokes the parallel of monastic mission in difficult terrain. His feast day is 31 August.

No substantive foundational date or architectural history for St. Aidan's Bull Savannah has been identified in the public heritage sources reviewed; the church does not appear in the 1891 Handbook of Jamaica clergy listings, suggesting a foundation date in the early twentieth century.

## Architecture
St. Aidan's is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Elizabeth south coast. The flat coastal position offers no shelter from storm surge; the community has shared in the broader hurricane vulnerability of the south-coast plains, including the destruction of nearby parishes in Hurricane Melissa (October 2025).

## Clergy
St. Aidan's Bull Savannah is served within the Montego Bay Region's St. Elizabeth deanery network.
