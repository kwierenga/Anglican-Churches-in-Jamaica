# St. Saviour's, Harewood
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

St. Saviour's Anglican Church serves Harewood, a community in the hill country of St. Catherine parish. Harewood is a small farming district in the interior of the parish, and St. Saviour's has provided Anglican ministry to the local community for several generations. The dedication 'The Saviour' — referring directly to Jesus Christ as Saviour — gives this church one of the most directly Christological dedications in Jamaica's Anglican network.

## History
St. Saviour's, Harewood, holds a particular distinction in Jamaican Anglican history: it is recognised by the Diocese as **the first Anglican church consecrated in Jamaica** following the diocese's establishment in 1824. (The colonial-era parish churches that pre-date the diocese were not consecrated under a Jamaican bishop.) For the diocese's 200th anniversary in 2024, the Postal Corporation of Jamaica issued a commemorative stamp featuring St. Saviour's Harewood. The 1900 Handbook of Jamaica records G. C. Linton serving Harewood and St. Faith's during that period.

Harewood sits in the interior hill country of St. Catherine, part of the agricultural landscape that extends north of Spanish Town into the hills. The Anglican Diocese established St. Saviour's to serve this community, and the church has served the local farming families through the transformations of the parish's interior economy.

## Architecture
St. Saviour's is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Harewood has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements. Note that a stub entry also recorded as 'Harewood' exists in the dataset at different coordinates, suggesting the Harewood district may support multiple worship points.

## Notable Facts
The Saviour dedication is among the most direct Christological church names in Anglican tradition, explicitly naming Jesus in his redemptive role rather than invoking a saint or a theological concept. Churches with this dedication — like Saviour's in various communities — carry a particularly evangelical character in their naming.
