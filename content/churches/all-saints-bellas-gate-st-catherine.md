# All Saints', Bellas Gate
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church serves Bellas Gate, a rural community in the interior hill country of St. Catherine parish, west of Linstead. The church, with its small adjoining churchyard, is one of a network of mission stations the Diocese of Jamaica established to serve the agricultural districts of the parish in the late nineteenth century. The Bellas Gate cemetery contains a small body of inscribed memorials catalogued in the Jamaican Ancestral Records project.

## History
Bellas Gate is one of the interior settlements that developed as cultivation moved inland from St. Catherine's coastal plain. The Anglican Diocese established All Saints' as a mission church to serve this farming community within the broader Linstead deanery, following the pattern of late-nineteenth-century rural church-planting under Bishop Enos Nuttall. The dedication to All Saints — commemorating the full company of Christian martyrs and witnesses on 1 November — is one of the more common Jamaican Anglican dedications, given to small mission chapels intended to serve a scattered rural community without claim to a single saintly patron.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a modest masonry chapel, consistent with the small-scale Anglican buildings erected to serve St. Catherine's interior districts in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries: a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard. The interior hill-country position offers some shelter from coastal storm surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
All Saints' has been served within the St. Catherine portion of the Mandeville Region, grouped with neighbouring missions under shared clergy arrangements typical of rural Anglican administration in Jamaica.
