# All Saints', Bellas Gate
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church serves Bellas Gate, a community in the interior hill country of St. Catherine parish. Bellas Gate is a notable community in the history of Jamaican popular music — the area near Frankfield and the interior of St. Catherine has strong connections to dancehall and reggae culture. The church has served the local community for several generations.

## History
Bellas Gate sits in the interior of St. Catherine, in the hill country of the western part of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established All Saints' to serve this community as part of its interior mission network. The church has served the local farming and residential community through the post-Emancipation period and the 20th-century transformations of rural Jamaica.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural St. Catherine in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of St. Catherine. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Bellas Gate has been served within the St. Catherine deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
The Bellas Gate area of interior St. Catherine has a significant place in Jamaican popular culture. The community is associated with several prominent figures in Jamaican dancehall music and entertainment. The All Saints' dedication — commemorating the full company of saints on 1 November — gives this community church a universal spiritual character.
