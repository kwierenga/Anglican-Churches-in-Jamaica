## History
The Craigton/Craighton area of eastern St. Andrew supports multiple Anglican worship points across its dispersed hill community. St. Alban's, dedicated to the first British Christian martyr, serves the part of the community associated with this stub entry.

## Notable Facts
St. Alban is the patron saint of England and the first British Christian martyr, executed during the Roman period for sheltering a Christian priest. Churches dedicated to St. Alban are found across the Anglican world. The dedication here connects this small Jamaican hill community church to one of the oldest strands in British Christian history.
