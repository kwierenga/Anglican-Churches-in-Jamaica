# All Saints', Guy's Hill
**St. Catherine** · Church · active

All Saints' Anglican Church serves Guy's Hill, a market town in the upland country of northern St. Catherine, on the parish boundary with St. Mary. The church is administered as a mission of the St. George's, Blackstonedge cure, alongside St. Paul's Clapham.

## History
Guy's Hill sits in the broken upland country of northern St. Catherine, a historic market town that grew up at a crossroads of the road network connecting the St. Catherine interior to the parishes of St. Mary, St. Ann, and the north coast. The town has been an important regional hub for the surrounding farming communities, with its weekly market drawing produce from across the upland districts.

The Anglican Diocese established All Saints' as a mission of the St. George's Blackstonedge cure, extending its presence into Guy's Hill as part of the wider rural church-planting expansion that the diocese carried out from the late nineteenth century onward. After the disestablishment of the Anglican Church in 1870, the diocese was able to organise its mission network on its own canons and resources, founding small chapels in upland market communities like Guy's Hill that had previously had no direct Anglican presence.

The dedication to All Saints — commemorating on 1 November the full company of recognised and unrecognised saints — is the most general dedication in the Anglican tradition and is a common choice for mission chapels intended to gather a scattered farming community.

## Architecture
All Saints' is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for mission stations across the St. Catherine uplands. The hill-country position creates wind exposure during storm passages.

## Clergy
All Saints' Guy's Hill is served as a mission of the St. George's, Blackstonedge cure within the Kingston Region's St. Catherine Deanery.
