# Holy Trinity, Park Hall
**Clarendon** · Church · active

Holy Trinity Anglican Church serves Park Hall, a community in the interior of Clarendon parish. Park Hall is a farming community in the interior hills, and Holy Trinity has provided Anglican ministry here for several generations. The Trinity dedication — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — is one of the most traditional Anglican church names.

## History
Park Hall sits in the interior of Clarendon, in the hill country of the parish. The Anglican Diocese established Holy Trinity to serve this community as part of its interior mission network. The church has served the Park Hall farming community through the post-Emancipation period.

## Architecture
Holy Trinity is a modest masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Anglican Diocese across rural Clarendon in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The structure is functional rather than ornate — a simple rectangular nave, a modest entrance, and a small churchyard — suited to a rural congregation of modest means. Set in the rural interior of Clarendon. The interior position offers some shelter from coastal surge while remaining exposed to wind and heavy rainfall during major storm events.

## Clergy
Park Hall has been served within the Clarendon deanery under shared clergy arrangements.

## Notable Facts
Park Hall's name preserves the memory of a colonial estate, the 'hall' designation being common in Jamaican estate naming (e.g. Harewood Hall, Somerset Hall). The estate would have had its 'park' — an enclosed area for cattle or horses — giving the community a name that combines two common colonial estate landscape features.
