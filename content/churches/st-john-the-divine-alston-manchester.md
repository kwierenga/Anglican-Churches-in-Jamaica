# St. John the Divine, Alston
**Manchester** · Church · active

St. John the Divine Anglican Church serves Alston, a community in the highland country of northern Manchester. The church is administered as a mission of the Christ Church, Christiana cure, alongside St. Jude's Battersea — both attached to the cure rectory at P.O. Box 15, Christiana P.O., Manchester (telephone 964-2274).

## History
Alston lies in the cool upland country of northern Manchester, in the broken terrain north and east of Christiana — one of the highest market towns of central Jamaica. The Christiana area was one of the focal points of the post-Emancipation small-farmer movement: cool, elevated, suited to coffee and provision crops, and accessible enough that freed people could establish viable farming communities. The Anglican Diocese established Christ Church Christiana as the cure church for this elevated landscape and extended its ministry into outlying districts including Alston through small mission stations like St. John the Divine.

The dedication "St. John the Divine" — distinct from "St. John the Evangelist" or "St. John the Baptist" — refers to John the Theologian, traditionally identified as the author of the Book of Revelation and the visionary of Patmos. The dedication carries a particularly contemplative, mystical character, fitting for a remote highland congregation.

## Architecture
St. John the Divine is a small rural masonry chapel of the kind erected by the Diocese for highland mission stations across northern Manchester. The elevated terrain creates wind exposure during storm passages and can make road access difficult during heavy rainfall.

## Clergy
St. John the Divine, Alston is served as a mission of the Christ Church Christiana cure within the Mandeville Region's Manchester Deanery.
