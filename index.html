Our Foundation

Pillars of Anglican Life in Jamaica

🙏
Worship

Liturgical worship rooted in scripture, tradition, and the Book of Common Prayer, bringing communities together in praise.

📖
Scripture

The living Word of God guides our faith, study, and practice across every congregation throughout the island.

🤝
Service

Serving Jamaica's communities through education, healthcare, social outreach, and advocacy for justice and dignity.

🌱
Stewardship

Caring for God's creation, our heritage, our resources, and one another as faithful stewards of all we've been given.

200+
Congregations

Anglican churches spanning every parish across Jamaica, from Kingston to Westmoreland.

6
Archdeaconries

Regional groupings of churches led by archdeacons, fostering local fellowship and administration.

180+
Years of Faith

A rich history of Anglican witness in Jamaica since the Diocese was established in 1824.
